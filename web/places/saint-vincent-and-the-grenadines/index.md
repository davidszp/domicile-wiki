## Tax on investments, inheritance and wealth

SVG has no capital gains, inheritance, gift or net wealth tax. Ordinarily resident individuals are taxed on worldwide income (top 28%); whether this reaches foreign portfolio dividends is not verified. Residents who are not ordinarily resident are taxed on foreign income only when remitted. Low confidence on dividends.

- **Ordinary tax residence**: Ordinarily resident individuals are taxed on worldwide income at 10%/20%/28%; residents who are not ordinarily resident are taxed on foreign income only when received in SVG. No capital gains, inheritance or net wealth tax.

## Inheritance law and trusts

Saint Vincent and the Grenadines allows wide testamentary freedom, with no reserved share and no inheritance tax per a commercial guide. Common-law trusts apply. No private foundation law was found and the Hague Trusts Convention is not in force.

### Saint Vincent and the Grenadines — family-office tax dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026
Reviewer: research agent (Claude), unreviewed by a tax adviser

#### Summary

SVG has no capital gains, inheritance, gift or net wealth tax. Ordinarily resident individuals are taxed on worldwide income (top 28%); whether this reaches foreign portfolio dividends is not verified. Residents who are not ordinarily resident are taxed on foreign income only when remitted. Low confidence on dividends.

#### Residence and eligibility

183 days rule; ordinarily resident = permanent home + presence (Deloitte SVG Highlights 2026). Worldwide basis for ordinarily resident; remittance basis for resident but not ordinarily resident.

#### Capital gains

Not taxed (Deloitte). JSON 0. Transfer tax 5% buyer + 5% seller on immovable property and shares; alien landholding licence 4-10%.

#### Dividends

Income Tax Act Cap. 435 s. 36(1) (IRD consolidation to 2009): "the assessable income of every resident person shall not include the actual amount of any dividend accrued to that person". The exclusion is not limited to SVG companies and s. 77, to which it was subject, was repealed in 1998. Foreign portfolio dividends are therefore untaxed for residents, whether or not remitted; no remittance-basis assumption is involved. Deloitte 2026 agrees (dividend income not taxed; no WHT). JSON: domestic_rate 0, foreign_gross_rate 0, foreign_tax_credit not_applicable, confidence medium (post-2009 amendments not read, being scanned images). Foreign withholding (e.g. US 30%, no treaty) is a final cost.

#### Inheritance and gifts to children

No inheritance or estate tax (Deloitte). JSON basis none.

#### Wealth tax

None.

None found.
