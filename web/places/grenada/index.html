Tax on investments, inheritance and wealth

Grenada taxes residents only on Grenada-source income. There is no capital gains tax, no tax on dividends received by residents, and no inheritance, gift or net wealth tax, so a portfolio of foreign shares and bonds is effectively untaxed locally. Costs arise on Grenadian real estate (transfer tax up to 15%, 10% alien landholding licence). No treaty relief exists for US/EU withholding taxes.

  • Ordinary tax residence: Resident individuals are taxed only on income derived from Grenada (Deloitte 2026). There is no capital gains tax, no tax on dividends paid to residents, no inheritance, estate or net wealth tax.

Income tax for employees and remote workers

Income tax is 10% on the first XCD 24,000 and 28% above, after a XCD 36,000 personal allowance, and Deloitte states that residents are taxed only on income derived from Grenada. Employees pay 6.25% NIS up to XCD 5,000 a month.

How to get residence

No free movement for anyone. Grenada citizenship costs USD 235,000 (National Transformation Fund, single applicant up to a family of four) or USD 270,000-350,000 in approved real estate, plus about USD 9,000 of application, due diligence, processing and interview fees. Grenada citizens can reportedly apply for the US E-2 treaty investor visa, the programme's main selling point; the programme is under an independent review announced 22 September 2026. Ordinary residence routes could not be sourced.

Inheritance law and trusts

Grenada follows English-derived common law and no forced heirship was found. The International Trusts Act (1996) was amended in 2018 to stop new international trusts after 31 December 2018. No private foundation law was found and the Hague Trusts Convention is not in force.

Research notes

Grenada — family-office tax dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15 Tax year: 2026 Reviewer: research agent (Claude), unreviewed by a tax adviser

Summary

Grenada taxes residents only on Grenada-source income. There is no capital gains tax, no tax on dividends received by residents, and no inheritance, gift or net wealth tax, so a portfolio of foreign shares and bonds is effectively untaxed locally. Costs arise on Grenadian real estate (transfer tax up to 15%, 10% alien landholding licence). No treaty relief exists for US/EU withholding taxes.

Residence and eligibility

183 days physical presence in the fiscal year makes an individual resident (Deloitte Grenada Highlights 2026). Residents are taxed on income derived from Grenada only. Non-citizens need a residence permit; Grenada also runs a citizenship-by-investment programme whose current amounts were not verified.

Capital gains

No capital gains tax (Deloitte 2026). JSON rates all 0. Real property transfer tax of 5%-15% (seller; above XCD 20,000; by deed above XCD 150,000) and a 10% Alien Landholding Licence for non-resident buyers are transaction costs, not CGT. Statutory reference to the Income Tax Act was not checked.

Dividends

Dividends paid to residents are not taxed and bear no withholding. Foreign dividends are outside the territorial base. JSON domestic 0, foreign 0, credit not_applicable. Outbound WHT 15%. Treaties: CARICOM only.

Inheritance and gifts to children

No inheritance, estate or gift tax. Gifts of Grenadian real property may attract the real property transfer tax. JSON basis none, fully_exempt.

Wealth tax

No net wealth tax. Property tax 0%-0.5% of market value with a XCD 100,000 building exemption for one owner-occupied home.

Special regimes

None. Grenada's territorial system already exempts foreign income.

Company taxation (kept separate from personal tax)

CIT 28% on worldwide income of resident companies; no CGT; no participation exemption; no Pillar Two commitment.
