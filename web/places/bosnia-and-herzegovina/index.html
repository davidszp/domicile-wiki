Tax on investments, inheritance and wealth

Personal taxes in Bosnia and Herzegovina are set by the two entities and Brčko District, not the state. The standard figures here are for the Federation of BiH (Sarajevo): flat 10% income tax; dividends are not income (Law on Income Tax Art. 5); gains on shares and securities are not a taxable category; real estate gains taxed at 10% only if sold within three years. Republika Srpska taxes capital income and capital gains at 13% (dividends to individuals reportedly exempt). No wealth tax. Inheritance and gift tax is cantonal in the Federation; Sarajevo Canton (8% inheritance, 10% gifts) and Zenica-Doboj both exempt children. Federation rules read from the statutes; Brčko not verified.

  • Ordinary tax residence (Federation of BiH): Ordinary residence in the Federation of BiH; residents taxed on worldwide income at a flat 10% under the FBiH Law on Income Tax (last amended Sl. novine FBiH 65/2013).

Inheritance law and trusts

Descendants, adoptees and the spouse are guaranteed half of their intestate share, two thirds if permanently incapable of work. The country has no trust law and is not party to the Hague Trusts Convention. Law differs between the Federation, Republika Srpska and Brcko District.

Residence and eligibility

Each entity and Brčko District has its own income tax law and residence rules (domicile/habitual residence, 183-day tests); not verified in this pass. FBiH residents are taxed on worldwide income.

  • Temporary residence permit (EU, US, OTHER): Not in the EU; foreigners need a BiH temporary residence permit. Not researched in this pass.

Standard regime eligibility: Tax resident of the Federation (residence test not verified in this pass). Foreigners need a BiH residence permit.

Capital gains

Federation of BiH (Sarajevo): the Law on Income Tax (Arts. 4, 20–22) has no category for gains on shares or other securities, so they are not taxed: property income covers only rentals and disposals of real estate (Art. 20), property-rights income covers IP and similar rights (Art. 21), and capital-investment income covers only interest on loans and certain insurance payouts (Art. 22). Gains on real estate are taxed at the flat 10% rate (Art. 9) on the PPI-indexed gain, but are not taxed if the property was the taxpayer's home or is sold more than three years after acquisition (Art. 20(9)). Republika Srpska is different (13% on capital gains).

JSON values: headline rate 10% (0.1); listed securities domestic 0% (0.0), foreign 0% (0.0); private business sale 0% (0.0); local real estate 0% (0.0); foreign real estate 0% (0.0).

  • How the JSON numbers were derived: FBiH: shares and securities (listed or private, domestic or foreign) 0% because their disposal is not a taxable income category (Arts. 4, 20–22). Real estate (local or foreign; Art. 20 is not limited to FBiH property) 0% when held more than three years, otherwise 10% (Art. 9) on the PPI-indexed gain. Headline 10% = the flat PIT rate that applies to short-held real estate. Republika Srpska would be 13% across the board (see regional_differences).
  • Holding periods: FBiH real estate: not taxed after three years from acquisition or if used as the taxpayer's residence (Art. 20(9)).
  • Professional-trader risk: Regular trading could be treated as independent business income (10% in FBiH); no guidance found.
  • Eligibility conditions: FBiH residents are taxed on worldwide income in the categories the law lists; securities gains are simply not a listed category, wherever the securities are.
  • Regional differences: Entity law decides. FBiH (Sarajevo, Mostar): securities gains not taxed; real estate 10%, exempt after three years. Republika Srpska (Banja Luka): capital gains, including on securities and real estate, taxed at 13% (RS Income Tax Law as amended December 2021); property inherited or gifted from direct-line relatives exempt (2020 amendment). Brčko District: flat 10% PIT; capital-gains treatment not verified. Residence in an entity determines which law applies.

Dividends

Federation of BiH: income from participation in company profits (dividends or profit shares) is not income under the Law on Income Tax (Art. 5(1)(1)), so dividends are untaxed for individuals. The article does not distinguish domestic from foreign companies. Republika Srpska: a 2025 municipal guidance note states dividends paid to individual founders are exempt; capital income otherwise 13%.

JSON values: headline 0% (0.0); domestic 0% (0.0); foreign gross 0% (0.0); foreign tax relief: not_applicable.

  • How the JSON numbers were derived: FBiH Art. 5(1)(1): dividends and profit shares 'are not considered income', with no domestic/foreign distinction, so domestic and foreign 0% (medium confidence for foreign dividends).
  • Outbound withholding: FBiH reportedly withholds 5% on dividends to non-resident legal entities; RS companies exempt (search snippet of a tax card, not verified from statute).
  • Treaty network: BiH treaty network not counted in this pass.
  • Regional differences: Republika Srpska: dividends to individuals exempt per municipal guidance (2025); capital income otherwise 13%. Brčko: not verified.

Inheritance and gifts to children

In the Federation, inheritance and gift tax is set by each canton. Sarajevo Canton (Sl. novine KS 28/2018): inheritances taxed at 8% and gifts at 10% (Art. 21), but heirs of the first order of succession (children, spouse) pay no inheritance tax and children, spouse and parents of the donor pay no gift tax (Art. 22). Zenica-Doboj Canton (Sl. novine ZDK 24/2025, Art. 18) likewise exempts first-order heirs and donees. Republika Srpska has no separate inheritance tax; property inherited or gifted from direct-line relatives is exempt from capital-gains income tax.

JSON values: basis beneficiary, bracket mode marginal, children fully_exempt, exemption BAM 0, marginal brackets above the exemption: none; other transfer costs 0% (0); gifts same as inheritance: True; tax-free after years: None.

  • Gifts: Sarajevo Canton: gifts taxed at 10%, but gifts to children, spouse and parents of the donor are exempt (Art. 22(2)); movables (cash, securities, claims) are in scope only above BAM 5,000 per item. Zenica-Doboj: first-order donees exempt. RS: gifts from direct-line relatives exempt; gifts between unrelated persons above BAM 10,000 a year taxed.
  • Business relief: No business relief relevant (children exempt or not researched). Covers listed portfolios: no.
  • Regional differences: FBiH: ten cantonal laws. Sarajevo: 8% inheritance / 10% gifts, first order exempt. Zenica-Doboj: 5% general, 3% second order, first order exempt. RS: no separate inheritance tax; transfers from direct-line relatives exempt from income tax. Brčko: not verified.

  • Surviving spouse: exempt in Sarajevo (Art. 22(1)) and Zenica-Doboj (Art. 18) cantons, as a first-order heir or as a second-order heir; Republika Srpska has no inheritance tax. JSON spouse_treatment: fully_exempt.

Wealth tax

No net wealth tax in either entity or Brčko. Property tax is cantonal in the FBiH, 0.20% of assessed value in RS, and 0.05%–1% in Brčko.

JSON type: none.

  • Property tax (not a wealth tax): FBiH: cantonal property tax by value and size; RS: 0.20% of assessed value; Brčko: 0.05%–1% of market value.

Open questions

  • Entity-level differences cannot be expressed fully in this national file; a subnational file (BA-BIH, BA-SRP, BA-BRC) would be needed to score Republika Srpska (13% capital gains) separately.
  • International school fee (Sarajevo) not published online by QSI Sarajevo or Richmond Park; Sarajevo International School's site was offline on 2026-10-15. Only rent is sourced, so the cost metric is unscored.
  • (standard / capital_gains) The statute text read (Paragraf, consolidated to Sl. novine FBiH 65/2013) may not show later amendments; no later amendment adding securities gains was found, and Eurofast's 2026 tax card agrees that capital gains are not taxed in the FBiH.
  • (standard / capital_gains) Brčko District capital gains rules not verified.
  • (standard / dividends) Art. 5(1)(1) does not limit the exclusion to FBiH companies; no FBiH tax-authority ruling on foreign portfolio dividends was found.
  • (standard / dividends) RS: whether the exemption covers foreign dividends and portfolio (non-founder) holdings was not confirmed.
  • (standard / inheritance) Only Sarajevo and Zenica-Doboj cantons were read from statute; the other eight FBiH cantons were not checked (none found taxing children).
  • (standard / inheritance) Brčko District inheritance rules not verified.

US citizens: The old US–Yugoslavia treaty (1880s/1950s) status for BiH was not verified.
