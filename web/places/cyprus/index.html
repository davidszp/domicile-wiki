Tax on investments, inheritance and wealth

No tax on gains from shares and bonds (listed or not, domestic or foreign) for any resident; capital gains tax (20%) applies only to Cyprus real estate and shares of companies holding it. No inheritance, estate or gift tax since 2000 and no wealth tax. Dividends: a newcomer without a Cyprus domicile of origin is 'non-dom' for 17 of 20 years and pays no Special Defence Contribution (SDC); only the 2.65% health contribution (GHS, capped at EUR 180,000 of income) applies. After 17 years SDC on dividends is 5% (2026 reform, down from 17%), usually fully offset by foreign withholding tax; non-dom status can be bought for two further 5-year blocks at EUR 250,000 each.

  • Tax resident, Cyprus-domiciled for SDC: Ordinary Cyprus tax residence for an individual who is domiciled in Cyprus for Special Defence Contribution (SDC) purposes: a Cypriot by domicile of origin, or a foreigner after 17 of the last 20 years of residence (unless the lump-sum extension is bought). A newly arriving foreign family is NOT in this regime for its first 17 years; see the non_dom regime.
  • Non-domiciled resident (SDC exemption, 17 years, extendable): Automatic for a new resident without a Cyprus domicile of origin: exempt from SDC on dividends and interest until resident 17 of the last 20 years. From 1 Jan 2026 (Art. 3D SDC Law) a deemed-domiciled person can extend non-dom status for up to two further 5-year periods by paying EUR 250,000 upfront per period (irrevocable, non-refundable, Commissioner approval; Circular 2/2026). Capital gains, inheritance and wealth treatment are the same as standard.

Income tax for employees and remote workers

From 1 January 2026 resident income tax is 0% to EUR 22,000, 20% to 32,000, 25% to 42,000, 30% to 72,000 and 35% above. Employees pay 8.8% social insurance up to EUR 68,904 and 2.65% GHS (health) up to EUR 180,000; the self-employed pay 16.6% and 4.0%. The recorded combined rates are 11.45% and 20.6% with the social-insurance cap.

How to get residence

EU citizens only register. Non-EU citizens (US and others) can get permanent residence by investing EUR 300,000 and showing EUR 50,000 of income, or through the no-investment Category F with EUR 9,568 of foreign income (but a multi-year queue), or a digital nomad visa at EUR 42,000 a year. Citizenship by investment ended in 2020.

A faster 4 to 5 year path exists for highly skilled workers who pass Greek and civics exams.

Inheritance law and trusts

Children are protected: they can claim up to a quarter of the estate, and a spouse or parent up to half where there are no children. A foreign resident can choose the law of their nationality (EU Succession Regulation art. 22). Cyprus has trusts and has been a Hague Trusts Convention state since 2017; foundations exist but their use for private family wealth is not clearly settled.

Research notes

Cyprus — family-office tax dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15 Tax year: 2026 Reviewer: research agent (Claude), unreviewed by a tax adviser

Summary

Cyprus does not tax gains on shares and bonds, and has no inheritance, gift or wealth tax. A new arrival without a Cyprus domicile of origin is "non-dom" for 17 of 20 years. During that time Cyprus charges no Special Defence Contribution (SDC) on dividends; the only charge is the 2.65% GHS health contribution, capped at EUR 180,000 of income. After 17 years, SDC on dividends is 5% (2026 reform, down from 17%), and a 15% foreign withholding tax usually offsets it in full. Non-dom status can be extended twice, by 5 years each time, for EUR 250,000 per extension.

Residence and eligibility

  • Tax residence: more than 183 days in the calendar year, or the 60-day rule. The 60-day rule requires at least 60 days in Cyprus, no more than 183 days in any other single state, a Cyprus business, employment or directorship held at year end, and a permanent home in Cyprus all year. From 1 Jan 2026 the condition "not tax resident elsewhere" was dropped from the 60-day rule (PwC).
  • EU citizens: free movement (MEU1 registration).
  • Non-EU (including US): permanent residence by investment of EUR 300,000 excl. VAT (property, company or fund), plus secured income of EUR 50,000 + EUR 15,000 for the spouse + EUR 10,000 per child (GK Law Firm).
  • Domicile for SDC: domicile of origin (father's at birth), or deemed domicile after 17 of the last 20 years of residence (SDC Law art. 2(3)).

Capital gains

  • Gains on "titles" (shares, bonds, options, fund units) are exempt from income tax unconditionally, including trading gains, for every resident, domiciled or not.
  • Capital Gains Tax Law: 20% applies only to Cyprus immovable property and to shares of companies holding it. From 2026 indirect holdings count where at least 20% of the value derives from Cyprus property (was 50%); listed shares are excluded.
  • Lifetime exemptions from 2026: EUR 150,000 principal residence, EUR 50,000 farmland, EUR 30,000 other; overall cap EUR 150,000.
  • Gifts to children are exempt from CGT.
  • A 0.4% transfer levy applies to the proceeds of Cyprus property sales.
  • Crypto gains are taxed at a flat 8% from 2026.
  • JSON: listed domestic, listed foreign, private business and foreign real estate = 0.0. Local real estate = 0.2. Headline = 0.2.

Dividends

  • Dividends are exempt from personal income tax.
  • SDC (domiciled residents only): 5% on profits of 2026 onwards. Profits up to 2025 stay at 17% if distributed by end-2031.
  • GHS: 2.65% on dividend income, capped at EUR 180,000 of income. This is excluded from the rates.
  • Foreign withholding tax is credited against SDC, unilaterally.
  • JSON standard (domiciled): 0.05 domestic and foreign, with a credit. Non-dom regime: 0.0, not_applicable.
  • Outbound withholding tax is generally 0%. 17% applies to companies in EU-blacklisted jurisdictions. From 2026 there is a rate for related low-tax-jurisdiction recipients, which PwC states as both 5% and 17%.
  • About 67 treaties, including the US.

Inheritance and gifts to children

  • Estate duty was abolished from 1 Jan 2000, and there is no gift tax.
  • Parent-to-child free transfers of property pay nil Land Registry fees.
  • Gifts to children are exempt from CGT.
  • The Stamp Duty Law was repealed from 1 Jan 2026.
  • The executor must file a statement of the deceased's assets with the Tax Department within 6 months of death.
  • JSON: basis none, fully_exempt, other_transfer_cost_rate 0.

Wealth tax

  • None.
  • The immovable property tax was abolished in 2017. Only small municipal fees remain.

Special regimes

  • non_dom: automatic for 17 of 20 years. It exempts dividends (and most interest) from SDC.
  • Art. 3D of the SDC Law (in force 1 Jan 2026, Circular 2/2026 of 29 May 2026) allows a deemed-domiciled person to stay non-dom for up to two further 5-year periods. The price is EUR 250,000 upfront per period, irrevocable and non-refundable, with Commissioner approval.
  • The application is due by 30 June of the first year of the period. The transitional deadline was 30 June 2026 for persons deemed domiciled in 2024-2026.
  • This is not modelled as lump_sum, because it only buys continued SDC exemption in years 18-27. It is worth little now that SDC is 5% and creditable.

Company taxation (kept separate from personal tax)

  • Corporate income tax is 15% from 2026 (was 12.5%).
  • Dividend income is generally exempt, and gains on titles are exempt.
  • Deemed dividend distribution was abolished. Disguised dividend rules were added.
  • Losses can be carried forward for 7 years.

Cost of living notes

  • Limassol: The Heritage Private School charges EUR 12,285 a year for Years 12-13 (2025-26).
  • Prime rent: EUR 6,950 a month = median asking rent of 64 four- and five-bedroom villas/detached houses listed on Bazaraki on 2026-10-15 in the eastern coastal belt (Agios Tychonas, Germasogeia incl. Potamos, Mouttagiaka, Parekklisia, Pyrgos, Limassol Marina). Interquartile range EUR 5,500-12,000. Sample: 480 Limassol-district house listings, filtered by title (4-5 bedroom villa/detached) and rent >= EUR 1,000. Asking rents, not contract rents.
  • Purchase price, health insurance and housekeeper costs are not sourced.

Open questions

  • The primary texts were not opened: the SDC Law as amended, Circular 2/2026 and the estate duty abolition law.
  • PwC gives both 5% and 17% as the 2026 withholding tax on dividends to related low-tax-jurisdiction companies.
  • The treatment of passive interest from 2026 is unresolved.
  • Circular 2/2026: KPMG Cyprus (17 June 2026) confirms EUR 250,000 upfront per 5-year extension, at most two extensions, irrevocable and non-refundable. The Tax Department PDF KPMG links to redirected to the Tax Department home page on 2026-10-15, so the circular itself remains unread.
  • Purchase price, health insurance and housekeeper costs have no source yet.
