Tax on investments, inheritance and wealth

Malawi taxes residents only on Malawi-source income, so foreign dividends and gains on foreign securities are untaxed; Malawian dividends bear a final 10% WHT and Malawian capital gains are taxed as income at up to 40% (Malawi Stock Exchange shares held over a year are exempt). There is no wealth tax. Malawi still has an estate duty: graduated rates reach 10% of the whole estate above MWK 100 million, and foreign movables are included when the deceased was domiciled in Malawi. Most figures rest on professional summaries and a 2017 statute consolidation; confidence is medium to low.

  • Malawian tax resident: Source-based income tax (foreign-source income exempt); 10% final WHT on Malawian dividends; estate duty up to 10%.

Inheritance law and trusts

There is no forced heirship: the Deceased Estates (Wills, Inheritance and Protection) Act 2011 lets a person make a will, but a court can award provision to immediate family not adequately provided for. The Act overrides customary inheritance rules. Trusts and foundations were not sourced.

Malawi — family-office tax dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15 Tax year: 2026/27 Reviewer: research agent (Claude), unreviewed by a tax adviser

Summary

Malawi taxes residents only on Malawi-source income, so foreign dividends and gains on foreign securities are untaxed; Malawian dividends bear a final 10% WHT and Malawian capital gains are taxed as income at up to 40% (Malawi Stock Exchange shares held over a year are exempt). There is no wealth tax. Malawi still has an estate duty: graduated rates reach 10% of the whole estate above MWK 100 million, and foreign movables are included when the deceased was domiciled in Malawi. Most figures rest on professional summaries and a 2017 statute consolidation; confidence is medium to low.

US citizens: US citizens remain fully US-taxed on worldwide income; Malawi's exemption of foreign-source income leaves the US layer as the main income tax. Malawi estate duty could apply alongside US estate tax (no estate tax treaty).

Residence and eligibility

Resident if physically present for an aggregate 183 days in any 12-month period (EY 2026), or, per PwC (2024), if resident or intending to reside for at least 183 days in a year, or holding a business residence, employment or temporary residence permit. The tax year runs 1 April to 31 March.

  • Business residence / temporary residence permit (EU, US, OTHER): Non-citizens need an immigration permit; investment thresholds for business residence permits were not researched.

Capital gains

Standard regime

Capital gains of individuals are included in assessable income and taxed at the progressive rates (top 40% from 2026). The gain is proceeds less cost indexed by the consumer price index. Gains on shares listed on the Malawi Stock Exchange held for more than one year are not taxable, nor are gains on the principal residence or on personal assets. Malawi taxes only Malawi-source income and exempts foreign-source income, so gains on foreign securities are not taxed.

  • Exemptions and thresholds: Exempt: MSE-listed shares held over one year; principal residence; personal and domestic assets not used in a trade; transfers between spouses. Losses only against capital gains.
  • Important exceptions, statute references and announced changes: Top rate: EY's March 2026 guide gives monthly PAYE bands of 0% to MWK 170,000, 30% to MWK 1,570,000, 35% to MWK 10,000,000 and 40% above (reported as effective 1 January 2026; the amending Act was not checked). Earlier summaries (PwC 2024) show 35% top.
  • Tax year / confidence / verification: 2026/27 / medium / partially_verified (checked 2026-10-15)
  • JSON numbers: headline 40% (0.4); listed domestic 0% (0.0); listed foreign 0% (0.0); private business sale 40% (0.4); local real estate 40% (0.4); foreign real estate 0% (0.0).
  • How derived: Listed domestic: 0 for MSE shares held over one year. Listed foreign and foreign real estate: 0 because foreign-source income is exempt (EY 2026; PwC: no tax on passive foreign income). Private Malawian company and Malawian real estate: 40% top progressive rate on the CPI-indexed gain.
  • Holding period: MSE-listed shares: exempt only if held more than one year.
  • Trader recharacterisation: Gains from a trade in shares or property are business income at the same progressive rates.
  • Foreign assets: Foreign-source income is exempt; no foreign tax credit system because taxation is source-based (PwC).

Dividends

Standard regime

Dividends paid by Malawian companies bear a final 10% withholding tax for resident individuals. Foreign dividends are foreign-source income and are not taxed in Malawi.

  • Exemptions and thresholds: Foreign-source income exempt.
  • Important exceptions, statute references and announced changes: Malawi is source-based: residents are taxed only on income from a source within or deemed within Malawi (EY 2026; PwC 2024; Andersen 2021).
  • Tax year / confidence / verification: 2026/27 / medium / partially_verified (checked 2026-10-15)
  • JSON numbers: headline 10% (0.1); domestic 10% (0.1); foreign gross 0% (0.0); foreign tax relief: not_applicable.
  • How derived: Domestic: 10% final WHT. Foreign: 0, exempt as foreign-source income, so no credit is needed.
  • Outbound withholding: 10% on dividends to residents and non-residents (EY corporate guide), reducible by treaty.
  • Treaty network: Small: France, Norway, South Africa, Sweden, Switzerland, United Kingdom (EY 2026). No US treaty, so US dividends bear 30% US WHT.
  • Holding companies: Companies pay 30% CIT (plus 10% on taxable income above MWK 10 billion per EY). Not modelled.

Inheritance and gifts to children

Standard regime

Estate duty under the Estate Duty Act (Cap. 43:02) is levied on the principal value of the deceased's estate at graduated rates: nil up to MWK 20 million, then 4% rising to 10% above MWK 100 million, with the rate of the band reached applying to the whole estate (marginal relief at band edges). Gifts made within three years of death are added back. There is no separate gift tax. For an estate of this size the charge is effectively 10% of the estate.

  • Exemptions and thresholds: Estates up to MWK 20,000,000: nil. Scale (Schedule as amended by Act 1 of 2016): >20m-25m 4%; >25m-35m 5%; >35m-50m 6%; >50m-70m 7%; >70m-90m 8%; >90m-100m 9%; >100m 10%.
  • Important exceptions, statute references and announced changes: EY's 2026 guide still prints an older scale (0-11%, thresholds in thousands of kwacha) and confirms the duty exists; the statute consolidation of 31 December 2017 is used here. A 2015 news report said many MPs wanted the duty abolished; no repeal was found (search budget exhausted, not fully checked). Rates may have been amended after 2017.
  • Tax year / confidence / verification: 2026/27 / low / partially_verified (checked 2026-10-15)
  • JSON numbers: basis estate, children taxed, scope worldwide, exemption 0 MWK, brackets (whole_amount): 0% from 0, 4% from 20,000,000, 5% from 25,000,000, 6% from 35,000,000, 7% from 50,000,000, 8% from 70,000,000, 9% from 90,000,000, 10% from 100,000,000; other transfer costs 0% (0.0).
  • Gifts: No separate gift tax; gifts within three years before death (other than marriage gifts, normal expenditure and small gifts) are included in the dutiable estate. (same as inheritance: False; tax-free after years: 3)
  • Foreign assets: Section 5: personal property outside Malawi is included only where the deceased was domiciled in Malawi at death; immovable property abroad appears to be excluded (not verified).
  • Residence/domicile rules: Malawi-situated property is always dutiable; foreign movable property only for a Malawi-domiciled deceased. A family that settles permanently is likely to acquire a domicile of choice, so worldwide movables (the portfolio) are scored as in scope.
  • Business relief covers listed shares: False

Surviving spouse: No spousal exemption: the whole estate is dutiable on the graduated scale whoever inherits; the Act's only spousal mention is a discretionary remission of up to half the duty where death was caused by war operations (Estate Duty Act Cap. 43:02, s. 7).

Wealth tax

Standard regime

No net wealth or net worth tax.

  • Tax year / confidence / verification: 2026/27 / medium / partially_verified (checked 2026-10-15)
  • JSON type: none
  • Property tax (not a wealth tax): City and district rates on property, based on value, payable semiannually (EY 2026).

Special regimes

None modelled; see Residence and eligibility.

Company taxation (kept separate from personal tax)

Headline CIT rate: 30% (0.3). 30% CIT for Malawian-incorporated companies, plus an additional 10% on taxable income above MWK 10 billion (EY); branches 35% per older summaries (not re-checked). 10% WHT on dividends. Special zones/regimes: Export allowances and investment allowances for manufacturing (not researched in detail).

Cost of living notes

Not researched in this pass: no cost item could be sourced quickly from an agency report, school fee page or comparable source, so cost_of_living is omitted.
