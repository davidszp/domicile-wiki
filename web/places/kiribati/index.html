Tax on investments, inheritance and wealth

Kiribati (Income Tax Act 2023) taxes residents on worldwide income at up to 30% above AUD 30,000. Foreign dividends are property income at those rates with a foreign tax credit. Whether private capital gains on shares are taxed, and the rate of the final withholding on Kiribati dividends, were not found: both are left unknown. No inheritance, gift or wealth tax was found. Low confidence overall.

  • Ordinary tax residence: Residents taxed on worldwide income at 0/20/25/30%.

Kiribati succession law could not be sourced here. Customary land passes by custom. No forced heirship rule was confirmed. The Hague Trusts Convention is not in force.

Kiribati — family-office tax dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15 Tax year: 2026 Reviewer: research agent (Claude), unreviewed by a tax adviser

Summary

Kiribati (Income Tax Act 2023) taxes residents on worldwide income at up to 30% above AUD 30,000. Foreign dividends are property income at those rates with a foreign tax credit. Whether private capital gains on shares are taxed, and the rate of the final withholding on Kiribati dividends, were not found: both are left unknown. No inheritance, gift or wealth tax was found. Low confidence overall.

US citizens: US citizens remain subject to US federal income, estate and gift tax on worldwide income and assets wherever they live; the app adds the federal layer. Where local tax on portfolio income is nil there is nothing to credit against US tax.

Residence and eligibility

Income Tax Act 2023 (per the 2023 individual return instructions): resident if a permanent home is available in Kiribati, or present for periods adding up to 183 days in any 12-month period beginning or ending in the tax year, or a citizen official posted overseas. Residents are taxed on worldwide income.

  • Residence permit (EU, US, OTHER): Not researched.

Dividends

Standard regime

Residents are taxed on income from all sources (return instructions, Question 19). Dividends are property income (Box 26A) taxed at resident rates: 0% to AUD 5,000, 20% to 15,000, 25% to 30,000, 30% above. Overseas tax on property income is credited separately (Q25). A dividend from a Kiribati resident company from which tax was withheld need not be returned (final withholding; rate not found).

  • Exemptions and thresholds: Resident scale (2023 return): nil to AUD 5,000; 20% on 5,000-15,000; AUD 2,000 + 25% on 15,000-30,000; AUD 5,750 + 30% above 30,000. Non-residents 30% flat.
  • Tax year / confidence / verification: 2026 / low / partially_verified (checked 2026-10-15)
  • JSON numbers: headline 30% (0.3); domestic null (unknown); foreign gross 30% (0.3); foreign tax relief: credit.
  • How derived: Foreign: top resident rate 30% before the foreign tax credit. Domestic: final withholding on Kiribati company dividends, rate not found (null).
  • Outbound withholding: Not verified (MFED page links an 'International withholding tax on 30%' notice, not read).
  • Treaty network: No comprehensive treaties found (not verified).
