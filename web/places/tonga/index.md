## Tax on investments, inheritance and wealth

Tonga taxes residents on worldwide income at up to 25%. The Income Tax Act 2007 has no capital gains tax: only gains on business assets are taxed, so a private investor's gains appear untaxed (low confidence). Dividends from Tongan companies are exempt; foreign dividends are property income taxed at up to 25% with a foreign tax credit. No inheritance, gift or wealth tax was found.

- **Ordinary tax residence**: Residents taxed on worldwide business, employment and property income at 0-25%; no tax on private capital gains found.

## Inheritance law and trusts

Tonga allows wills, and intestacy gives a widow one third and children two thirds, but no fixed share was found for a will. Land is hereditary and mostly cannot be sold to foreigners. Trust and foundation law and the Hague Trusts Convention were not confirmed.

### Tonga — family-office tax dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026
Reviewer: research agent (Claude), unreviewed by a tax adviser

#### Summary

Tonga taxes residents on worldwide income at up to 25%. The Income Tax Act 2007 has no capital gains tax: only gains on business assets are taxed, so a private investor's gains appear untaxed (low confidence). Dividends from Tongan companies are exempt; foreign dividends are property income taxed at up to 25% with a foreign tax credit. No inheritance, gift or wealth tax was found.

US citizens: US citizens remain subject to US federal income, estate and gift tax on worldwide income and assets wherever they live; the app adds the federal layer. Where local tax on portfolio income is nil there is nothing to credit against US tax.

#### Residence and eligibility

Income Tax Act 2007 s. 4(3): resident if the individual has a home in Tonga at any time during the year, is present 183 days in any 12-month period commencing or ending in the year, or is a government employee posted abroad. Part-year rules apply on arrival and departure.

- **Residence permit** (EU, US, OTHER): Not researched.

#### Capital gains

##### Standard regime

No capital gains tax. Gross income consists of business, employment, property and other income (s. 11); gains are taxed only on disposal of a business asset (s. 12(c)). Gains on a privately held portfolio, private company shares or a non-business property appear outside the tax.

- **Important exceptions, statute references and announced changes:** s. 44(5): transmission of an asset by succession or under a will is treated as a disposal by the deceased (relevant to business assets). Business assets include any asset used or held ready for use in a business.
- **Tax year / confidence / verification:** 2026 / low / partially_verified (checked 2026-10-15)
- **JSON numbers:** headline 0% (0.0); listed domestic 0% (0.0); listed foreign 0% (0.0); private business sale 0% (0.0); local real estate 0% (0.0); foreign real estate 0% (0.0).
- **How derived:** Income Tax Act 2007 s. 11-14: only gains on disposal of business assets are in business income; no other charge on gains found. Assumes the family is a passive investor and not 'conducting a business'.
- **Trader recharacterisation:** If investing amounted to a business, gains on business assets would be business income at up to 25%. A rented property could be a business asset.
- **Foreign assets:** Residents are taxed on worldwide income (s. 11(2)), but private capital gains are not income.


#### Dividends

##### Standard regime

A dividend paid by a resident company to a resident person is exempt (s. 21). Foreign dividends are property income of a resident (s. 14), taxed at the individual rates (25% above TOP 70,000 from July 2021), with a foreign tax credit (s. 62).

- **Exemptions and thresholds:** Individual rates from July 2021 (Ministry page): 0% to TOP 12,000; 10% to 30,000; 15% to 50,000; 20% to 70,000; 25% above 70,000. The 2017 Order set the rates on 'chargeable income of an individual' (then 0/10/20/25%).
- **Tax year / confidence / verification:** 2026 / medium / partially_verified (checked 2026-10-15)
- **JSON numbers:** headline 25% (0.25); domestic 0% (0.0); foreign gross 25% (0.25); foreign tax relief: credit.
- **How derived:** Domestic: s. 21 exempt = 0. Foreign: top individual rate 25% before the s. 62 foreign tax credit.
- **Outbound withholding:** 15% non-resident withholding on dividends, interest, royalties and fees (s. 79; Ministry page).
- **Treaty network:** No comprehensive tax treaties found (not verified). US dividends bear 30% US WHT, creditable up to Tongan tax.


#### Inheritance and gifts to children

##### Standard regime

No inheritance, estate or gift tax was found. Under the Income Tax Act, transmission of an asset on death is a deemed disposal by the deceased (s. 44(5)), but only gains on business assets are taxable.

- **Tax year / confidence / verification:** 2026 / low / unverified (checked 2026-10-15)
- **JSON numbers:** basis none; children fully_exempt; exemption 0; brackets []; other transfer costs 0.0.
- **Gift tax:** No gift tax found.


#### Wealth tax

##### Standard regime

No net wealth tax and no recurring levy on financial assets.

- **Tax year / confidence / verification:** 2026 / medium / partially_verified (checked 2026-10-15)

- **Property tax (not a wealth tax):** Not researched.


#### Special regimes

None found for wealthy new residents.

#### Company taxation (kept separate from personal tax)

Headline rate in JSON: 25% (0.25). Resident and non-resident companies pay 25% on chargeable income (Ministry of Revenue and Customs). Partnerships are taxed as companies. Dividends between resident persons exempt.
