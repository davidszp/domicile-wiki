Tax on investments, inheritance and wealth

Armenia taxes residents on worldwide income at a flat 20%, but income from the sale of shares and other investment securities is exempt for individuals, and dividends are taxed at 5%. Inheritances and gifts received from individuals are exempt from income tax, and there is no wealth tax (only annual property tax on real estate and vehicles). Corporate tax 18%. Medium-to-low confidence: the securities exemption comes from a 2023 KPMG summary and was not checked against the current Tax Code.

  • Armenia tax residence: Gains on securities exempt; dividends 5%; no inheritance or wealth tax.

Inheritance law and trusts

Minor children, disabled children, a disabled spouse, parents and dependants are guaranteed at least half of what they would inherit by law, whatever the will says. The sources do not settle whether a foreign resident can choose their own national law. Armenia has no common-law trust and the Hague Trusts Convention is not in force there; foundations exist but are set up for public-benefit purposes.

Research notes

Armenia — family-office tax dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15 Tax year: 2026 Reviewer: research agent (Claude), unreviewed by a tax adviser

Summary

Armenia taxes residents on worldwide income at a flat 20%, but income from the sale of shares and other investment securities is exempt for individuals, and dividends are taxed at 5%. Inheritances and gifts received from individuals are exempt from income tax, and there is no wealth tax (only annual property tax on real estate and vehicles). Corporate tax 18%. Medium-to-low confidence: the securities exemption comes from a 2023 KPMG summary and was not checked against the current Tax Code.

Residence and eligibility

Resident if physically present 183 days or more in the calendar year, or centre of vital interests in Armenia, or in the Armenian civil service (PwC).

  • Temporary / permanent residence permit (EU, US, OTHER): Many nationalities can stay visa-free up to 180 days a year; long-term stay needs a temporary (1-year) or permanent residence permit on grounds such as business, employment or Armenian descent. No golden visa programme. Details not verified.

Capital gains

Armenia has no separate capital gains tax. Income from the sale of stocks and other investment securities is exempt income for individuals (KPMG). Income from the sale of property paid by a tax agent is taxed at 10%; sales of non-business property to other individuals are exempt (PwC).

  • Rate basis: Securities and private company shares scored 0 (exempt income). Local real estate scored at 10%: the rate when the buyer is a tax agent (company or entrepreneur); a sale to another individual is exempt. Foreign real estate sold to a foreign individual assumed exempt.
  • Professional trader: Individual entrepreneurs are taxed on business income; frequent trading by a private investor is not known to be recharacterised.
  • Holding period: None found.
  • Foreign assets: Residents are taxed on worldwide income with a foreign tax credit, but the securities exemption is not limited to Armenian securities (per KPMG).
  • Confidence: medium (partially_verified), verified 2026-10-15.
  • JSON numbers: headline 0.1; listed domestic 0.0, listed foreign 0.0, private business 0.0, local real estate 0.1, foreign real estate 0.0.

Dividends

Dividends are taxed at 5% (for profits from 2020 onward), withheld by Armenian payers. Dividends on shares listed in Armenia by 31.12.2024 are exempt. Foreign tax is credited up to the Armenian tax.

  • Rate basis: 5% statutory dividend rate; assumed to apply to foreign dividends received by residents (self-declared).
  • Outbound WHT: 5% WHT on dividends to non-residents; treaty rates; no WHT on gains from securities.
  • Treaty network: About 47 treaties (PwC list: most EU states, Switzerland, UK, UAE, Qatar, Hong Kong, Cyprus, Luxembourg). No modern US treaty listed.
  • Confidence: medium (partially_verified), verified 2026-10-15.
  • JSON numbers: headline 0.05, domestic 0.05, foreign gross 0.05, credit method credit.

Inheritance and gifts to children

No inheritance or gift tax. Property and money received by inheritance or as a gift from individuals are exempt income (KPMG).

  • Gift tax: Gifts from individuals are exempt; gifts from tax agents (companies) are taxed as other income at 20%.
  • Foreign assets: No tax on any asset.
  • Domicile rules: Not relevant.
  • Confidence: medium (partially_verified), verified 2026-10-15.
  • JSON numbers: basis none, children fully_exempt, exemption 0, brackets none, other transfer cost 0.0.

Wealth tax

No net wealth tax.

  • Property taxes: Annual property tax on real estate (cadastral value) and vehicles is levied by communities; rates not verified. Not a wealth tax.
  • Confidence: medium (partially_verified), verified 2026-10-15.
  • JSON: type none.

Special regimes

None modelled. No special tax regime for newly arriving wealthy residents was found.

Company taxation (kept separate from personal tax)

CIT 18%; investment funds 0.01% of net assets; turnover tax and micro-enterprise regimes for SMEs.

Cost of living notes

No cost-of-living items were sourced in this pass.

US citizens: US citizens remain US-taxed. The 1973 US-USSR treaty has been reported to apply to Armenia; not verified.

Open questions

  • The securities exemption (Tax Code art. 147) was taken from KPMG's 2023 summary; confirm that it still covers foreign listed securities in 2026.
  • Whether the 10% on property sold to a tax agent is levied on gross proceeds or on the gain was not verified.
  • PwC and KPMG state the 5% dividend rate without distinguishing foreign-source dividends; confirm that foreign dividends are not taxed at the general 20% rate.
