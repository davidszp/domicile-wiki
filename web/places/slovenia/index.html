Tax on investments, inheritance and wealth

Slovenia taxes capital gains and dividends at 25%. The capital gains rate falls to 20% after 5 years, 15% after 10 and 0% after 15 years of holding. Children are fully exempt from inheritance and gift tax, and there is no wealth tax. No special regime for wealthy arrivals.

  • Ordinary tax residence: Worldwide taxation of residents; capital income taxed separately at schedular rates as a final tax.
  • Family holding company (d.o.o.): The family holds the portfolio through a Slovenian d.o.o. owned by the parents. The company pays corporate income tax at 22% (2024-2028; 19% afterwards) on realised gains; a listed minority holding gets no gains exemption (the 50% exemption needs a stake of at least 8% and an employee). Dividends are excluded from the base except a flat 5% treated as non-deductible expense, so they bear 22% x 5% = 1.1%. Money taken out is taxed again as a dividend at 25%. Wealth tax (none) and inheritance tax (children exempt) are the same as for direct holding, so those metrics are inherited from the standard regime.

Income tax for employees and remote workers

Progressive income tax of 16% to EUR 9,721.43, 26% to 28,592.44, 33% to 57,184.88, 39% to 82,346.23 and 50% above, after a general allowance of EUR 5,551.93 (higher at low incomes). Employees pay 22.1% social security plus 1% long-term care; the self-employed bear the employee and employer shares.

Inheritance law and trusts

Descendants, the spouse and partners are guaranteed half of their intestate share, other close relatives a third. A foreign resident can choose the law of their nationality (EU Succession Regulation art. 22). Slovenia has no trusts; foundation law was not confirmed for private use.

Research notes

Slovenia — family-office tax dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-16 Tax year: 2026 Reviewer: research agent (Claude), unreviewed by a tax adviser

Summary

Personal Income Tax Act (ZDoh-2). Capital income (interest, dividends, capital gains) is taxed separately from other income as a final tax at 25% (Art. 132). Capital gains get holding-period reductions to 0% after 15 years. Children are fully exempt from inheritance and gift tax (ZDDD Art. 9). No wealth tax. No special regime for wealthy arrivals.

Residence and eligibility

Resident with a registered permanent residence, a habitual abode, a centre of personal and economic interests, or more than 183 days of presence in the tax year (PwC). EU citizens: free movement and registration. US/other: a temporary residence permit for a purpose (employment, business, family). No investor-visa route was found (not verified).

Capital gains

  • Rate (Art. 132 ZDoh-2, FURS guide of 4 Aug 2026): 25% for 0–5 years of holding, 20% after 5, 15% after 10, and exempt after 15 years (20 years until 31 Dec 2021). Applies to securities, company shares, fund units and real estate, domestic or foreign.
  • Main home exempt if owned, registered as permanent residence and lived in for the last 3 years (Art. 96).
  • FIFO per identical security (Art. 103). Losses only offset gains of the same year. 30-day wash-sale rule.
  • Gift of capital = disposal for the donor. For gifts to a spouse or child, the gain can be deferred to the child's later sale (notify within 15 days). Inheritance is not a disposal.
  • INR (individual investment account, ZINR, from 5 Mar 2026): 15% only on withdrawal, first withdrawal after 15 years exempt. Contribution cap is EUR 20,000 in year one, then EUR 5,000/yr (+5,000 for Slovenian instruments), EUR 150,000 lifetime. Immaterial at family-office scale.
  • JSON: all long-term rates 0.0 (held >15 years); headline_rate = 0.25. A rebalanced portfolio will in practice pay 25% on most realised gains.
  • Professional trading carried on as a business activity is taxed as business income at progressive rates.
  • No individual exit tax is described in the FURS guide (open question).

Dividends

Flat 25% final tax on domestic and foreign dividends (Arts 91, 132). Foreign withholding tax is credited. JSON: domestic_rate = foreign_gross_rate = 0.25, credit. Outbound WHT: 15% standard to non-residents (PwC), 0% to qualifying EU parents. 59 treaties in force, including the US.

Inheritance and gifts to children

ZDDD (Uradni list RS 117/2006). The tax is on the recipient. Under Art. 9, the first inheritance order is fully exempt for both inheritances and gifts: children, adopted children and their descendants, spouse, unmarried partner, plus sons/daughters-in-law and stepchildren (FURS; Uradni list 2016). Others (Art. 8) pay 5–14% (parents, siblings), 8–17% (grandparents) or 12–39%. Movables-only transfers below EUR 5,000 are not taxed. JSON: fully_exempt, no brackets, gifts same as inheritance.

  • Surviving spouse (spouse_treatment fully_exempt): Spouse and unmarried partner are exempt as first-order heirs (ZDDD Art. 9, FURS).

Wealth tax

None. Municipal building-land use charge (NUSZ) on real estate. JSON: type = none. Status of any national real property tax proposal not verified.

Special regimes

None relevant. INR is a small retail account, not a regime.

Structure regime holding_doo (family d.o.o.), added 2026-10-16 for the 60-year projection: gains 22%, dividends 1.1% effective (22% x 5%), payout to the family 25% (Art. 132 ZDoh-2), wealth tax and inheritance inherited from the standard regime. Not a special regime for arrivals: any resident can found a d.o.o. Private foundation and trust were considered and not added: Slovenian law reportedly allows private foundations only for public-benefit purposes and does not regulate a family foundation (University of Ljubljana thesis abstract, 2018; not checked against the Ustanove Act).

Company taxation (kept separate from personal tax)

CIT 22% for 2024-2028 (Art. 64 ZORZFS; general rate 19%) (FURS). Dividends received are excluded from the tax base (Art. 24 ZDDPO-2) but 5% of them counts as a non-deductible expense, so the effective rate is 1.1%; there is no minimum holding, the payer must be subject to a comparable profit tax, and non-EU payers resident in countries with a profit tax below 12.5% on the Ministry of Finance list are excluded (PwC). Gains on shares: 50% exempt only for stakes of at least 8% held 6 months where the company employs at least one person (FURS guide, section 5.1). A portfolio company with listed minority stakes therefore pays 22% on every realised gain. Foreign withholding on an excluded dividend is not credited (FURS 0920-13147/2022-3).
