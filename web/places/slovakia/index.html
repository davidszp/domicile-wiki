Tax on investments, inheritance and wealth

Slovakia exempts gains on listed shares held over one year (foreign exchanges included), taxes dividends at 7%, and has no inheritance, gift or wealth tax. The catch is a private business sale or short-term trading, which is taxed at up to 35% from 2026 plus 15% uncapped health insurance (about 50% at the top).

  • Ordinary tax residence: Progressive PIT (19/25/30/35% from 2026) with exemptions for long-held listed securities and real estate; 7% dividends.

Income tax for employees and remote workers

2026 scale: 19% on the tax base up to EUR 43,983.32 (154.8x the subsistence minimum), 25% to EUR 60,349.21, 30% to EUR 75,010.32, 35% above. Non-taxable amount EUR 5,966.73 a year, full up to a tax base of EUR 26,367.26 and zero from EUR 50,234.18. Employee contributions: 9.4% social (maximum monthly assessment base EUR 16,764, i.e. EUR 201,168 a year) and 5% health (no cap); the recorded rate is the combined 14.4% and the cap applies to the social part only.

Inheritance law and trusts

Only children are protected: minors keep their full intestate share, adults half. A foreign resident can choose the law of their nationality (EU Succession Regulation art. 22). Slovakia has no trusts, and foundations are for public benefit.

Slovakia — family-office tax dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-16 Tax year: 2026 Reviewer: research agent (Claude), unreviewed by a tax adviser

Summary

Income Tax Act No. 595/2003 Coll. (ZDP), time version effective 1.1.2026. Listed securities held over one year: exempt. Dividends: 7%. No inheritance, gift or wealth tax. Private business sales and short-term gains: progressive up to 35% (new 2026 brackets) plus health insurance.

Residence and eligibility

Resident with permanent residence, a habitual home with intent to stay permanently, or 183+ days in a calendar year (PwC). EU: free movement. US/other: purpose-based temporary residence; no investor programme found (unverified).

Capital gains

  • Gains on securities and company stakes are "other income" (§8(1)(e)-(f)) in the general base (§4(1)(a)) taxed under §15(a)(1): 19% up to 154.8x, 25% to 212.4x, 30% to 264x, 35% above 264x the subsistence minimum (2026; the 30% and 35% bands are new for 2026). PwC says capital gains are in a separate 19% base; the law text puts them in the general base, so we follow the law.
  • Exempt (§9(1)(k)): securities admitted to an EU regulated market or a comparable foreign regulated market, held more than one year (and listed more than one year before sale); not for business assets.
  • Real estate held over 5 years: exempt (§9(1)(a)). EUR 500 a year allowance for net other income (§9(1)(i)).
  • Health insurance: 15% on taxable §8 income, uncapped (Act 580/2004 §10b(1)(d), §13(7), §12(1)(b)).
  • JSON: listed securities 0.0; real estate 0.0; private_business_sale = 0.50 (35% tax + 15% health insurance); headline 0.35.

Re-verified 2026-10-15 from the law text: §8(1)(e)-(f) gains are part of the general base under §4(1)(a), taxed at 19/25/30/35% (§15(a)(1)); the 19% separate base (§15(a)(4)) is for §7 capital income only. Health insurance: Act 580/2004 §10b(1)(d), §13(7), §12(1)(b): 15% on the §8 tax base, uncapped (the 60x cap in §13(6) applies only to dividends). JSON private_business_sale raised from 0.35 to 0.50 (35% + 15%).

Dividends

  • §15(a)(5): 7% on dividends from 2025+ profits (10% for 2024 profits; 7% for 2017-2023; 2004-2016 profits untaxed); withheld by Slovak payers, separate base for foreign dividends (§51e). 35% from non-cooperative states (§15(a)(6)). No health insurance on dividends from 2017+ profits.
  • JSON: domestic and foreign 0.07, credit. Foreign dividends: separate base §51e(1)(a), (3)(a), 7% (§15(a)(5)); credit §45(1). Holding structures: a Slovak holding s.r.o. would pay 21% on gains from portfolio shares (PwC; not read in the statute) and 7% again on the payout; there is no private foundation or family trust, so no structure is scored.

Inheritance and gifts to children

No inheritance or gift tax (PwC). Income from gifts and inheritance is outside the income tax (§12(7)(b) ZDP; the previous text cited §3(2)(b), which is not the provision). JSON: basis = none, fully_exempt.

Wealth tax

None (PwC). Local real estate tax is a municipal property tax.

Special regimes

None.

Company taxation (kept separate from personal tax)

§15(b): 10% (revenue up to EUR 100,000), 21% standard, 24% (revenue above EUR 5m). Minimum tax rules.

Open questions

  • PwC describes capital gains as falling in a separate 19% base; the law (§8(1)(e)-(f), §4(1)(a), §15(a)(1)-(4) ZDP, version effective 1 Jan 2026) puts gains on securities and company stakes in the general progressive base and reserves the separate 19% base for §7 capital income. We follow the law text.
  • Euro amounts of the 2026 bracket thresholds (multiples of the subsistence minimum valid on 1 Jan 2026) not computed here.
  • The credit method (§45(1) ZDP) credits the foreign tax up to the treaty rate and up to the Slovak tax on that income, so a 15% foreign withholding extinguishes the 7%; confirmed for treaties with a 15% cap (US, most EU states); a treaty capping at less than 7% would leave a residual.
  • Notary probate fees not quantified; treated as negligible.
