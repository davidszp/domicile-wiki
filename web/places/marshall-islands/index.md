## Tax on investments, inheritance and wealth

The Marshall Islands' Income Tax Act 1989 taxes wages and salaries (8% on the first USD 10,400, 12% above), business gross revenue (3% above USD 10,000), land lease income (3%) and non-residents' service fees (10%). There is no tax on an individual's dividends, interest or capital gains outside a business, and no inheritance, gift or wealth tax was found.

- **Ordinary residence**: Wages tax, gross revenue tax and land-lease tax; no tax on private investment income.

## Inheritance law and trusts

The Marshall Islands follows a US-style approach and no forced heirship rule was found. Customary land (with its own inheritance rules) is outside ordinary wills. Trust and foundation law and the Hague Trusts Convention (not in force) were not fully sourced.

#### Capital gains

##### Standard regime

No capital gains tax: the Income Tax Act 1989 taxes only wages and salaries (s. 103), business gross revenue (s. 109), income from leased land (s. 116) and non-residents' services (s. 117).

- **Important exceptions, statute references and announced changes:** Lessors of land pay 3% on lease income, withheld by the lessee (s. 116).

- **JSON numbers:** headline 0% (0.0); listed domestic 0% (0.0); listed foreign 0% (0.0); private business sale 0% (0.0); local real estate 0% (0.0); foreign real estate 0% (0.0).
- **How derived:** 48 MIRC Ch. 1: no charge on capital gains of individuals outside a business. 'Gross revenue' (s. 102) covers receipts of a trade or business, including returns on the capital of the business.
- **Trader recharacterisation:** A business would pay 3% gross revenue tax on receipts above USD 10,000.
