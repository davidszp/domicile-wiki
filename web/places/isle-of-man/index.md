## Tax on investments, inheritance and wealth

Self-governing Crown Dependency outside the UK and EU. No capital gains tax, no inheritance or gift tax, no wealth tax. Income, including dividends, is taxed at 10%/21%; residents can fix their total income tax at £220,000 a year (£440,000 per couple) for 5 or 10 years.

- **Ordinary Isle of Man residence**: Resident individuals pay income tax at 10% and 21% on worldwide income. No capital gains, inheritance, gift or wealth tax.
- **Income tax cap election**: A resident individual may elect to cap total Isle of Man income tax at £220,000 a year (£440,000 for a jointly assessed couple) for 2026/27 elections. The cap amount in the first year applies for the whole 5- or 10-year election period, and it is payable each year even if normal tax would have been lower.
- **Manx investment holding company (0% company)**: The family funds a Manx company that holds the listed portfolio. Manx companies pay 0% income tax on investment income, and there is no capital gains tax, so the company pays nothing on dividends or gains. A distribution to a resident shareholder is income taxed at 10%/21%. Profits kept in the company are not taxed on the shareholder: the distributable profits charge (repealed for periods from 6 April 2008) and the attribution regime that replaced it (abolished for accounting periods after 6 April 2012) no longer apply. Foreign withholding tax on the company's dividends is a final cost.

## How to get residence

The Isle of Man is not in the EU and has had no EU free movement since 31 December 2020. British and Irish citizens may live here freely under the Common Travel Area (they still need a work permit to work unless exempt or an 'Isle of Man worker'). Everyone else, EU and US citizens included, needs a visa. There is no investor, retirement or digital nomad visa (Tier 1 Investor is closed). The open routes are the Start-up visa (no investment threshold, 2 years 4 months, no settlement), the Innovator visa (at least GBP 50,000 of investment funds, endorsed by the Department for Enterprise, settlement after 3 years) and the Worker Migrant visa (an Island employer, salary at least GBP 25,447.50).

- **Common Travel Area (British and Irish citizens)** (Other; EU citizens, other passports; open): British and Irish citizens can move to the Isle of Man and live here without immigration permission. Only these two nationalities benefit; other EU and other-passport holders do not.
- **Business Migrant: Start-up visa** (Entrepreneur; EU citizens, US citizens, other passports; open): For first-time founders with an innovative, viable and scalable business idea endorsed by the Department for Enterprise. No investment threshold; leave is 2 years 4 months and cannot lead to settlement, but holders may switch to Innovator.
- **Business Migrant: Innovator visa** (Entrepreneur; EU citizens, US citizens, other passports; open): For experienced business people who set up, join or take over a business on the Island with at least GBP 50,000 of investment funds, endorsed by the Department for Enterprise. Leave is 3 years at a time, with settlement possible after 3 years.
- **Worker Migrant visa (Appendix W) and work permit** (Work; EU citizens, US citizens, other passports; open): A job offer from an Isle of Man employer in an eligible occupation, with a Confirmation of Employment from the Immigration Service. Salary must be at least GBP 25,447.50 a year or the occupation rate, and the employer must first test the local market.
- **Isle of Man, UK or Channel Islands ancestry** (Other; other passports; open): Commonwealth citizens aged 17 or over with a grandparent born in the Isle of Man, UK or Channel Islands can get up to 5 years' leave to work here, then settle.
- **Tier 1 (Investor) visa** (Investor residence; EU citizens, US citizens, other passports; closed to new applicants): The Tier 1 Investor visa is closed and nothing replaced it. A wealthy person with no business plan has no passive investor route today.
- **Tier 1 (Entrepreneur) and Graduate Entrepreneur visas** (Entrepreneur; EU citizens, US citizens, other passports; closed to new applicants): The old Tier 1 entrepreneur routes were replaced on 6 April 2020 by the Start-up and Innovator visas.
- **EU Settlement Scheme (Appendix EU)** (Free movement; EU citizens; closed to new applicants): EU, EEA and Swiss citizens who lived on the Island before 2021 could apply for status. New arrivals from the EU need a visa like anyone else.

Citizenship: British citizenship under the British Nationality Act 1981, applied for through the Isle of Man Nationality Service, normally after settlement (indefinite leave to remain); naturalisation fee GBP 1,858.20 (gov.im, accessed 17 October 2026). The usual UK residence period (5 years, with ILR held 12 months) was not stated on the gov.im page read. Irish citizens have a simplified route since 24 September 2025.

## Inheritance law and trusts

The Isle of Man has no forced heirship, though a court may provide for family. Trusts are well established, the Hague Trusts Convention applies, and foundations are available.

### Isle of Man — family-office tax dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-16
Tax year: 2026/27
Reviewer: research agent (Claude), unreviewed by a tax adviser

#### Summary

Self-governing Crown Dependency outside the UK and EU. No capital gains tax, no inheritance or gift tax, no wealth tax. Income, including dividends, is taxed at 10%/21%; residents can fix their total income tax at £220,000 a year (£440,000 per couple) for 5 or 10 years.

#### Residence and eligibility

No statutory definition; the Assessor decides on the facts. Individuals arriving with the intention of establishing residence are generally resident from arrival (PwC). Day-count guidance from the Income Tax Division was not verified here.

- **British/Irish citizens (Common Travel Area)** (OTHER): British and Irish citizens can live in the Isle of Man without permission; work permits may still apply to some employment.
- **Isle of Man Tier 1 (Investor) or other immigration permission** (EU, US, OTHER): Non-British, non-Irish nationals (including EU citizens since Brexit) need immigration permission under Isle of Man immigration rules, which broadly follow the UK system. Routes and thresholds were not verified in this research.

Standard regime eligibility: Tax residence in the Isle of Man (see residency test). Living there requires a right of abode / immigration permission for non-British, non-Irish nationals.

#### Capital gains

The Isle of Man has no capital gains tax. Gains by a resident private investor on listed shares, bonds, a private business or real estate are not taxed. Gains that amount to trading (frequent dealing, property development) are taxed as income at up to 21%.

- **rate basis**: No capital gains tax exists. PwC lists income tax as the only tax on income ('There are no other taxes on income'); capital gains are outside the income tax charge unless they are trading profits.
- **professional trader**: Profits from a trade in shares or land (badges of trade: frequency, financing, intention) are income taxed at 10%/21%. A buy-and-hold family portfolio is not normally a trade.
- **holding period rules**: None.
- **foreign assets**: Foreign gains are equally untaxed.
- **JSON values**: `{"headline_rate": 0.0, "rates": {"listed_securities_domestic": 0.0, "listed_securities_foreign": 0.0, "private_business_sale": 0.0, "real_estate_local": 0.0, "real_estate_foreign": 0.0}}`
- **Confidence / verification**: high / partially_verified (tax year 2026/27)
- **Open question**: The PwC 'other taxes' page does not state 'no CGT' in terms; confirm with the Income Tax Division's own statement if precision on land-development profits is needed.

#### Dividends

Dividends are taxed as ordinary income: 10% on the first £6,500 of taxable income (single), 21% above. A large dividend income is therefore taxed at 21% (personal allowance £17,000 is withdrawn above £100,000). Residents can elect a tax cap of £220,000 a year (see special regime).

- **rate basis**: Top marginal income tax rate 21% for 2026/27 (gov.im rates table). Foreign tax relief with no agreement: restricted to the lower of the overseas tax suffered and the Manx income tax due on the income, if reasonable steps were taken to minimise the overseas tax (Income Tax Division); so a 15% withholding is credited in full against 21%. Manx companies are mostly taxed at 0%, so there is no imputation credit on domestic dividends.
- **exemptions thresholds**: Personal allowance £17,000 single / £34,000 jointly assessed, reduced by £1 per £2 of income above £100,000 / £200,000. 10% band £6,500 / £13,000.
- **withholding outbound**: No withholding tax on dividends paid by Isle of Man companies (PwC).
- **treaty network**: Small network of double tax agreements (UK, Guernsey, Jersey and a handful of others); no income tax treaty with the United States, so US-source dividends suffer 30% US withholding. Relief for foreign withholding tax is by credit against Manx tax.
- **JSON values**: `{"headline_rate": 0.21, "domestic_rate": 0.21, "foreign_gross_rate": 0.21, "foreign_tax_credit": "credit"}`
- **Confidence / verification**: high / verified (tax year 2026/27)
- **Open question**: Credit relief mechanics (unilateral relief for non-treaty source countries) and exact treaty count not verified from an official list.

#### Inheritance and gifts to children

No inheritance tax, estate duty or gift tax. Only a court probate fee on Isle of Man assets is payable, capped at £9,422.50 for estates over £1 million (2024 schedule).

- **exemptions thresholds**: Probate court fee on the gross value of Isle of Man estate only: £31.50 up to £10,000 rising to a flat £9,422.50 above £1,000,000 (fees as at 2024). Negligible relative to a large estate, hence 0.
- **foreign assets**: No tax on foreign assets; foreign assets are not counted for the probate fee.
- **gift tax**: No gift tax.
- **domicile rules**: No domicile or residence test applies because there is no tax. A family arriving from the UK should check the UK's residence-based inheritance tax (from 6 April 2025: long-term UK residents remain within UK IHT for up to 10 years after leaving).
- **JSON values**: `{"basis": "none", "children_treatment": "fully_exempt", "exemption": 0, "other_transfer_cost_rate": 0.0, "gifts_same_as_inheritance": true}`
- **Confidence / verification**: high / verified (tax year 2026/27)
- **Open question**: Probate fee table is a 2024 law-firm reproduction, not the official Courts schedule; the 2026 amounts may be slightly higher.

#### Wealth tax

No wealth tax or net worth tax.

- **property tax note**: Local rates (property tax) are charged on occupied property; amounts are modest and not a wealth tax.
- **JSON values**: `{"type": "none"}`
- **Confidence / verification**: high / verified (tax year 2026/27)

#### Special regimes

##### Income tax cap election (`tax_cap`)

A resident individual may elect to cap total Isle of Man income tax at £220,000 a year (£440,000 for a jointly assessed couple) for 2026/27 elections. The cap amount in the first year applies for the whole 5- or 10-year election period, and it is payable each year even if normal tax would have been lower.

- **Eligibility**: Must be, or be about to become, Isle of Man tax resident. Election in writing with details of wealth and income, approved by the Assessor; new residents must elect within 60 days of starting residence. Fixed period of 5 or 10 consecutive tax years, renewable.
- **Duration (years)**: indefinite
- **Why it does not expire**: Each election fixes the cap for 5 or 10 consecutive tax years (Income Tax Division guidance note GN51) and can be renewed by a further election, with no limit on renewals, so the cap is available for as long as the person stays resident. It ends if the person leaves or does not renew; the cap amount is fixed at the first-year figure for each election period.
- **Lump sum / cap in JSON**: 220,000 GBP a year, covers foreign_dividends, foreign_capital_gains, foreign_income_all, domestic_income. £220,000 single / £440,000 jointly assessed couple (2026/27). Elected by the individual who holds the income: spouses are taxed independently by default (joint taxation is an option), so a couple with the portfolio in one name pays one cap (GBP 220,000) and a jointly assessed couple pays GBP 440,000. Not indexed: fixed per election period at the first-year figure; changes only by Budget. Payable each year of the 5- or 10-year election regardless of actual income (GN51 s3.2). Capital gains are untaxed anyway. Renewable, so treated as indefinite.

**Dividends under this regime:** Under the cap, the whole income tax bill is fixed at £220,000 (single) or £440,000 (couple) a year, so additional dividends carry no extra Manx tax once ordinary tax at 21% would exceed the cap (roughly £1.05m of taxable income for a single person).

- **rate basis**: Marginal rate above the cap is 0; the fixed annual cap is modelled as the lump sum. Only worthwhile if 21% of taxable income would exceed £220,000.
- **withholding outbound**: No withholding tax on dividends paid by Isle of Man companies.
- **treaty network**: As standard regime; foreign withholding tax is a final cost once the cap binds.
- **JSON values**: `{"headline_rate": 0.0, "domestic_rate": 0.0, "foreign_gross_rate": 0.0, "foreign_tax_credit": "not_applicable"}`
- **Confidence / verification**: high / verified (tax year 2026/27)

#### Holding structures

**Structure modelled: Manx investment holding company, regime id `holding_company`.**

- **Company tax:** the standard rate for all resident and non-resident companies is 0% (Income Tax Division); banking and retail profits above GBP 500,000 pay 10%, land and property 20%. There is no capital gains tax. Dividends and gains in the company are untaxed (JSON dividends 0, `exemption`; gains 0); foreign withholding tax is a final cost.
- **Payout:** dividends to a resident individual are income taxed at 21% at the top (`payout_tax_rate` 0.21).
- **Retained profits:** not taxed on the shareholder. The Distributable Profits Charge was repealed for accounting periods from 6 April 2008, and the Attribution Regime for Individuals that replaced it was abolished for periods after 6 April 2012 (Income Tax Division).
- **Inheritance:** the shares of a Manx company are Manx assets, so a grant of probate attracts the Manx court fee (GBP 9,422.50 above GBP 1,000,000, as at November 2024), whereas foreign shares held directly pay none. The structure metric is the standard regime's (no fee modelled).
- **Running costs:** not sourced (omitted). US citizens are excluded (CFC/PFIC rules).
- **Considered, not added:** Manx trusts and foundations (no income tax saving for a resident family; not sourced).

#### Company taxation (kept separate from personal tax)

Headline CIT rate in JSON: 0.0.

Standard corporate income tax rate 0%. 10% applies to deposit-taking banking and to retail profits above £500,000; 20% applies to Isle of Man land and property income. No withholding tax on dividends. Economic substance rules apply to relevant activities.
