Tax on investments, inheritance and wealth

The Dominican Republic is territorial except that residents are taxed on foreign investment income and financial gains (dividends, interest, gains on shares and securities) at up to 25% (27% top band from 2027 under Law 30-26). Foreigners who become resident pay this only from their third tax year. Dominican dividends bear a final 10% withholding. Inheritance tax is 3% for heirs; gifts are taxed at 25%-27%. No wealth tax for passive individuals (real estate holdings above a threshold pay the 1% IPI).

  • Ordinary tax residence (from the third year): Dominican-source income plus foreign investment income and financial gains taxed at progressive rates up to 25% (2026); 10% final WHT on Dominican dividends; 3% inheritance tax.
  • New resident: foreign investment income untaxed until the third tax year: Foreign individuals who become resident are taxed on foreign-source investment income only from the third taxable year in which they are resident (Deloitte 2026).

Income tax for employees and remote workers

The Dominican Republic taxes on a territorial basis: Dominican-source income is taxed at 0% up to DOP 416,220, then 15%, 20% and 25%; foreign-source income is generally not taxed, except foreign investments and financial gains, and new residents are taxed on these after the third year. Employees withhold 2.87% pension and 3.04% health.

Inheritance law and trusts

The Dominican Republic has forced heirship: children are guaranteed a share of the estate, and a will cannot reduce it. Law 544-14 applies the law of the deceased's last domicile, so a foreigner who lives there is generally under Dominican rules. The Hague Trusts Convention is not in force.

Dominican Republic — family-office tax dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15 Tax year: 2026 Reviewer: research agent (Claude), unreviewed by a tax adviser

Summary

The Dominican Republic is territorial except that residents are taxed on foreign investment income and financial gains (dividends, interest, gains on shares and securities) at up to 25% (27% top band from 2027 under Law 30-26). Foreigners who become resident pay this only from their third tax year. Dominican dividends bear a final 10% withholding. Inheritance tax is 3% for heirs; gifts are taxed at 25%-27%. No wealth tax for passive individuals (real estate holdings above a threshold pay the 1% IPI).

US citizens: No US-DR income tax treaty. US citizens remain subject to US federal tax.

Residence and eligibility

More than 182 days in a calendar year (PwC Residence).

  • Residence for pensioners and rentistas (Law 171-07) and investors (EU, US, OTHER): Law 171-07 grants residence and some tax incentives to persons with foreign pension or passive income; details and any income-tax exemption not verified.

Capital gains

Standard regime

Capital gains are taxed as ordinary income (top 25% in 2026). Residents are taxed on foreign financial gains (gains on shares and securities). Law 30-26 (June 2026) cut the tax on individuals' gains from Dominican real estate to a single 10% (exempt if reinvested in a main residence within 6 months), effective immediately.

  • Important exceptions, statute references and announced changes: From 2027 the top band becomes 27% above DOP 4,800,000 (Law 30-26). Inflation adjustment of cost allowed.
  • Tax year / confidence / verification: 2026 / medium / partially_verified (checked 2026-10-15)
  • JSON numbers: headline 25% (0.25); listed domestic 25% (0.25); listed foreign 25% (0.25); private business sale 25% (0.25); local real estate 10% (0.1); foreign real estate n/a (null).
  • How derived: PwC 2026 scale top 25% (above DOP 867,123); Deloitte: gains on capital assets in the DR generally 25%; foreign financial gains of residents taxable. Local real estate 10% under Law 30-26.
New resident: foreign investment income untaxed until the third tax year (special regime)

Foreign financial gains untaxed in the first two resident years.

  • Tax year / confidence / verification: 2026 / medium / partially_verified (checked 2026-10-15)
  • JSON numbers: headline 25% (0.25); listed domestic 25% (0.25); listed foreign 0% (0.0); private business sale 25% (0.25); local real estate 10% (0.1); foreign real estate 0% (0.0).

Dividends

Standard regime

Cash dividends from Dominican companies are exempt in the hands of individuals once the 10% withholding has been applied (final). Foreign dividends are foreign investment income taxed at up to 25% (2026) with a credit for foreign tax up to the Dominican tax on that income.

  • Tax year / confidence / verification: 2026 / medium / partially_verified (checked 2026-10-15)
  • JSON numbers: headline 25% (0.25); domestic 10% (0.1); foreign gross 25% (0.25); foreign tax relief: credit.
  • How derived: Domestic: 10% final WHT (PwC Income determination). Foreign: top PIT rate 25% in 2026 (27% from 2027).
  • Outbound withholding: 10% on dividends to residents and non-residents (Canada and Spain treaties 0%/10%).
  • Treaty network: Only Canada and Spain.
  • Holding companies: Dividends received by a holding company that already suffered 10% WHT are not taxed again on onward distribution.
New resident: foreign investment income untaxed until the third tax year (special regime)

Foreign dividends untaxed in the first two resident years.

  • Tax year / confidence / verification: 2026 / medium / partially_verified (checked 2026-10-15)
  • JSON numbers: headline 25% (0.25); domestic 10% (0.1); foreign gross 0% (0.0); foreign tax relief: not_applicable.
  • How derived: Foreign-source investment income deferred to the third taxable year of residence.

Inheritance and gifts to children

Standard regime

Inheritance tax of 3% of the inherited estate is charged to the heirs. It covers personal and real property in the estate, Dominican-situs gifts, and foreign-situs personal property of a deceased who had Dominican nationality or whose last domicile was in the DR (Deloitte 2026). Gifts to individuals are taxed at 25% (Deloitte) or 27% (PwC).

  • Tax year / confidence / verification: 2026 / medium / partially_verified (checked 2026-10-15)
  • JSON numbers: basis beneficiary, children taxed_low, exemption 0 DOP, brackets (marginal): from 0 DOP: 3% (0.03); other transfer costs 0% (0.0).
  • Gifts: Gifts taxed at 25%-27% of the value (PwC: 27% withholding; Deloitte: 25%). (same as inheritance: False; tax-free after years: None)
  • Foreign assets: Foreign personal (movable) property included if the deceased had Dominican nationality or last domicile in the DR.
  • Residence/domicile rules: Last domicile in the DR or Dominican nationality.
  • Business relief covers listed shares: False

Open points for this tax: Gift tax rate 25% (Deloitte) vs 27% (PwC) conflict; probably 27% following the general rate. | Whether foreign real estate of a domiciled deceased is included (Deloitte mentions foreign personal property only).

Wealth tax

Standard regime

No general wealth tax for individuals. A 1% net worth tax applies only as a minimum tax for individuals carrying on a trade or business in the DR (Deloitte).

  • Tax year / confidence / verification: 2026 / medium / partially_verified (checked 2026-10-15)
  • JSON type: none
  • Property tax (not a wealth tax): IPI (Impuesto al Patrimonio Inmobiliario): 1% a year on the value of an individual's Dominican real estate above an indexed threshold (not re-verified); 3% transfer tax on real estate purchases.

Special regimes

  • New resident: foreign investment income untaxed until the third tax year (new_resident_two_years; EU, US, OTHER; duration 2 years): Foreign individuals who become resident are taxed on foreign-source investment income only from the third taxable year in which they are resident (Deloitte 2026). Eligibility: Foreign individuals who become Dominican tax residents.
  • Metrics that differ from standard: capital_gains, dividends (details in the tax sections above).

Company taxation (kept separate from personal tax)

Headline CIT rate: 27% (0.27). 27% CIT (30% for 2026-2028 for taxpayers with income of DOP 1 billion or more, Law 30-26); 1% asset tax as minimum; 10% dividend WHT. Free-zone regimes.
