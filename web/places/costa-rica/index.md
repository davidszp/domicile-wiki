## Tax on investments, inheritance and wealth

Costa Rica taxes individuals only on Costa Rican-source income, so a foreign portfolio's dividends and gains are not taxed locally. Local capital gains and dividends are taxed at 15%. There is no inheritance, gift or net wealth tax; property tax is 0.25% plus a luxury home tax on expensive homes. Foreign passive income held through a Costa Rican company in a multinational group can be taxed.

- **Ordinary tax residence (territorial)**: Individuals are taxed only on Costa Rican-source income. Foreign dividends, interest and gains are not taxed for individuals. Costa Rican-source capital gains and dividends 15%.

## Income tax

Territorial tax on Costa Rican-source income. Employment income is taxed monthly at 0% to CRC 918,000, then 10%, 15%, 20% and 25% above CRC 4,727,000 a month; self-employed income uses a separate annual scale starting above CRC 6,244,000. Employees pay about 10.84% to the CCSS and others.

## How to get residence

Costa Rica grants renewable two-year residence for investment (USD 150,000), passive income (rentista, USD 2,500 a month) or a pension (USD 1,000 a month), with permanent residence after three years. A separate non-residency digital nomad visa needs USD 3,000 a month. All groups (EU, US, other) use the same routes.

- **Inversionista (investor) residence** (Investor residence; EU citizens, US citizens, other passports; open): Two-year renewable residence for investing at least USD 150,000 (USD 100,000 for forestry) in Costa Rica; permanent residence after 3 years.
- **Rentista residence** (Passive income / retirement; EU citizens, US citizens, other passports; open): Two-year renewable residence for people with USD 2,500 a month of non-work income for two years, or a USD 60,000 deposit.
- **Pensionado residence** (Passive income / retirement; EU citizens, US citizens, other passports; open): Two-year renewable residence for retirees with a lifetime pension of at least USD 1,000 a month.
- **Digital nomad visa (Law 10008)** (Digital nomad; EU citizens, US citizens, other passports; open): A one-year stay, renewable once, for remote workers earning USD 3,000 a month (USD 4,000 for a family). It is not a residency category.

Citizenship: Seven years of legal residence, or five for nationals of Central American and Ibero-American countries and Spain; language and history tests (details not read officially).

## Inheritance law and trusts

Costa Rica has freedom of testation. A will must, however, secure maintenance (alimentos) for young or disabled children, the spouse and parents who need it (Civil Code art. 595); the legítima of the pre-1888 code no longer exists. Trusts exist by statute (fideicomiso), foundations are non-profit, and Costa Rica is not in the Hague Trusts Convention.

#### Residence and eligibility

Permanent residents or six months' presence (Deloitte Costa Rica Highlights 2026). Territorial basis (PwC reviewed 30 Jun 2026).

#### Capital gains

Costa Rican-source gains 15% (Law 7092/9635), optional 2.25% of price on first sale under conditions. Foreign gains not taxed. JSON domestic 0.15, foreign 0.

#### Dividends

Local dividends 15% WHT; foreign dividends untaxed. JSON 0.15 / 0.

#### Inheritance and gifts to children

None (Deloitte). JSON basis none.

#### Wealth tax

None. Property tax 0.25%; luxury home tax not detailed.

#### Special regimes

None.

#### Company taxation (kept separate from personal tax)

CIT 30%; foreign passive income taxable for non-qualified group entities.
