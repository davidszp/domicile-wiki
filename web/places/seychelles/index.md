## Tax on investments, inheritance and wealth

Seychelles levies personal income tax on employment emoluments and business tax on business income; there is no capital gains tax, and foreign-source dividends and investment income of a private individual are not taxed (territorial system). No wealth, inheritance or gift tax; foreigners owning residential property pay an annual immovable property tax of 0.25% of market value. Residence for foreigners needs a permit. Low-to-medium confidence: no Big-4 summary was available.

- **Seychelles residence**: Territorial: no CGT; foreign dividends untaxed; no wealth or inheritance tax.

## Inheritance law and trusts

Children are reserved heirs under the Civil Code (art. 913): reportedly one half with one child, two thirds with two, three quarters with three or more. The Constitutional Court upheld this in 2013. Seychelles has a Trusts Act 2021 and Foundations Act 2009 that offer anti-forced-heirship protection.

#### Residence and eligibility

Generally resident if present 183+ days in a year (not verified against the statute).

- **Residence permit / Gainful Occupation Permit** (EU, US, OTHER): Foreigners need a residence permit (e.g. for retirees, investors or dependants) or a Gainful Occupation Permit; foreign purchase of property requires government sanction. Terms not verified.

#### Capital gains

Capital gains are not subject to tax in Seychelles (Lloyds Bank Trade). Business tax applies only to Seychelles-sourced business income (territorial since 2019, Business Tax Act 2009 as amended).

- **Rate basis:** No CGT; all rates 0.
- **Professional trader:** Gains of a business (trading) could fall under business tax; private investors not affected.
- **Confidence:** medium (unverified), verified 2026-10-15.

#### Dividends

Individuals' income tax is levied on employment emoluments (SRC). Foreign dividends of a private individual are not taxed. Seychelles-company dividends: 15% WHT to non-residents; 0% between resident entities.

- **Rate basis:** Foreign dividends 0 (outside both the employment-income tax and Seychelles-source business tax). Domestic rate unknown (null).
- **Outbound WHT:** 15% WHT on dividends, interest and royalties paid to non-residents; 0% between resident entities (Lloyds).
- **Treaty network:** Treaty network size not verified; no treaty with the United States.
- **Confidence:** low (unverified), verified 2026-10-15.

#### Wealth tax

No net wealth tax.

- **Property taxes:** Foreigners who own residential immovable property pay an annual immovable property tax of 0.25% of market value (Lloyds). Stamp duty 5% on purchases by residents; special rules for non-residents. Not a wealth tax.
- **Confidence:** medium (unverified), verified 2026-10-15.
- **JSON:** type `none`.

#### Special regimes

None modelled. No special tax regime for newly arriving wealthy residents was found.

#### Company taxation (kept separate from personal tax)

Business tax 25% on the first SCR 1M of taxable income and 33% above (Uniwide, 2019 figures; not verified for 2026). Companies with economic substance can be exempt on foreign passive income.
