Tax on investments, inheritance and wealth

Croatia taxes dividends at a flat 12%. Gains on securities are taxed at 12% only if they are sold within 2 years, and are untaxed after that. Real estate gains are 24% within 2 years. Children are exempt from inheritance and gift tax, and there is no wealth tax. No special regime for wealthy arrivals.

  • Ordinary tax residence: Worldwide taxation of residents; capital income taxed at a flat 12% final rate.
  • Family holding company (d.o.o.): The family holds the portfolio through a Croatian d.o.o. owned by the parents. Dividends received from companies are not taxed in the company (Art. 6(1) item 1 Profit Tax Act), with no minimum holding. Realised gains on shares are part of its profit and bear profit tax at 18% (10% only if the company's revenue is below EUR 1m a year). Money taken out as dividends is taxed again at 12%. Wealth tax (none) and inheritance tax (direct line exempt) are the same as for direct holding, so those metrics are inherited from the standard regime. For a Croatian resident, personal holding is the lower-tax route (gains on financial assets are untaxed after 2 years and dividends bear 12%); the company is shown because advisers use it to defer the 12% on dividends.

Income tax for employees and remote workers

Cities set their own income tax rates. Zagreb applies 23% up to EUR 60,000 of annual taxable income and 33% above (decision NN 28/2025, in force from 1 March 2025; no 2026 change found). The basic personal allowance is EUR 600 a month (EUR 7,200 a year). Employees pay 20% pension contributions (15% + 5%) up to EUR 143,496 a year; employer pays health insurance.

Inheritance law and trusts

Children, a spouse and partners are guaranteed half of their intestate share, and dependent parents a third. A foreign resident can choose the law of their nationality (EU Succession Regulation art. 22). Croatia has no trusts, and foundations are for public benefit only.

Research notes

Croatia — family-office tax dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-16 Tax year: 2026 Reviewer: research agent (Claude), unreviewed by a tax adviser

Summary

Income Tax Act (Zakon o porezu na dohodak, NN 115/16 as amended to 152/24). Dividends and interest are taxed at a flat 12%. Gains on financial assets are taxed at 12% only if sold within 2 years. Real estate gains are taxed at 24% within 2 years, and both are untaxed after that. Children are exempt from inheritance and gift tax and from real estate transfer tax. No wealth tax. No special regime for wealthy arrivals.

Residence and eligibility

Resident if a home in Croatia is owned or available for an uninterrupted 183+ days across one or two calendar years (actual use irrelevant), or physical presence is 183+ days. Note that simply keeping a Croatian home makes you resident. EU citizens: free movement. US/other: temporary residence permit for a purpose. No golden visa (not verified).

Capital gains

  • Art. 67: disposals of financial assets 2+ years after acquisition are not taxed. Transfers between spouses and close relatives, on divorce or inheritance are not taxed. Otherwise the rate is 12% (Art. 70; Tax Administration). Losses only offset gains of the same year. FIFO.
  • Art. 58/62: real estate sales taxed at 24% only within 2 years of acquisition, or if more than three same-type properties are sold in five years. Own or close-family home is exempt.
  • Gifted assets sold within 2 years of the donor's acquisition: donor's date applies.
  • JSON: all long-term rates 0.0; headline_rate = 0.24 (real estate within 2 years).
  • Business-like trading is taxed as self-employment income at progressive local rates.

Dividends

Flat 12% final tax (Art. 70) on domestic and foreign dividends. Foreign tax credited per item on proof from the foreign tax authority (Art. 80). With 15% foreign WHT, no Croatian tax remains (excess credit not refunded). JSON: 0.12/0.12, credit. Outbound WHT 10% to non-resident companies, 0% to qualifying EU/EEA/Swiss parents (PwC). About 70 treaties. A US treaty was signed in 2022 but is not confirmed in force.

Inheritance and gifts to children

Local Taxes Act (NN 115/16 … 152/24): 4% on cash, receivables, securities and movables over EUR 6,700. Spouses, direct-line descendants and ancestors, and adoptive relations are exempt for inheritances and gifts (Tax Administration "Darovanja"). Real estate goes through the 3% real estate transfer tax, with direct-line descendants and ancestors exempt. JSON: fully_exempt, no brackets.

  • Surviving spouse (including out-of-wedlock and life partners): exempt from the 4% inheritance and gift tax; the 3% real estate transfer tax exemption covers direct-line relatives only.

Wealth tax

None. From 1 Jan 2025, a mandatory municipal property tax of EUR 0.60–8.00/m² applies to residential property not used for permanent living or long-term rental. It replaced the holiday-home tax (CMS). JSON: type = none.

Special regimes

None for wealthy arrivals. The 2025 repatriation incentive (5-year salary exemption for returning emigrants) covers only employment income.

Structure regime holding_doo (family d.o.o.), added 2026-10-16 for the 60-year projection: gains 18% (10% below EUR 1m revenue), dividends 0% (deducted from the base), payout to the family 12%, wealth tax and inheritance inherited from the standard regime. Personal holding is cheaper for a Croatian resident (gains untaxed after 2 years, Art. 67 para. 8; dividends 12%). Foundation and trust were not added: no family-wealth foundation or trust vehicle in common use was found (not researched against a statute).

Company taxation (kept separate from personal tax)

Profit tax (Art. 28 Profit Tax Act): 18%, or 10% when the period's revenue is below EUR 1,000,000. Dividends and profit shares received are deducted from the tax base (Art. 6(1) item 1) when the payer is subject to profit tax or a similar tax, is a legal form comparable to a capital company and did not deduct the payment; the article states no percentage or holding period. Realised gains on shares are taxable profit (PwC); unrealised revaluation gains are left out of the base (Art. 6(1) item 2). Distributions to individuals bear 12% (Art. 70 para. 19 Income Tax Act).

  • Prime rent: EUR 3,500 a month = median asking rent of 47 detached family houses (at EUR 1,000 or more) on Nekretnine.hr on 2026-10-15 in Šestine-Mlinovi, Gračani, Podsljeme, Pantovčak, Tuškanac, Gornji Grad-Medveščak and Srebrnjak. Sample drawn from 396 detached houses for rent in Zagreb.
