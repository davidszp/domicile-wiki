Tax on investments, inheritance and wealth

The Federated States of Micronesia taxes wages and salaries (6% on the first USD 11,000, 10% above) and the gross revenue of businesses (3% above USD 10,000). There is no tax on an individual's investment income or capital gains outside a business, and no inheritance, gift or wealth tax was found. Based on the 2014 FSM Code; later amendments and state-level taxes were not checked.

  • Ordinary residence: Wage and salary tax plus a gross revenue tax on businesses; no tax on private investment income.

Inheritance law and trusts

Succession is governed by each of the four states (Chuuk, Kosrae, Pohnpei, Yap) with custom, not by one national law. No fixed heirship share was found. Trust and foundation law and Hague status are not confirmed.

Micronesia (Federated States of) — family-office tax dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15 Tax year: 2026 Reviewer: research agent (Claude), unreviewed by a tax adviser

Summary

The Federated States of Micronesia taxes wages and salaries (6% on the first USD 11,000, 10% above) and the gross revenue of businesses (3% above USD 10,000). There is no tax on an individual's investment income or capital gains outside a business, and no inheritance, gift or wealth tax was found. Based on the 2014 FSM Code; later amendments and state-level taxes were not checked.

US citizens: US citizens remain subject to US federal income, estate and gift tax on worldwide income and assets wherever they live; the app adds the federal layer. Where local tax on portfolio income is nil there is nothing to credit against US tax.

Residence and eligibility

No tax on investment income, so no relevant residence test.

  • Entry/residence permit (EU, US, OTHER): Not researched.

Capital gains

Standard regime

No capital gains tax: national taxes are the wage and salary tax (54 FSMC 121) and the gross revenue tax on businesses (54 FSMC 141).

  • Tax year / confidence / verification: 2026 / medium / partially_verified (checked 2026-10-15)
  • JSON numbers: headline 0% (0.0); listed domestic 0% (0.0); listed foreign 0% (0.0); private business sale 0% (0.0); local real estate 0% (0.0); foreign real estate 0% (0.0).
  • How derived: 54 FSMC 112(5): 'gross revenue' is receipts of a trade or business (including returns on the capital of the business); a non-business investor's gains are outside both taxes.
  • Trader recharacterisation: A business dealing in securities or property would pay 3% gross revenue tax on its receipts (above USD 10,000).

Dividends

Standard regime

No tax on dividends received by an individual outside a business.

  • Tax year / confidence / verification: 2026 / medium / partially_verified (checked 2026-10-15)

  • How derived: 54 FSMC 121 (wages) and 141 (business gross revenue) are the national income taxes; dividends of a private investor are in neither base.
  • Treaty network: No comprehensive treaties found (not verified). US dividends bear 30% US WHT.
