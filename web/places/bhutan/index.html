Tax on investments, inheritance and wealth

Bhutan's Income Tax Act 2025 took effect on 1 January 2026: progressive personal rates up to 30% above Nu 3.5M, dividends above Nu 300,000 taxed at 10%, and (per press reports of the Act) no capital gains tax on individuals' non-business assets. No wealth, inheritance or gift tax was found. Practically, Bhutan offers no residence route for a wealthy foreign family; the Gelephu Mindfulness City special region is still being set up. Research is thin (no Big-4 summary exists): low confidence.

  • Bhutan tax residence: Income Tax Act of Bhutan 2025: 10% on dividends above Nu 300,000; gains on personal assets reportedly untaxed.

Inheritance law and trusts

Bhutan's Inheritance Act 1980 lets a person pass property by written or oral will, with shares for the family fixed by the Act when there is no will. Non-Bhutanese, including foreign spouses and children, cannot inherit land or houses and are shut out of any share of an inheritance. There is no trust or private foundation law.

Residence and eligibility

Defined in the Income Tax Act of Bhutan 2025; not verified in this pass.

  • No investor residence route (EU, US, OTHER): Bhutan admits foreigners mainly on work permits; no residence-by-investment route was found. Gelephu Mindfulness City (a special administrative region with its own laws) plans its own residency; status not verified.

Capital gains

Press reports on the Income Tax Act of Bhutan 2025 (in force 1 January 2026) state there is no capital gains tax for individuals selling personal assets outside business use. Treatment of listed shares, foreign securities and real estate was not confirmed from the Act.

  • Rate basis: Scored 0 for securities and private shares on the basis of the reported 'no CGT on personal assets'. Real estate left null: not verified (a property transfer tax existed under the old law).
  • Confidence: low (unverified), verified 2026-10-15.

Dividends

Dividends up to Nu 300,000 a year are exempt and dividends above are taxed at 10% (Income Tax Act 2025, as passed by the National Assembly; 10% TDS on dividends for resident and non-resident individuals per the DRC notification).

  • Rate basis: 10% flat on dividends above Nu 300,000. Foreign dividends assumed taxed the same way (not verified); credit method assumed.
  • Outbound WHT: 10% TDS on dividends paid to resident and non-resident individuals (DRC notification).
  • Treaty network: Very limited (treaties with India and Bangladesh reported; not verified).
  • Confidence: low (partially_verified), verified 2026-10-15.
  • JSON numbers: headline 0.1, domestic 0.1, foreign gross 0.1, credit method credit.
