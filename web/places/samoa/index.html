Tax on investments, inheritance and wealth

Samoa taxes residents on worldwide income at up to 27%. Its capital gains tax (27%) applies only to capital assets held for three years or less; gains on assets held longer, and on foreign assets bought before becoming resident, are exempt. Dividends from Samoan companies are exempt, but foreign dividends are taxable at up to 27% with a foreign tax credit. No inheritance, gift or wealth tax was found.

  • Ordinary tax residence: Residents taxed on worldwide income (0/20/27%); CGT 27% only on assets held 3 years or less; Samoan dividends exempt.

Inheritance law and trusts

Samoa has no fixed heirship share found in this research, but custom and communal (matai) titles and land sit outside ordinary wills. Samoa has trust law and a Foundations Act 2016. The Hague Trusts Convention is not in force.

Research notes

Samoa — family-office tax dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15 Tax year: 2026 Reviewer: research agent (Claude), unreviewed by a tax adviser

Summary

Samoa taxes residents on worldwide income at up to 27%. Its capital gains tax (27%) applies only to capital assets held for three years or less; gains on assets held longer, and on foreign assets bought before becoming resident, are exempt. Dividends from Samoan companies are exempt, but foreign dividends are taxable at up to 27% with a foreign tax credit. No inheritance, gift or wealth tax was found.

US citizens: US citizens remain subject to US federal income, estate and gift tax on worldwide income and assets wherever they live; the app adds the federal layer. Where local tax on portfolio income is nil there is nothing to credit against US tax.

Residence and eligibility

Income Tax Act 2012 s. 6: resident individual if the individual has a home in Samoa during the year, or is present for 183 days in any 12-month period commencing or ending in the tax year (part-year rules on arrival and departure).

  • Residence permit (EU, US, OTHER): Not researched.

Capital gains

Standard regime

Capital gains tax at 27% applies to a resident's net capital gain on disposal of capital assets anywhere, but gains on assets held for more than three years are exempt, as are gains on foreign (non-Samoan) assets acquired before becoming resident and on the principal residence. Cost is uplifted by 5% a year. For a long-term investor the rate is 0%.

  • Exemptions and thresholds: Income Tax Act 2012 s. 66(1): (a) assets acquired on or before 31 December 1989; (b) principal place of residence; (c) assets held for more than 3 years; (d) non-Samoan assets acquired before becoming resident; (f) estate disposals that would have been exempt for the deceased. s. 65(2): cost increased by 5% per annum.
  • Important exceptions, statute references and announced changes: s. 63 and Schedule 1 para (8): CGT rate 27%. The 2025 IR2 return still asks 'Did you make a capital gain on an asset you bought and sold within three years?' at 27%. Gains on assets acquired for resale (s. 16(1)(b)) or held in a business are ordinary income at up to 27%.
  • Tax year / confidence / verification: 2026 / medium / partially_verified (checked 2026-10-15)
  • JSON numbers: headline 27% (0.27); listed domestic 0% (0.0); listed foreign 0% (0.0); private business sale 0% (0.0); local real estate 0% (0.0); foreign real estate 0% (0.0).
  • How derived: Assumes holding periods over 3 years (guide: held longer than any holding period), so s. 66(1)(c) exempts the gain: 0 for all classes. Short-term (<= 3 years) gains: 27% with credit for foreign tax (s. 67).
  • Holding period: More than 3 years = exempt; 3 years or less = 27% CGT.
  • Trader recharacterisation: Net gains from assets acquired with the purpose of disposal at a profit are property income (s. 16(1)(b)) and business gains are business income, both at up to 27%.
  • Foreign assets: Residents' net capital gain includes worldwide disposals (s. 64(1)); foreign assets acquired before becoming resident are exempt (s. 66(1)(d)).

Scoring note: the family's portfolio is long-term, so the scored CGT is 0%; active rebalancing within 3 years would be taxed at 27% on the gain (after the 5% annual cost uplift).

Dividends

Standard regime

Dividends paid by a resident (Samoan) company are exempt income (Schedule 2 Part A (1)(b)). Foreign dividends are property income of a resident (s. 16(1)(a)), taxed at the individual rates (27% above WST 25,000 per the 2025 return), with a foreign tax credit for foreign tax paid (s. 57).

  • Exemptions and thresholds: 2025 individual rates (IR2 2025): 0% to WST 15,000; 20% on 15,000-25,000; 27% above 25,000. (The 2012 Act text had 0% to 12,000, 10%, 20%, 27% bands.)
  • Tax year / confidence / verification: 2026 / medium / partially_verified (checked 2026-10-15)
  • JSON numbers: headline 27% (0.27); domestic 0% (0.0); foreign gross 27% (0.27); foreign tax relief: credit.
  • How derived: Domestic: Schedule 2 Part A (1)(b) exempts 'a dividend paid by a resident company' = 0. Foreign: top individual rate 27% on property income before the s. 57 foreign tax credit (credit limited to Samoan tax on that income).
  • Outbound withholding: Schedule 1 (4) lists non-resident WHT of 15% on interest, royalties and fees; dividends of resident companies are not in the list (no dividend WHT found).
  • Treaty network: No comprehensive tax treaty found; Samoa has 17 tax information exchange agreements and applies the Multilateral Convention on Mutual Administrative Assistance (Ministry for Revenue). US dividends bear 30% US WHT, creditable up to the Samoan tax.

Inheritance and gifts to children

Standard regime

No inheritance, estate or gift tax was found among the taxes administered by the Ministry for Revenue. For CGT, transmission of an asset on death to the personal representative or a beneficiary is not a disposal giving rise to a gain (s. 73(1)(b)). A lifetime gift is a non-arm's-length disposal at market value (s. 74), which only matters for assets held 3 years or less.

  • Tax year / confidence / verification: 2026 / low / partially_verified (checked 2026-10-15)
  • JSON numbers: basis none; children fully_exempt; exemption 0; brackets []; other transfer costs 0.0.
  • Gift tax: No gift tax found. A gift of an asset held 3 years or less can trigger 27% CGT on the market-value gain.
  • Foreign assets: No Samoan transfer tax found on any assets.

Wealth tax

Standard regime

No net wealth tax and no recurring levy on financial assets.

  • Tax year / confidence / verification: 2026 / medium / partially_verified (checked 2026-10-15)
  • JSON: type none.
  • Property tax (not a wealth tax): Not researched.

Special regimes

None found for wealthy new residents.

Company taxation (kept separate from personal tax)

Headline rate in JSON: 27% (0.27). Company income tax 27% (Income Tax Act 2012 Schedule 1 (2)). Dividends from resident companies exempt. Samoa also has an international (offshore) companies regime, not researched.
