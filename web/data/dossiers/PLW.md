# Palau — family-office tax dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026
Reviewer: research agent (Claude), unreviewed by a tax adviser

## Summary

Palau has no general personal income tax: after the January 2023 tax reform (10% Palau Goods and Services Tax, Business Profits Tax, reformed Wages Tax) the Ministry of Finance states that the regime 'does not include a general personal income tax'. Investment income and capital gains of individuals are therefore untaxed as far as found. No inheritance, gift or wealth tax was found.

US citizens: US citizens remain subject to US federal income, estate and gift tax on worldwide income and assets wherever they live; the app adds the federal layer. Where local tax on portfolio income is nil there is nothing to credit against US tax.

## Residence and eligibility

No personal income tax on investment income, so no relevant tax residence test. Palau's 'digital residency' card is an ID product, not tax or immigration residence.
