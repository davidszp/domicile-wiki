# Solomon Islands — family-office tax dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026
Reviewer: research agent (Claude), unreviewed by a tax adviser

## Summary

Solomon Islands taxes residents on worldwide income at up to 40% (above SBD 60,000, after a SBD 30,080 tax-free threshold). Dividends, domestic and foreign, are income: Solomon dividends bear 20% withholding credited against the 40% scale; foreign dividends get a credit for overseas tax. There is no specific capital gains tax, and capital gains of a private investor appear untaxed (low confidence). No inheritance, gift or wealth tax was found.

US citizens: US citizens remain subject to US federal income, estate and gift tax on worldwide income and assets wherever they live; the app adds the federal layer. Where local tax on portfolio income is nil there is nothing to credit against US tax. Solomon Islands tax on foreign dividends (up to 40%) generally exceeds the US rate, so the local tax is the binding layer.

## Residence and eligibility

Income Tax Act Cap. 123 s. 2: an individual is resident if he resides in Solomon Islands except for temporary absences; deemed resident if present more than six months in aggregate in the year, or the Commissioner is satisfied he intends to reside more than six months, or he is present under an employment contract of at least six months.

- **Residence permit** (EU, US, OTHER): Not researched.

## Capital gains

### Standard regime

No specific capital gains tax. Tax is charged on income (business gains, employment, dividends, interest, and 'income from any other source whatsoever', s. 3); capital gains of a private investor are not listed and on ordinary common-law principles are not income.

- **JSON numbers:** headline 0% (0.0); listed domestic 0% (0.0); listed foreign 0% (0.0); private business sale 0% (0.0); local real estate 0% (0.0); foreign real estate 0% (0.0).
- **How derived:** Inference from Income Tax Act Cap. 123 s. 3 (charge on income); no CGT provision found. 0 for all classes, low confidence.
- **Trader recharacterisation:** Gains from a business of dealing in shares or land are business income at up to 40%. The Act also mentions gains of a landowner from the sale of land in its deduction rules (not analysed).
- **Foreign assets:** Residents are taxed on income from all sources, but capital gains are not income.


## Dividends

### Standard regime

Dividends are charged to tax (s. 3(1)(b)) and residents are taxed on income from all sources (s. 3(2)(a)). Resident companies withhold 20% from dividends paid to resident individuals (s. 36; IRD), which is final only if the individual's total income is under SBD 10,000; otherwise the dividend is taxed on the scale (top 40%) with credit for the 20%. Foreign dividends are taxed on the scale with relief for overseas tax at the lower of the two rates (s. 44).

- **Exemptions and thresholds:** IRD: tax-free threshold SBD 30,080 from January 2020; above it 11% to 15,000, 23% to 30,000, 35% to 60,000, 40% above 60,000 (bands applied to income above the threshold as published).
- **Tax year / confidence / verification:** 2026 / medium / partially_verified (checked 2026-10-15)
- **JSON numbers:** headline 40% (0.4); domestic 40% (0.4); foreign gross 40% (0.4); foreign tax relief: credit.
- **How derived:** Top marginal rate 40% (IRD income tax page) applies to dividends of a high-income resident; the 20% dividend WHT is a credit, not a final tax, above SBD 10,000 total income (s. 36(5)). Foreign: 40% before s. 44 relief.
- **Outbound withholding:** 30% on dividends paid to non-residents (IRD withholding page); 20% to residents.
- **Treaty network:** No comprehensive treaties found (not verified). US dividends bear 30% US WHT, creditable.


## Inheritance and gifts to children

### Standard regime

No inheritance, estate or gift tax appears among the tax laws the Inland Revenue Division administers (Income Tax, Goods Tax, Sales Tax, Stamp Duty, Tax Administration). Stamp duty (Cap. 126) may apply to instruments transferring land or shares.

## Company taxation (kept separate from personal tax)

Headline rate in JSON: 30% (0.3). Resident companies 30%, non-resident companies 35% (IRD). Dividends paid out bear 20% (resident) or 30% (non-resident) withholding.
