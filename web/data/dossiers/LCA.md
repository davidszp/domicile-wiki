# Saint Lucia — family-office tax dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026
Reviewer: research agent (Claude), unreviewed by a tax adviser

## Summary

Saint Lucia taxes ordinarily resident individuals on worldwide income (top rate 30%), but capital gains are not taxed and dividends ('distributions from companies') are exempt income by statute. There is no inheritance, gift or net wealth tax. The main residual exposure for a portfolio investor is foreign interest income, which is taxable at up to 30%.

## Residence and eligibility

Permanent place of abode + presence, or 183 days (Deloitte St. Lucia Highlights 2026; PwC reviewed 12 Oct 2026). Worldwide basis for ordinarily resident individuals; Saint Lucia-source/remittance basis for others. CBI programme exists (amounts not verified).

## Capital gains

Not taxed (Deloitte; PwC). ITA Cap. 15.02 s.25(1)(zs) exempts income from trading in securities other than by way of a business. JSON rates 0. Real property stamp duty: seller 2.5-5% resident / 10% non-resident; buyer 2%; Alien Landholding Licence 7.5% for non-residents.

## Dividends

ITA s.25(1)(x) exempts 'distributions from companies' (not limited to local companies). JSON domestic 0 and foreign 0 (medium confidence). Foreign interest is taxable at 15/20/30% after XCD 25,000 allowance; local bank deposit interest exempt. No dividend WHT outbound.

## Inheritance and gifts to children

No inheritance, estate or gift tax (PwC; Deloitte). JSON basis none, fully_exempt.

## Wealth tax

None. Property tax 0.25% residential / 0.4% commercial.

## Special regimes

None researched beyond the remittance basis for residents who are not ordinarily resident.

## Company taxation (kept separate from personal tax)

CIT 30%; dividends and gains of companies not taxed.

## Cost of living notes

City: Rodney Bay / Cap Estate (Gros Islet, north of Castries), where expatriate families mostly live.

- Prime rent: USD 4,222.50 a month (2026), the midpoint of the only two 4+ bedroom rentals on Terra Caribbean's Saint Lucia listings on 15 Oct 2026 (Flamboyant Drive House, Rodney Bay, 5 bed, USD 5,500; Allamanda townhouse, Cap Estate, 4 bed, USD 2,945). Indicative only.
