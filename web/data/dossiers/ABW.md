# Aruba — family-office tax dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026
Reviewer: research agent (Claude), unreviewed by a tax adviser

## Summary

Aruba taxes residents on worldwide income (top rate 52%). Portfolio capital gains are exempt, but dividends and substantial-interest gains are taxed at a special 25% rate. There is no inheritance, gift or wealth tax. Interest on bank deposits is exempt. No wealthy-immigrant regime was verified.

## Residence and eligibility

Centre-of-existence test; worldwide basis (Deloitte Aruba Highlights 2026; BDO, law effective 1 Jan 2026).

## Capital gains

Exempt except substantial interest (25%+) at 25%. JSON: securities 0, private_business_sale 0.25.

## Dividends

Special 25% rate (BDO). Temporary 10% applied 2019-2023 only. JSON 0.25 with credit. Deposit interest exempt.

## Inheritance and gifts to children

None (Deloitte). Deemed sale of a substantial interest on death if heirs non-resident (25% income tax).

## Wealth tax

None.

## Special regimes

None verified.

## Company taxation (kept separate from personal tax)

CIT 22%; IPC regime phased out.

## Cost of living notes

City: Oranjestad (Eagle Beach / Palm Beach / Noord).

- Prime rent: USD 3,500 a month, the median of five 4-5 bedroom houses on RE/MAX Aruba's rental page (15 Oct 2026; range USD 2,360-3,600). CENTURY 21 Aruba listed a 4-bedroom Westpunt house at USD 4,000.
- International school: AWG 39,755 a year (USD 22,460), International School of Aruba, Secondary Grades 9-12, from the school's admissions page as archived by the Wayback Machine on 12 Jan 2026 (school year 2025-26; the page states no year). Grades 7-8 AWG 34,324; new students also pay a Capital Levy Fee of AWG 14,000 a year and a AWG 375 application fee.
