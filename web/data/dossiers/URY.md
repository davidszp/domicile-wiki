# Uruguay — family-office tax dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026
Reviewer: research agent (Claude), unreviewed by a tax adviser

## Summary

Uruguay taxes residents' foreign interest and dividends at 12% and, since 1 January 2026, also foreign capital gains (12% of the gain, or an elective 2.4% of sale price). New residents from 2026 can opt for an 11-year holiday on foreign capital income if they spend 183 days a year or make a qualifying investment (USD 2m real estate or about USD 100k a year in funds). There is no inheritance tax (3% ITP on inherited Uruguayan real estate) and a 0.1% wealth tax on Uruguayan assets only.

## Residence and eligibility

183 days, centre of interests, or investment presumptions (PwC reviewed 2 Sep 2026). Law 20.446 (Diario Oficial 8 Jan 2026, effective 1 Jan 2026).

## Capital gains

Standard: 12% IRPF on actual gain; election of deemed 20% of price (2.4%) or 15% for real estate (1.8%). Foreign gains newly taxed from 2026; pre-2026 listed assets stepped up to 31 Dec 2025 value. JSON 0.12 across classes.

## Dividends

Foreign dividends/interest 12% with credit; Uruguayan dividends 7%. JSON 0.07 / 0.12.

## Inheritance and gifts to children

No inheritance tax. ITP 3% of cadastral value on Uruguayan real estate inherited by children (DGI, 2006 publication; Título 19 TO 1996 art. 6). JSON basis none, taxed_low.

## Wealth tax

Impuesto al Patrimonio: residents 0.1% (PwC) on Uruguay-located net wealth above UYU 6,653,000 (family unit 13,306,000). JSON broad, net_wealth_local, exempt 6,653,000, 0.1%.

## Special regimes

new_resident_holiday_2026: year of arrival + 10 years under IRNR for foreign capital income. 183-day route needs no investment; other routes need real estate > UI 12.5m (~USD 2m) or UI 625,000/year fund capitalisation. After the holiday: 50% rate for 5 years (with UI 6.25m real estate or UI 625,000/yr), or a fixed UI 1,875,000 a year for 20 years. The permanent 7% option (2020-2025 arrivals) is closed to new arrivals.

Re-check 2026-10-15 (Law 20.446): the law's text on IMPO shows article 653 rewriting T.O. 2023 Title 7 art. 6 (foreign capital income and gains of residents now within IRPF from 2026, see article 3 on entry into force). The consolidated wording of the new-resident holiday itself was not located on IMPO, so the 1+10-year IRNR election and the closing of the permanent 7% option for 2026 arrivals still rest on Galante & Martins and Forvis Mazars. Confidence left at medium.

## Company taxation (kept separate from personal tax)

IRAE 25%; 7% dividend WHT; minimum top-up tax from 2026.

## Cost of living notes

City: Montevideo (Carrasco) / Punta del Este.

- Prime rent: USD 5,500 a month, the median of 20 four-bedroom houses for rent in Carrasco on InfoCasas (checked 15 Oct 2026; range USD 3,300-12,000).
- Price per m2: USD 4,266 (Carrasco average, mid-2025, El Observador via Global Property Guide). Punta del Este frontline luxury apartments USD 7,000-10,000/m2.
- International school: not sourced. The Uruguayan American School does not publish its fees online.
- Health insurance and housekeeper: not sourced.

## Open questions

- Law 20.446 implementing decree (6 May 2026) details on the holiday investment routes and post-holiday options (50% rate for 5 years; fixed annual payment of UI 1,875,000 for 20 years) not read in the original.
- Residents who opted for the old regime before 2026 (including the permanent 7% option) keep it; not modelled.
- [standard/capital_gains] Implementing decree of 6 May 2026 not read in the original; withholding mechanics (possible 8% final rate via local agents) still pending as of April 2026.
- [standard/wealth_tax] Conflict: PwC/calculator give a single 0.1% resident rate; another source describes progressive 0.1%-1.6% bands being phased down. 0.1% used.
- [standard/inheritance] ITP rate for inheritances confirmed only by a 2006 DGI publication.
- [new_resident_holiday_2026/capital_gains] Implementing decree details for the investment routes.
