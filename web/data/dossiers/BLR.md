# Belarus — family-office tax dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026
Reviewer: research agent (Claude), unreviewed by a tax adviser

## Summary

Belarus is under extensive EU, US and UK sanctions, which in practice rule it out for a family holding a global portfolio. From 2026 (Law No. 127-Z of 30 December 2025) employment-type income is taxed at 13% up to BYN 350,000, 25% to BYN 600,000 and 30% above, and Belarusian dividends at 13% up to BYN 350,000 and 25% above. Capital gains (securities, company shares, real estate) and foreign dividends stay at the basic 13%, with no holding-period exemption for securities. No net wealth tax and no inheritance or gift tax; inheritances and gifts from close relatives are exempt from income tax. Tax rules read from the Tax Code; no professional cross-check available.

## Capital gains

Gains on securities (Belarusian or foreign), shares in companies and real estate are taxed at the basic 13% personal income tax rate (Tax Code Art. 214(1.1)), on proceeds minus documented costs (Art. 202 for securities; property deduction Art. 211). The 25%/30% rates introduced for 2026 apply only to a listed basket of Belarus-source employment-type income and to Belarusian dividends (Art. 199(82)–(83)), not to capital gains. There is no holding-period exemption for securities or company shares. Residents may sell one dwelling, one plot, one garage etc. of each type tax-free once every five years (Art. 208(44)).

JSON values: headline rate 13% (`0.13`); listed securities domestic 13% (`0.13`), foreign 13% (`0.13`); private business sale 13% (`0.13`); local real estate 13% (`0.13`); foreign real estate 13% (`0.13`).

- **How the JSON numbers were derived:** 13% basic PIT rate (Art. 214(1.1)) on net gains; the 25%/30% bands do not include capital gains (Art. 199(82)). Real estate 13% assumes more than one sale of that property type in five years; a single sale is exempt.
- **Holding periods:** None for securities. Real estate: one sale per property type per five years exempt regardless of holding period.
- **Exemptions and thresholds:** Once per five years per property type: one house, one flat, one plot, one dacha, one garage (Art. 208(44)); gains on Belarusian government and certain bank bonds exempt. No exemption for shares or other securities.

## Dividends

Dividends are taxed at 13%; Belarusian dividends above BYN 350,000 a year are taxed at 25% on the excess from 2026 (Tax Code Art. 214(1.2), base defined in Art. 199(83) as Belarus-source dividends). Foreign dividends stay at the basic 13% rate. 0% applies to Belarusian dividends where the company distributed no profit for the five preceding years (Art. 214(6)).

JSON values: headline 25% (`0.25`); domestic 25% (`0.25`); foreign gross 13% (`0.13`); foreign tax relief: credit.

- **How the JSON numbers were derived:** Domestic top 25% above BYN 350,000 of Belarusian dividends (Art. 214(1.2)); foreign dividends 13% basic rate because the 25% band covers only Belarus-source dividends (Art. 199(83)).
- **Treaty network:** Belarus suspended parts of treaties with 27 'unfriendly' states from 1 June 2024 to 31 December 2026 (REVERA).

## Inheritance and gifts to children

There is no inheritance or gift tax in the Belarusian Tax Code. Inheritances received from individuals are exempt from personal income tax (Art. 208(21)), except royalties paid to heirs of authors. Gifts from close relatives (including parent to child) are exempt (Art. 208(22¹)); a 10% charge on such gifts above 100 base units applies only to recipients registered as able-bodied persons not employed in the economy, which does not concern a family office. Gifts from non-relatives are taxable income at 13% above small allowances.

JSON values: basis `none`, bracket mode `marginal`, children `fully_exempt`, exemption BYN 0, marginal brackets above the exemption: none; other transfer costs 0% (`0`); gifts same as inheritance: True; tax-free after years: None.

- **Gifts:** No gift tax. Gifts from close relatives exempt from income tax (Art. 208(22¹)); gifts from others taxed as income at 13%.
- **Business relief:** No business relief relevant (children exempt or not researched). Covers listed portfolios: no.

## Wealth tax

Belarus has no net wealth tax: the Tax Code's Special Part contains no such tax (its taxes on property are the real estate tax, land tax and transport tax). From 2026 the real estate tax uses multipliers for large homes (houses over 200/300 m² x2/x3; apartments over 150/250 m² x2/x3).

JSON type: `none`.

- **Property tax (not a wealth tax):** Real estate tax with coefficients 2 or 3 for large houses (>200/300 m2) and apartments (>150/250 m2) from 2026.
