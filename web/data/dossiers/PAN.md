# Panama — family-office tax dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026
Reviewer: research agent (Claude), unreviewed by a tax adviser

## Summary

Panama has a territorial tax system: foreign dividends, interest and gains on foreign securities are not taxed, and there is no inheritance, gift or wealth tax. Panamanian-source investment income is taxed lightly (10% on local securities gains, 10%/5% final WHT on local dividends). US/EU withholding taxes on the portfolio are a final cost. The balboa (PAB) is at par with the US dollar, which circulates as legal tender.

## Residence and eligibility

183 days in current or preceding year, or permanent residence (Deloitte Panama Highlights 2026). Territorial basis for everyone.

## Capital gains

Panamanian securities 10% (5% price withholding may be final); Panamanian real estate 10% or 3% of price final; foreign assets untaxed. JSON domestic 0.10, foreign 0.

## Dividends

Foreign dividends untaxed; domestic 10%/5% WHT final. JSON domestic 0.10, foreign 0.

## Inheritance and gifts to children

None (PwC reviewed 4 Aug 2026; Deloitte). JSON basis none.

## Wealth tax

None. Property tax up to 0.7% (main residence) / 1%.

## Special regimes

None needed; territorial system.

## Company taxation (kept separate from personal tax)

CIT 25% territorial; MHQ/free-zone regimes.

## Cost of living notes

City: Panama City (Costa del Este / Punta Pacifica).

- Prime rent: USD 5,500 a month, the average asking rent for 4+ bedroom units in Panama City from Global Property Guide's April 2026 survey. Mostly large apartments; gated-community houses can cost more.
- Price per m2: USD 2,500 (Costa del Este resale average, Q1 2026, Galleria Inmobiliario via Panama Equity, reported by Global Property Guide). New-build: Costa del Este USD 3,800, Punta Pacifica USD 4,300.
- International school: International School of Panama, grades 9-12, 2026-27: tuition USD 19,328 plus USD 1,000 annual non-institutional registration fee = USD 20,328 per child. One-off capital donation USD 16,000 per student excluded.
- Health insurance and housekeeper: not sourced.
