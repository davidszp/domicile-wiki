# Bermuda — family-office tax dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026
Reviewer: research agent (Claude), unreviewed by a tax adviser

## Summary

Bermuda has no personal income, capital gains, dividend or wealth tax. It does levy stamp duty on the estates of deceased persons, but only on property situated in Bermuda (Bermuda real estate, local-currency deposits, shares in Bermuda companies outside the exemptions): first BMD 100,000 nil, then 5.25%, 10.5%, 15.75% and 21% above BMD 2 million. A portfolio of foreign securities held abroad is outside the charge. Gifts of Bermuda property bear stamp duty of 2.1%-7.35%. Since 2025 a 15% corporate income tax applies to Bermuda entities of multinational groups with revenue of EUR 750 million or more.

## Residence and eligibility

No income tax, so no tax residence test. EIRC: BMD 2.5m qualifying investment (gov.bm, June 2024); 2026 Policy adds BMD 25,000 charitable donation, BMD 10,000 BDA fee and enhanced due diligence. Residential Certificate fee BMD 2,755.

## Capital gains

None for individuals (Deloitte 2026, PwC 2026). JSON 0. Sale of Bermuda land carries stamp duty of 2.1%-7.35% marginal (Head 14; gov.bm).

## Dividends

None; no withholding; no comprehensive treaties. JSON 0.

## Inheritance and gifts to children

Stamp Duties Act 1976 s.47 and Schedule Head 2 (as amended 1 April 2023): stamp duty on the affidavit of value of the deceased's estate, payable within 90 days of the grant/resealing. Bands (marginal): first BMD 100,000 nil; next 100,000 at 5.25%; next 800,000 at 10.5%; next 1,000,000 at 15.75%; above BMD 2 million 21%. (gov.bm's probate page still shows the pre-2023 5/10/15/20%; Deloitte 2026 and the consolidated Act show the 5.25-21% rates used here.) JSON: basis estate, exemption 100,000, brackets 5.25% from 0, 10.5% from 100,000, 15.75% from 900,000, 21% from 1,900,000 (relative to the amount above the exemption), children_treatment taxed. Scope: only property situated in Bermuda; s.47(3) excludes foreign-currency deposits, securities of exempted undertakings and foreign assets held through a qualifying Bermuda PIC. A foreign portfolio held abroad is therefore not charged. Deductions: surviving spouse, primary family homestead (Bermudian status only), debts. Gifts: Head 17 voluntary dispositions inter vivos, Bermuda property 2.1%-7.35% marginal, non-Bermuda property 1.05%.

## Wealth tax

None. Annual land tax on annual rental value; foreign currency purchase tax 1.25%.

## Special regimes

None.

## Company taxation (kept separate from personal tax)

No tax on profits for ordinary Bermuda companies; exempted undertakings hold tax assurance certificates to 31 March 2035. Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (law since 27 December 2023): 15% CIT for fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025, only for Bermuda entities of MNE groups with annual revenue of EUR 750 million or more (GloBE-style base; no IIR/UTPR). cit_rate recorded as 0 because a family holding or family-office company is outside scope. Payroll tax (employee portion 0.5%-12.5% marginal, 2025/26) and land tax apply.
