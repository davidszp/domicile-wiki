# Bulgaria — earned income, nomad and expat dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026

## Summary

Bulgaria taxes income at a flat 10% (euro adopted 1 Jan 2026). Employees pay 10.58% social security plus 3.2% health (13.78%) on a monthly base capped at EUR 2,111.64, rising to EUR 2,300 from 1 August 2026; freelancers pay about 27.8% on a chosen base between EUR 550.66 and the cap. A digital nomad permit launched in December 2025 requires average annual income of 50 minimum monthly wages (about EUR 27,533 to 31,010), up to 2 years. Sofia rents are about EUR 600 (1-bed) and EUR 780 (2-bed).

## Income tax and social security

Marginal rates on taxable income (EUR):

- from 0.00: 10.00%

Personal allowance deducted before the brackets: 0.00 EUR.

Employee social security and health: 13.78% (cap: 26,281.48). Self-employed: 27.80% (cap: 26,281.48).

Flat 10% personal income tax on worldwide income of residents, with some exceptions. Employee contributions 10.58% social security plus 3.2% health (13.78% combined) on a maximum monthly insurance base of EUR 2,111.64 from 1 Jan to 31 Jul 2026 and EUR 2,300 from 1 Aug 2026; the recorded cap is the time-weighted annual figure (7 x 2,111.64 + 5 x 2,300 = EUR 26,281.48).

Self-employed notes: Freelancer insurance base: EUR 550.66 minimum (to 31 Jul) then EUR 620.20 (from 1 Aug), maximum as above. Rate: one secondary source gives 27.8% (31.3% with the optional illness and maternity fund) and elsewhere about 32-33%, so the recorded 27.8% is unverified. A 25% normative expense deduction for freelancers is commonly cited but was not confirmed in any opened source. PwC's other-taxes page (opened again 2026-10-15) gives freelancer minimum and maximum insurance bases but no freelancer contribution rate, and the NSSI site opened has none either.

## Digital nomad visa

Bulgaria has had a digital nomad permit since 20 December 2025 (Foreigners Act Art. 24s): average annual gross income of at least 50 minimum monthly wages (EUR 27,533 to 31,010), one year plus one renewal, health insurance, no Bulgarian clients. Remote income is generally outside Bulgarian tax while the holder is not resident (under 183 days, centre of vital interests abroad).

## Expat regimes

No expat or impatriate regime was found.

Regimes recorded in the JSON file:

- **Digital nomad residence permit (Foreigners Act Art. 24s)** (digital_nomad_visa, treatment: standard): Type D visa then one-year residence permit for third-country nationals who are remote employees, owners of more than 25% of a foreign company, or freelancers with at least one year of activity. Average annual gross income (the Act measures gross income) of at least 50 minimum monthly wages for the previous calendar year (EUR 27,533 on the 2025 wage; EUR 31,010 on the 2026 wage; practice unsettled). Work for Bulgarian clients prohibited; health insurance of at least EUR 30,000. Under 183 days and with the centre of vital interests abroad, remote income is generally not taxable in Bulgaria; a resident pays 10%. Minimum income: 27,533 EUR. Duration (years): 2. Social security still applies: False.

## Living costs

Sofia: 1-bedroom about EUR 600, 2-bedroom about EUR 780 a month (Investropa, June 2026).

## Residence routes

EU citizens may live and work freely. US and other third-country remote workers can use the nomad permit; skilled employees need a work permit or EU Blue Card via a Bulgarian employer (not researched from an official page).
