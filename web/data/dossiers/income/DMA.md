# Dominica — earned income, nomad and expat dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026

## Summary

Dominica taxes residents at 15% / 25% / 35% on taxable income above a XCD 30,000 allowance (bands at XCD 20,000 and 50,000). Employees pay 6.75% social security up to XCD 84,000 a year; the self-employed pay 13.75% in 2026. The WIN visa (up to 18 months, USD 50,000, USD 900) comes with an official income tax waiver for people working remotely from Dominica. Rents (Diamond Realty): 1-bed EC$1,600 (one listing), 2-bed median EC$2,700 (five listings).

## Income tax and social security

Deloitte 2026: 15% on the first XCD 20,000, 25% on the next XCD 30,000, 35% above; allowance XCD 30,000; PAYE for employees; no capital gains tax; foreign tax credit available. Social security (Dominica Social Security tables, 2012-2031): employee 6.75% and employer 8% from 1 January 2026 (7.75% for the employer without redundancy), ceiling XCD 7,000 a month (Deloitte). Self-employed: 13.75% of annual insurable earnings in 2026, paid quarterly, rising 0.25 points a year to 15% in 2031.

## Digital nomad visa

WIN visa: remote work for non-Dominican clients, up to 18 months, expected income of US$50,000 or more over 12 months and/or the means to support oneself, USD 100 application plus USD 800 / USD 1,200 visa fee, valid health insurance. The official WIN site (FAQ and visa details) states that a waiver on income tax is granted to people working remotely from Dominica under the visa and that they remain responsible for tax in their home country. This is recorded as exempt with covers_local_work true: the waiver is for work done from Dominica. Statutory text was not located.

## Expat regimes

None found.

## Living costs

Diamond Realty Dominica long-term rentals (10 listings, asking rents in EC$, furnishing mixed): 1-bed EC$1,600 (Morne Daniel, one listing); 2-bed median EC$2,700 from five units (Jimmit 850 unfurnished, Wallhouse 2,000, Gommier 2,700, Guillet 3,500 furnished, Morne Daniel house 4,500). One agency, small sample: indicative only.

## Residence routes

Remote workers of any citizenship can use the WIN visa (up to 18 months, USD 50,000 expected income, USD 100 + USD 800 fees). Employees of a Dominican employer need a work permit (not researched). Citizenship by investment (Economic Diversification Fund from USD 200,000, or approved real estate from USD 200,000) is a separate route cited in data/jurisdictions/DMA.json. US citizens stay taxable in the US on worldwide income; Dominica tax on employment income would be creditable against US tax only to the extent the foreign tax credit rules allow.

## Open questions

- Deloitte and Expanship give the XCD 7,000 monthly ceiling; Expanship says it may be revised. Whether the same ceiling caps the self-employed insurable earnings was not stated by the DSS table.
- Whether the XCD 30,000 allowance is deducted before the 15/25/35% bands (as modelled) is inferred from Deloitte's wording and the Grenada and Saint Lucia pattern.
- The statutory instrument behind the waiver was not located; the statement is from the official WIN programme site.
- Renewal and the interaction of the 18-month stay with the 183-day residence test are unclear; social security for visa holders is not addressed.
- Rents rest on one agency (1 one-bed, 5 two-bed listings).
- Work-permit terms for employees not researched.
- The IRD IT4 guide (opened 2026-10-15) states a resident allowance of $15,000 (line 201) while the file uses XCD 30,000 (Deloitte 2026); the guide may predate the increase. Not changed here.
