# Bahrain - earned income, nomad and expat dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026

## Summary
Bahrain has no personal income tax (PwC, reviewed 26 Jul 2026). Employees in Bahrain contribute to the Social Insurance Organisation: expatriates pay 1% (employer 3%), Bahrainis 8% (employer 18%). Income paid outside Bahrain is not subject to Bahraini tax or social insurance rules.

## Income tax and social security
Marginal rates on taxable income (BHD):
- from 0 BHD: 0%

- Employee social security: 1%, income cap none stated.
- Self-employed social security: 0%, income cap none stated. Self-employed contributions are not covered by the PwC page; none are assumed. Foreign-paid income is outside the Bahraini social insurance rules.
- Notes: No local income-based taxes.
- Confidence: medium (partially_verified).

## Digital nomad visa
No digital-nomad visa was found in the sources opened.

## Expat regimes
No expat or impatriate regime found; the standard resident rules apply.

## Living costs
City: Manama.

- 1-bedroom: 380 BHD a month. Bahrain-wide average 1-bedroom monthly rent as of June 2026 (range BHD 250-650; Property Finder asking rents, sample size not disclosed). Not Manama-specific. Numbeo city-centre Manama 1-bedroom is BHD 375.
- 2-bedroom: 520 BHD a month. Bahrain-wide average 2-bedroom monthly rent as of June 2026 (range BHD 330-900; asking rents, luxury outliers trimmed). Not Manama-specific.

## Residence routes
EU, US and other citizens: no visa-free right to work. Skilled employees obtain a work permit and residence through a Bahraini employer. One news article (May 2026, not opened) mentions expanded digital-residency pathways, but no nomad visa terms were verified.
