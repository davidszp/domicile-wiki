# Norway — earned income, nomad and expat dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026

## Summary
Top marginal rate 47.4% for employees. Employee national insurance 7.6%, self-employed 10.8%. The standard deduction for foreign workers no longer exists (abolished 2019) and no nomad visa or expat regime was found. Oslo rent about NOK 15,989 (2-room) and NOK 19,954 (3-room).

## Income tax and social security
General income 22% after NOK 114,550 allowance; bracket tax on personal income: 1.7% (226,101), 4.0% (318,301), 13.7% (725,051), 16.8% (980,101), 17.8% (1,467,201). National insurance 7.6% employees, 10.8% self-employed (Skatteetaten 2026).

## Digital nomad visa
None found.

## Expat regimes
The brief asked about the 'standard deduction for foreign workers'. KPMG's 2019 alert says it was abolished from 1 January 2019 (it had been 10% of income, max NOK 40,000). PwC's 2026 deductions page does not list it. The 25% flat PAYE regime applies to non-residents on temporary work, not to residents, so it is not recorded as a regime.

## Living costs
Oslo, active contracts Q2 2026: 2-room NOK 15,989; 3-room NOK 19,954.

## Residence routes
EU: register. Others: employer-tied permit.

## Open questions
- Minimum deduction (minstefradrag) not modelled in the brackets; effective tax is a little lower than the model for employees.
- No nomad visa or expat tax regime found. The standard deduction for foreign workers was abolished from 1 January 2019 (KPMG); the 25% PAYE source-tax scheme is for non-residents on temporary work only.
- Residence routes not verified on UDI.
- Minimum deduction (minstefradrag) is 46% of employment income, maximum NOK 95,700 and minimum NOK 4,000 (PwC); it is not in the brackets or the NOK 114,550 personal allowance, so employee tax is overstated at salaries above about NOK 208,000.
