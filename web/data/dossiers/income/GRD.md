# Grenada — earned income, nomad and expat dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026

## Summary

Grenada charges 10% / 28% on taxable income above a XCD 36,000 allowance (band at XCD 24,000), but Deloitte says residents are taxed only on Grenadian-source income. Employees pay 6.25% NIS up to XCD 60,000 a year. The Remote Employment Act 2021 gives a renewable one-year permit (EC$100,000 income, USD 1,500); section 9(1) provides that no income tax is payable on income generated outside Grenada under the Act, read as covering remote work done while living there. Rents (Terra Caribbean, asking): 1-bed USD 1,450, 2-bed USD 1,800.

## Income tax and social security

Deloitte 2026: 10% on the first XCD 24,000, 28% above; personal allowance XCD 36,000; residents (183 days) taxed on Grenada-derived income; PAYE for employees. NIS: employee 6.25%, employer 7.25%, ceiling XCD 5,000 a month. Self-employed NIS 13.5% on XCD 5,200 a month is unverified.

## Digital nomad visa

Remote Employment Act 2021 (Act No. 3 of 2021): apply with proof of EC$100,000 annual income from outside Grenada, health insurance, police certificate and USD 1,500 (USD 2,000 with up to three dependants). Valid up to one year, renewable for periods of up to one year with no stated cap. Section 9(1): no income tax is payable on income generated outside Grenada in accordance with the Act. The permit authorises working remotely in Grenada (s.3(1)) but bars Grenadian clients and a local business (s.3(2)(b)), so the exemption is read as covering pay for work done while living there (covers_local_work true, medium confidence: the phrase 'generated outside of Grenada' is undefined).

## Living costs

Terra Caribbean Grenada (144 priced residential rentals, asking rents in USD, furnishing not stated): 1-bed median USD 1,450 from 47 apartments and studios (range 650 to 3,300); 2-bed median USD 1,800 from 52 apartments and townhouses (range 810 to 5,300). Areas: Grand Anse, Lance aux Epines, True Blue, Point Salines, Morne Rouge, Calliste, Egmont and nearby, all in St George.

## Residence routes

Remote workers of any citizenship (other than Grenadian and OECS Protocol states) can use the one-year renewable remote-work permit (EC$100,000 income from abroad, health insurance, police certificate, USD 1,500). Employees of a Grenadian employer need a work permit under the Foreign Nationals and Commonwealth Citizens (Employment) Act (not researched). Citizenship by investment is a separate route (amounts not verified in data/jurisdictions/GRD.json). US citizens remain taxable in the US on worldwide income; Grenada does not tax foreign income of residents per Deloitte.

- Allowance treatment (deducted before the 10%/28% bands) is inferred from the wording.
- Social security for permit holders is not addressed in the Act.
- Interpretation: the wording 'generated outside of Grenada' is not defined; the reading that it covers remote work done while living in Grenada rests on the Act's purpose and on s.3, not on a definition or a tax-authority ruling.
- EC$100,000 is about USD 37,000 at the XCD 2.70 peg.
- Work-permit terms not researched.
