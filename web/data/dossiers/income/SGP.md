# Singapore — earned income, nomad and expat dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026

## Summary

Residents pay progressive tax from 0% to 24% (YA2026 schedule, income of 2025); non-residents pay 24% on most income or the higher of 15% and resident rates on employment. Foreign-sourced income received in Singapore by individuals is generally exempt. Foreign nationals cannot contribute to CPF, so there is no social security cost.

- Notes: Foreign nationals and their employers are precluded from CPF; permanent residents contribute at reduced rates for two years (20% employee rate for citizens and PRs after year three, OW ceiling SGD 8,000 a month). - Local tax: No local income tax. The brackets are on chargeable income for YA2026 (income earned in 2025); the tax year 2026 schedule (YA2027) was not separately verified.

None.

## Expat regimes

### Employment Pass (EP)

Work pass for foreign professionals; not a tax regime. The minimum qualifying salary is SGD 5,600 a month at age 23 and under, rising to SGD 10,700 at 45+ (financial services SGD 6,200-11,800), and applications must also earn 40 COMPASS points unless the fixed salary is at least SGD 22,500 a month. For new applications from 1 January 2027 the minimum becomes SGD 6,000-11,500 (financial services SGD 6,600-12,700). Tax is standard; foreign income received in Singapore is generally exempt for individuals, but pay for work done in Singapore is Singapore-sourced.

- Treatment: standard
- Minimum income: SGD 67,200; duration: None years. Passes are renewable; validity length not stated on the opened page. Minimum shown is the entry level (SGD 5,600 x 12), age-dependent.
- Foreign income required: False; local employment required: True; social security applies: False
- Confidence: high (verified)

### Overseas Networks and Expertise (ONE) Pass

Five-year personalised pass for top talent: a fixed monthly salary of at least SGD 30,000 (SGD 22,500 on the AI and Tech track from 28 January 2027) from one employer, with an established overseas or Singapore company (market cap USD 500 million or revenue USD 200 million). Holders can start, operate and work for multiple companies. It does not change tax: pay for work done in Singapore is taxed at the normal resident rates, and as a foreigner the holder has no CPF.

- Treatment: standard
- Minimum income: SGD 360,000; duration: 10 years. Up to 5 years for first-time holders, renewable for 5 years each time (10 shown as first term plus one renewal).
- Foreign income required: False; local employment required: True; social security applies: False
- Confidence: high (verified)

## Living costs

City: Singapore.

- 1-bedroom rent: SGD 3,750 a month (2026). Numbeo average 1-bedroom apartment in city centre, 1,689 entries from 269 contributors in 12 months (all city price data); range SGD 2,500-6,000.
- 2-bedroom rent: SGD 4,300 a month (2026). Median monthly rent for a 2-bedroom private condo or apartment in the Rest of Central Region (9,946 URA rental contracts, leases starting Q4 2025 to Q3 2026; middle 50% SGD 3,900-4,850), computed by PropKaki from URA data. Contract rents, whole units; furnishing not stated. Core Central Region median is SGD 5,300 and Outside Central Region SGD 3,600. Cove's April 2026 listing median for 2-bedrooms is SGD 5,000 (furnished, all of Singapore).

2-bedroom is a regional median from URA contract data, not a city-centre-only figure; the 1-bedroom is a Numbeo city-centre average.

## Residence routes

EU, US and other citizens: there is no digital nomad visa; remote workers cannot use a tourist stay to work. Skilled employees need an Employment Pass (SGD 5,600+ a month and COMPASS points, or SGD 22,500+ a month exempt from COMPASS). The Overseas Networks and Expertise (ONE) Pass for high earners was not verified on an opened page.
