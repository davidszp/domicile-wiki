# Saint Lucia — earned income, nomad and expat dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026

## Summary

Saint Lucia charges 15% / 20% / 30% on taxable income above a XCD 25,000 allowance (bands at XCD 15,000 and 30,000). Employees pay 5% NIC up to XCD 60,000 a year; the self-employed rate could not be found (10% derived, unverified). There is no nomad visa or expat tax regime. Rents: 2-bed USD 1,748 from four Terra Caribbean listings; no 1-bed.

## Income tax and social security

Deloitte 2026: 15% on the first XCD 15,000 of taxable income, 20% on the next 15,000, 30% above; personal allowance XCD 25,000; residents with a permanent abode taxed on worldwide income; no capital gains tax. NIC (PwC, reviewed 12 October 2026): 5% employee up to XCD 250 a month (XCD 5,000 of salary), employer matches. Self-employed rate not found on any source opened: 10% is a derived placeholder (5% + 5%) and is unverified.

## Digital nomad visa

No. Stamped Nomad (third party) lists Saint Lucia as having no nomad visa; remote workers enter as visitors (6 weeks, extendable) or on a multiple-entry non-immigrant visa. No tax regime for remote workers; tax residence after 183 days.

## Expat regimes

None found.

## Living costs

Terra Caribbean Saint Lucia (8 residential rentals, asking rents in USD, furnishing not stated): no 1-bedroom unit; 2-bed median USD 1,748 from four apartments (Gros Islet 1,472 and 2,024, Cap Estate 1,656, Rodney Bay 1,840). The rest are 3 to 5-bedroom houses at USD 2,760 to 5,500. Chestertons Saint Lucia (HTTP 403) and Numbeo (HTTP 429) could not be read.

## Residence routes

No remote-worker programme exists in law: the 'Live It' scheme is a tourism marketing label for the standard visitor entry (up to 6 weeks on arrival, extendable in 30-day steps at EC$200) or a multiple-entry non-immigrant visa (EC$190, 12 months) for nationals who need a visa. Skilled employees of a Saint Lucian employer need a work permit (not researched). Citizenship by investment (National Economic Fund, real estate, bonds) is a separate route; amounts not verified. EU, US and other citizens are treated alike in these routes. US citizens remain taxable in the US on worldwide income.

## Open questions

- Self-employed NIC rate not verified (10% is derived from the 5% + 5% employee and employer shares, not sourced).
- PwC's table layout was scrambled when fetched for the income tax page; rates were taken from Deloitte and the PwC text on residence.
- No 1-bed rent; the 2-bed figure rests on 4 listings from one agency.
- Work-permit terms and the status of the 'Live It' one-year option not confirmed on an official page.
- Saint Lucia: the IRD tax-facts sheet covers 2023-2025 and also caps total allowances and deductions at XCD 30,000; the self-employed 10% rate sits exactly at the 10%-of-assessable-income limit, so it is deductible only if earnings are fully insurable.
