# Latvia — earned income, nomad and expat dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026

## Summary

Latvia taxes income at 25.5% up to EUR 105,300 and 33% above, with an additional 3% on income over EUR 200,000. Employees pay 10.5% social security (31.07% for the self-employed), both on income capped at EUR 105,300. The remote work visa requires 2.5 times the average gross monthly wage (EUR 3,192.50 a month on the PMLP page) for six months, lasts one year and bars local employment. Riga rents are about EUR 720 (1-bed) and EUR 1,020 (2-bed).

## Income tax and social security

Marginal rates on taxable income (EUR):

- from 0.00: 25.50%
- from 105,300.00: 33.00%
- from 200,000.00: 36.00%

Personal allowance deducted before the brackets: 6,600.00 EUR.

Employee social security and health: 10.50% (cap: 105,300.00). Self-employed: 31.07% (cap: 105,300.00).

Progressive resident income tax: 25.5% up to EUR 105,300 of annual income, 33% above, and an additional 3% on the part above EUR 200,000 (first applied in 2026 for 2025 income), recorded as 36%. Fixed personal allowance EUR 550 a month (EUR 6,600 a year, PwC deductions page), applied regardless of income; dependant allowance EUR 250 a month not modelled. Employee state social insurance contributions (NSIC) 10.50% (9.25% for those eligible for retirement), income capped at EUR 105,300; minimum wage EUR 780 a month in 2026.

Self-employed notes: Self-employed NSIC 31.07% (29.36% if eligible for retirement) on income up to EUR 105,300, with minimum contributions based on the EUR 780 monthly minimum wage. Sole traders may apply for micro-enterprise tax status (details in the PwC corporate summary, not opened) and apply the fixed personal allowance to PIT-subject income. Self-employed persons with income below three minimum monthly wages a quarter can opt to pay 10% NSIC on actual income (PwC).

Local tax: No local income tax stated.

## Digital nomad visa

Latvia's remote work visa lasts one year and requires an OECD-based employer or self-employment, income of 2.5 times the average gross monthly wage (EUR 3,192.50 a month on the PMLP page, about EUR 38,310 a year) and Schengen health insurance with at least EUR 42,600 cover. Holders cannot take local employment. Taxes are the standard ones once the holder is resident.

## Expat regimes

No expat or impatriate regime was found.

Regimes recorded in the JSON file:

- **Long-term visa for remote work (digital nomad visa)** (digital_nomad_visa, treatment: standard): One-year long-term visa for third-country nationals employed by or self-employed in an OECD member state and able to work remotely from Latvia. Income at least the average gross monthly wage times 2.5 (EUR 3,192.50 per the PMLP page), employees with six months at the same employer, self-employed with six months of income documented. Health insurance valid in Latvia and Schengen with at least EUR 42,600 cover. No right to work for Latvian employers. No special tax regime: a stay over 183 days in a year creates tax residence and standard rates apply. Minimum income: 38,310 EUR. Duration (years): 1. Social security still applies: False.

## Living costs

Riga: 1-bedroom EUR 720, 2-bedroom EUR 1,020 a month (Investropa, June 2026).

## Residence routes

EU citizens may live and work freely. US and other non-EU remote workers can use the one-year remote work visa; skilled employees need a work-based residence permit with a Latvian employer (not researched).
