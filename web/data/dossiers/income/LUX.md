# Luxembourg — earned income, nomad and expat dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026

## Summary

Luxembourg taxes residents on a 23-bracket class 1 scale: 0% to EUR 13,230, then 8% rising by one point or two points per band to 39% from EUR 54,090, 40%, 41% and a top rate of 42% above EUR 234,870, plus a 7% solidarity surcharge (9% above EUR 150,000), giving a top rate of about 45.8%. Employees pay about 12.7% social security on income up to a ceiling of about EUR 166,280; the self-employed pay both shares, 25.15% of income by the CCSS 2026 table.

## Expat regimes

### Impatriate regime (from tax year 2025)

Half of the gross annual remuneration of an impatriate is exempt from income tax, capped at an exemption of EUR 400,000 a year. Conditions: Luxembourg resident; recruited from abroad or seconded; no residence or Luxembourg tax liability and no living within 150 km of the Luxembourg border in the previous five years; gross annual pay at least EUR 75,000; impatriates not above 30% of the company's workforce (waived for companies under ten years old); at least 75% of working time on the qualifying activity; employer reports impatriates by 31 January each year.

- Treatment: partial_exemption, exempt share 50%
- Minimum income: 75,000 EUR; maximum: none stated
- Duration: 8 years. Until the end of the eighth year after the initial start date in Luxembourg.
- Foreign income required: no; local employment required: yes; social security still due: yes

Open points: Whether social security still applies to the exempt half is not stated on the opened page; the regime is described as an income tax exemption. Self-employed people are not covered by the article (employees only). Official ACD / guichet.lu pages were not reachable.

## Residence routes

EU/EEA and Swiss citizens may live and work freely and register with the commune. Luxembourg has no digital nomad visa (not confirmed on an official page). Skilled non-EU employees need a Luxembourg employment contract and a work and residence authorisation or EU Blue Card; thresholds were not verified.
