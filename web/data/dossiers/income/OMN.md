# Oman - earned income, nomad and expat dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026

## Summary
Scored on the law in force for 2026: Oman levies no personal income tax in 2026. A personal income tax takes effect on 1 January 2028 (5% on annual income above OMR 42,000 after eligible deductions, worldwide income for residents), so it does not enter the 2026 scoring. Only Omani employees pay social insurance (8%); no expat employee contribution is listed.

## Income tax and social security
Marginal rates on taxable income (OMR):
- from 0 OMR: 0%

- Employee social security: 0%, income cap none stated.
- Self-employed social security: 0%, income cap none stated. No self-employment contributions apply to expatriates in the sources found. From 2028, residents including expatriates are taxed at 5% above OMR 42,000 a year.
- Notes: No local income-based taxes. For Omani employees, retirement contributions use a subscription wage ceiling (initially OMR 3,000).
- Confidence: high (verified).

## Digital nomad visa
No digital-nomad visa was found in the sources opened.

## Expat regimes
No expat or impatriate regime found; the standard resident rules apply.

## Living costs
City: Muscat.

- 1-bedroom: 290 OMR a month. Muscat 1-bedroom monthly rent, June 2026 reference (range OMR 180-450; Al Khuwair used as mid-market anchor, asking rents plus author judgment, sample size not disclosed). Numbeo city-centre 1-bedroom is OMR 262.
- 2-bedroom: 430 OMR a month. Muscat 2-bedroom monthly rent, 2026 (range OMR 300-710; Savills reports and dubizzle Al Mouj listings, sample size not disclosed).

## Residence routes
EU, US and other citizens: no visa-free right to work. Skilled employees obtain a work visa and residence through an Omani employer. A travel-cost site (not opened) states Oman has no dedicated digital-nomad visa; this was not confirmed from an official source.
