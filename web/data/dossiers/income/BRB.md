# Barbados — earned income, nomad and expat dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026

## Summary

Barbados taxes residents at 11.5% on taxable income up to BBD 50,000 (after a BBD 25,000 allowance) and 27.5% above, from income year 2026 (BRA). Social security is 11% for employees (cap BBD 64,320 a year) and 17% for the self-employed, plus a 0.25% uncapped levy. The Welcome Stamp (USD 50,000 a year, 12 months, USD 2,000) leaves remote income free of Barbados income tax, including work done while living there (official programme page). Rents: 1-bed in Bridgetown averages BBD 2,367 a month (Numbeo, low confidence); 2-bed median USD 2,763 (Terra Caribbean, 8 units).

## Income tax and social security

Rates: 11.5% on the first BBD 50,000 of taxable income and 27.5% above, with a BBD 25,000 basic personal allowance. The 2026 cut from 12.5%/28.5% is documented by the Barbados Revenue Authority and PwC; Deloitte's January 2026 highlights still show the old rates. National Insurance: employees 11% on insurable earnings up to BBD 5,360 a month; self-employed 17% on the same ceiling; both also pay 0.25% (uncapped) to the Resilience and Regeneration Fund. Residents who are not domiciled are taxed on Barbados-source income and foreign income only to the extent a benefit is obtained in Barbados.

## Digital nomad visa

Welcome Stamp: 12 months, USD 50,000 a year of income from outside Barbados, USD 2,000 individual or USD 3,000 family fee, remote work only. The official programme site states that holders 'will not be liable to pay Barbados Income Tax' and still pay 17.5% VAT; since the visa lets the holder work remotely from Barbados, the relief is recorded as covering work done there (covers_local_work true). The page is the Ministry of Tourism's, not the revenue authority's, so confidence is medium. Renewal is described only as 'easily reapply'.

## Expat regimes

No impatriate regime found. Residents who are not domiciled get a partial remittance-style treatment (foreign income taxed only when a benefit is obtained in Barbados), but that is a residence rule rather than a ruling-based regime and is not listed as a regime in the data.

## Living costs

1-bed: Numbeo (Bridgetown, updated 2 Oct 2026, 61 entries from 6 contributors) city centre BBD 2,367 (range 1,500 to 4,000). 2-bed: Terra Caribbean rentals (53 residential listings read, asking rents in USD, furnishing not stated): median USD 2,763 across 8 apartments and townhouses in Christ Church, St James and St Michael (range 1,100 to 3,500); all 11 two-bed units island-wide have a median of USD 2,800. Numbeo did not give a 2-bed figure and returned HTTP 429 on retry.

## Residence routes

EU, US and other citizens use the same route for remote work: the 12-month Welcome Stamp (USD 50,000 a year of income from outside Barbados; USD 2,000 individual or USD 3,000 family fee). Skilled employees of a Barbadian employer need a local work permit and a long-term residence status; the Special Entry and Reside Permit (SERP) is a separate high-net-worth/retiree route. Permit thresholds, fees and processing for employees were not verified on an official page. US citizens stay taxable in the US on worldwide income regardless of the Welcome Stamp exemption.

## Open questions

- Deloitte's January 2026 highlights still show the old 12.5% / 28.5% rates; the BRA policy note (income year 2026: 11.5% on BBD 25,001 to 75,000, 27.5% above) and PwC agree on the new rates, which are used. The BRA page implies but does not state that the BBD 25,000 allowance is unchanged (Deloitte confirms it).
- Compensatory income credit and other credits (BRA policy note OGC 001/2026) not researched.
- The exemption is stated on the Ministry of Tourism programme pages, not in a revenue-authority ruling or the statute; the legal instrument was not found.
- Whether National Insurance is due for a holder is not addressed.
- Renewal is described only as 'you can easily reapply'; the interaction of repeated stamps with the 183-day residence test is not stated.
- Employee work-permit and SERP terms not verified on an official page.
- 1-bed rent rests on 6 Numbeo contributors; 2-bed on 8 listings from one agency.
