# Albania — earned income, nomad and expat dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026

## Summary
Top marginal rate 23% on employment income above ALL 2,040,000. Employee contributions 11.2%; self-employed pay about ALL 14,900 a month at minimum bases (about 3.4% of EUR 54k). Unique Permit for digital nomads: yes (reported), income threshold unclear, tax treatment disputed. No expat regime. Tirana rent EUR 650 (1-bed), EUR 850 (2-bed).

## Income tax and social security
Employment income 13% up to ALL 2,040,000 and 23% above. Employee social insurance 9.5% + health 1.7%. Self-employed: PwC lists 15% (0% for gross income up to ALL 14 million until 2029) and 23% above; minimum social and health contributions about ALL 14,900 a month (ALL 178,800 a year, about 3.4% of EUR 54k at roughly ALL 98 per EUR; the earlier 26.4% added percentages of different bases and was wrong).

## Digital nomad visa
Unique Permit: one year, renewable, remote work for clients outside Albania; the statutory income level is unspecified. Tax: disputed, treated as standard.

## Expat regimes
None found.

## Living costs
Tirana: 1-bed EUR 650, 2-bed EUR 850.

## Residence routes
Short visa-free stays, then Unique Permit.

## Open questions
- Whether a 0% band exists at low monthly pay was not confirmed (PwC shows 13% from the first lek of the 2025 annual schedule); at EUR 54k a zero band of ~ALL 480,000 would change tax by about 1% of income.
- Maximum insurable salary not found.
- The self-employed 0%/15% wording on PwC is ambiguous; self-employed contributions are minimum-base figures only.
- No official Albanian page opened; Fragomen is from January 2022 and said decrees were still expected.
- Claimed first-year tax exemption unverified; treated as standard.
- EU citizens' need for the permit not addressed.
- Because the treatment is 'standard', covers_local_work is left null (not applicable).
- Official requirements, fees and tax treatment of the nomad permit unverified.
