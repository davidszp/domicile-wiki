# United Arab Emirates - earned income, nomad and expat dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026

## Summary
No federal or emirate-level personal income tax on employment or self-employment income (PwC, reviewed 9 Sep 2026). Non-GCC expatriates pay no UAE social security; only a flat unemployment-insurance fee (AED 5 or 10 a month) applies. A person running a business pays 9% UAE Corporate Tax only if business turnover exceeds AED 1 million.

## Income tax and social security
Marginal rates on taxable income (AED):
- from 0 AED: 0%

- Employee social security: 0%, income cap none stated.
- Self-employed social security: 0%, income cap none stated. No self-employment social security for non-GCC nationals. Self-employment through a licensed business can come under 9% Corporate Tax above AED 1,000,000 turnover; wages and personal investment income do not count toward that turnover.
- Notes: Dubai has no income-based local tax. Unemployment insurance: AED 5 per month (basic salary up to AED 16,000) or AED 10 per month (above), from 1 Jan 2023.
- Confidence: high (verified).

## Digital nomad visa
### Dubai Virtual Working Programme (remote work visa) (digital nomad visa)
One-year Dubai residence visa for remote workers employed by or owning a company outside the UAE. Holders live in the UAE with 0% personal income tax because the UAE levies none. Officially reported minimum is USD 5,000 a month; older and third-party sources still quote USD 3,500. Covers local work: the UAE levies no personal income tax at all, so pay for work done while living in the UAE is untaxed (PwC).

- Treatment: exempt; minimum income: 60,000 USD a year; duration: open-ended years (Valid for one year, renewable on re-application; no cap on renewals found.); foreign income required: True; local employment required: False; local social security applies: False; covers work done locally: true.
- Confidence: low (partially_verified).

## Expat regimes
No expat or impatriate regime found; the standard resident rules apply.

## Living costs

- 2-bedroom: 10,000 AED a month. Citywide average 2-bedroom monthly rent in Dubai, 2026 estimate (range AED 5,500-20,000; triangulated from DLD index, Cavendish Maxwell, Property Finder; sample size not disclosed).

## Residence routes
EU, US and other citizens: no visa-free right to work. A remote worker employed by a non-UAE company, or owning one, can apply for the Dubai Virtual Working Programme (one-year residence visa, reported minimum USD 5,000 a month). A skilled employee normally enters on a UAE work permit and residence visa sponsored by a local employer; the Golden Visa is a longer-term route (see data/jurisdictions/ARE.json). Citizenship does not change the route.
