# North Macedonia — earned income, nomad and expat dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026

## Summary
Flat 10% income tax; employee contributions 28.0%. No nomad visa or expat regime found. Skopje 1-bed rent about EUR 374; 2-bed not found.

## Income tax and social security
Flat 10% on employment, self-employment and most other income. Employee contributions: pension 18.8%, health 7.5%, employment 1.2%, additional health 0.5%. Self-employed: no separate rate found; the same 28% set is used as a low-confidence estimate.

## Digital nomad visa
None found (several guides say there is no dedicated route).

## Expat regimes
None found.

## Living costs
Skopje 1-bed EUR 374 (LifeIndexed); 3-bed EUR 695.

## Residence routes
Long-stay visa then temporary residence on a business or employment ground.

## Open questions
- Self-employed contribution rate and base not found on any page opened; 28% (the same statutory set of rates) is a best estimate.
- Personal allowance and average salary (MKD) not found.
- PwC dates the 10% flat rate from 2023; no 2026 change confirmed.
- Nomad visa absence rests on secondary sources (search results), not an official page.
- 2-bedroom rent missing.
