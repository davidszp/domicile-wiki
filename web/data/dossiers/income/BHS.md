# The Bahamas — earned income, nomad and expat dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026

## Summary

The Bahamas has no personal income tax. Employees pay 4.65% National Insurance and the self-employed 10.3%, on earnings up to about BSD 43,160 a year. BEATS gives remote workers a 12-month permit (USD 1,025) renewable to three years with no published income floor. Rent not verified.

## Income tax and social security

No personal income tax (PwC, reviewed July 2026; Deloitte 2026). NIB (PwC, 21 July 2026): employee 4.65%, self-employed 10.3%, on maximum earnings of BSD 830 a week (Deloitte says BSD 810 a week, adjusted July 2024). Employers also pay 6.65%. A business licence fee based on turnover can apply to local businesses.

## Digital nomad visa

BEATS: official FAQ lists job letter or proof of self-employment, passport and medical insurance; no income threshold is published. USD 25 application fee plus USD 1,000 permit fee for professionals; 12-month permit, annual renewals considered up to three years. Official pages say nothing about tax; with no income tax the permit is tax-neutral.

## Expat regimes

None. There is no income tax to relieve.

## Living costs

No usable rent figures: Coldwell Banker Bahamas lists only luxury homes, Bahamas Realty blocks automated access, the Bahamas Real Estate Association site shows vacation rentals only, and Numbeo (HTTP 429) and Expatistan (HTTP 403) could not be read.

## Residence routes

Remote workers of any citizenship (EU, US, other) can use BEATS: a 12-month permit renewable annually for up to three years, USD 1,025 for an individual. Employees of a Bahamian employer need a work permit (not researched). Permanent residence is by economic investment of BSD 1 million or more (Department of Immigration, per repo research in data/jurisdictions/BHS.json); that route is not tied to income. US citizens remain taxable in the US on worldwide income; the Bahamas has no tax to credit.

## Open questions

- NIB wage ceiling: PwC (reviewed 21 July 2026) gives BSD 830 a week and the 10.3% self-employed rate; Deloitte's January 2026 highlights give BSD 3,510 a month / BSD 810 a week, 'last adjusted July 2024'. PwC's newer figure is used; the NIB site is a script-only page and could not be read.
- No minimum income is published on the official FAQ (job letter or proof of self-employment only); some third-party guides quote a threshold, not verified.
- The official pages do not address tax or National Insurance for BEATS holders.
- The 'professionals' page is the official FAQ; the 3-year maximum is the FAQ's wording ('considered for a maximum of three years').
- No rent data; Nassau long-term 1-bed/2-bed rents need a listing source that can be opened (several agent sites block automated access).
- Employee work-permit terms not researched.
