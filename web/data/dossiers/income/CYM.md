# Cayman Islands — earned income, nomad and expat dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026

## Summary

The Cayman Islands levies no income tax. The only mandatory contribution is a pension: 5% for employees and 10% for the self-employed, up to CI$87,000 of earnings. The Global Citizen Concierge Program (up to 24 months) is closed: last applications 31 October 2022, regulations expired 30 November 2024; it is kept in the data as kind 'other' with standard treatment and is not an open route. Rents (CIREBA asking): 1-bed median CI$2,000 (7 units), 2-bed median CI$3,500 (6 units).

## Income tax and social security

PwC (reviewed 29 May 2026): no income or withholding taxes on individuals. Deloitte: no social security or payroll taxes. The National Pensions Act (2024 Revision) still requires employees to contribute 5% of earnings (employer matches) and the self-employed 10%, up to the maximum pensionable earnings of CI$87,000 (default in the Act, changeable by Cabinet Order). Health insurance is mandatory for employees; the employee share was not verified.

## Digital nomad visa

None open. The Global Citizen Concierge Program offered remote workers up to 24 months; the 2021 amendment set 31 October 2022 as the last application date and 30 November 2024 as the expiry of the regulations (Maples), and the Tourism Minister said it was 'officially no longer available as of October 2022' (Caribbean Journal, 2023). No relaunch was found. The data keeps it listed as kind 'other' (standard treatment) so it is not scored as an open route. The income threshold (USD 100,000) is from search summaries only. No tax effect because the islands have no income tax.

## Expat regimes

None needed; no income tax.

## Living costs

CIREBA Rentals (34 priced listings read across 3 pages, asking rents in CI$ a month, lease term and furnishing not stated): 1-bed median CI$2,000 (7 units, range 1,700 to 2,500; two shared rooms excluded); 2-bed median CI$3,500 (6 units: South Sound 2,300, Savannah 2,800 and 3,800, West Bay 3,200, George Town 3,650, Spotts 3,800). Most listings are 3+ bedroom houses; Seven Mile Beach apartments are thinly represented, so treat as indicative.

## Residence routes

No nomad route is currently open (GCCP closed in 2022). Employees need a work permit sponsored by a Cayman employer (not researched). Non-working residents can use the 25-year Residency Certificate for persons of independent means (CI$1,000,000 investment incl. CI$500,000 in developed real estate, and income of at least CI$120,000 a year without local employment; no right to work) or the Certificate of Permanent Residence (CI$2 million in real estate), per the earlier jurisdiction research in data/jurisdictions/CYM.json. EU, US and other citizens use the same routes. US citizens remain taxable in the US on worldwide income; Cayman has no tax to credit.
