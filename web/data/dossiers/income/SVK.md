# Slovakia — earned income, nomad and expat dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026

## Summary

Slovakia has four income tax brackets for 2026: 19% to EUR 43,983.32, 25% to 60,349.21, 30% to 75,010.32 and 35% above, with a EUR 5,966.73 non-taxable amount that phases out. Employees pay 9.4% social plus 5% health; the self-employed pay 33.15% social plus 16% health on an assessment base. Self-employed income up to EUR 100,000 is taxed at 15%, with an option of 60% flat expenses (max EUR 20,000). No nomad visa or expat regime was found.

## Income tax and social security

Marginal rates on taxable income (EUR):

- from 0.00: 19.00%
- from 43,983.32: 25.00%
- from 60,349.21: 30.00%
- from 75,010.32: 35.00%

Personal allowance deducted before the brackets: 5,966.73 EUR.

Employee social security and health: 14.40% (cap: 201,168.00). Self-employed: 49.15% (cap: 201,168.00).

2026 scale: 19% on the tax base up to EUR 43,983.32 (154.8x the subsistence minimum), 25% to EUR 60,349.21, 30% to EUR 75,010.32, 35% above. Non-taxable amount EUR 5,966.73 a year, full up to a tax base of EUR 26,367.26 and zero from EUR 50,234.18. Employee contributions: 9.4% social (maximum monthly assessment base EUR 16,764, i.e. EUR 201,168 a year) and 5% health (no cap); the recorded rate is the combined 14.4% and the cap applies to the social part only.

Self-employed notes: Self-employed contributions 2026: social 33.15% (sickness 4.40, old-age 18, disability 6, solidarity reserve 4.75) on a base of about one twelfth of the business tax base times 1.486, minimum EUR 914.40 a month (minimum payment EUR 303.11), maximum EUR 16,764 a month; health 16% on a base of at least EUR 762 (minimum EUR 121.92 a month), no maximum. The recorded 49.15% is the statutory sum on the assessment base, not an effective rate on gross income. Business income: a 15% rate applies to self-employed income up to EUR 100,000 (Forvis Mazars); option of 60% lump-sum expenses up to EUR 20,000 a year for non-VAT-payers (PwC).

Local tax: No local income tax in the sources opened.

## Digital nomad visa

No Slovak digital nomad visa was found.

## Expat regimes

No expat or impatriate tax regime was found in the sources opened.

Regimes recorded in the JSON file:

- **Flat-rate expenses for self-employed (60%, max EUR 20,000)** (other, treatment: partial_exemption): Self-employed people who are not Slovak VAT payers and do not have only rental income may deduct 60% of income as lump-sum expenses up to EUR 20,000 a year instead of actual costs. Minimum income: none stated. Duration (years): None. Social security still applies: True.
- **15% rate on self-employed income up to EUR 100,000** (other, treatment: flat_rate): A 15% income tax rate applies to natural persons earning entrepreneurial income, per the Forvis Mazars 2026 CEE guide, with an annual cap of EUR 100,000. Minimum income: none stated. Duration (years): None. Social security still applies: True.

## Living costs

Bratislava average rents (Bencont 1Q 2026 via Investropa): 2-room (1 bedroom) EUR 908, 3-room (2 bedrooms) EUR 1,199.

## Residence routes

EU citizens can live and work freely. Non-EU nationals need a temporary residence permit for employment (with a job offer) or for business; no remote-worker visa was found and no official page was opened, so the details are unverified.
