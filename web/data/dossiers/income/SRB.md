# Serbia — earned income, nomad and expat dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026

## Summary
Flat 10% income tax; employee contributions 19.9%; self-employed entrepreneurs pay 35.05% (paušal option up to RSD 6 million turnover). Nomad visa: no official dedicated visa confirmed (some sources report a USD 42,000 permit). No expat regime. Belgrade rent EUR 620 (1-bed), EUR 850 (2-bed).

## Income tax and social security
Flat 10% on employment income after a monthly non-taxable amount (about RSD 25,000, not applied). Employee contributions: pension 14%, health 5.15%, unemployment 0.75%. Contribution base capped at five times the average monthly salary. Self-employed entrepreneurs: pension 24% + health 10.3% + unemployment 0.75% = 35.05% of their base (Law on Contributions, Art. 44).

## Digital nomad visa
Disputed. WFA reports a temporary residence permit for remote workers with USD 42,000 a year, valid one year; CountryTaxCalc says there is no official digital nomad visa. Treated as unverified; the Law on Foreigners (Art. 40(4)) names "digital nomads" as a category the Government may define by a separate act, but that act was not found.

## Expat regimes
None found.

## Living costs
Belgrade asking rents: 1-bed EUR 620, 2-bed EUR 850.

## Residence routes
Visa-free 90 days, then temporary residence permit.

## Open questions
- 2026 non-taxable amount in RSD (law text shows 37,369, CountryTaxCalc ~25,000), supplementary-tax thresholds in RSD, and the contribution cap in RSD not found.
- Self-employed 35.05% is the statutory rate; minimum/maximum bases in RSD not found, so the effective rate at low or very high incomes may differ.
- Employee total is 19.9% on PwC (with unemployment); CountryTaxCalc says 19.15%.
- The Law on Foreigners (Art. 40(4)) names digital nomads as a category the Government may define, but the implementing act was not found; income threshold USD 42,000 rests on WFA only.
- Social-security treatment of a foreign-paid worker is stated only by WFA.
- Nomad route: legal basis exists (Law on Foreigners Art. 40(4)) but operating criteria, fees and income test unverified on an official page.
