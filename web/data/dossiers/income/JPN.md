# Japan — earned income, nomad and expat dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026

## Summary

National income tax runs 5% to 45% (seven brackets) plus a 2.1% surtax on the tax and a flat 10% inhabitant tax, giving a combined top marginal rate of about 55.9%. Employees pay about 14.7% in social insurance (health 4.925%, child support 0.115%, welfare pension 9.15%, unemployment 0.5%), each with its own ceiling; the self-employed use National Pension (flat JPY 17,920 a month) and National Health Insurance. The basic deduction is JPY 620,000 for total income of JPY 6.55m to 23.5m (JPY 1.04m below JPY 4.89m, JPY 670,000 up to JPY 6.55m).

Per-capita inhabitant tax JPY 5,000 and forest tax JPY 1,000 not included. personal_allowance is the national basic deduction of JPY 620,000 that applies to total income between JPY 6.55m and 23.5m (PwC deductions page, 2026 and 2027); it is higher (JPY 670,000 to JPY 1.04m) below JPY 6.55m, and the inhabitant-tax basic deduction is JPY 430,000. The employment income deduction (JPY 740,000 minimum, up to JPY 1.95m above JPY 8.5m of salary; employees only) and the blue-return and social-insurance deductions are not included. Employee social insurance: health 4.925% to JPY 1.39m/month, welfare pension 9.15% to JPY 650,000/month; the 14.69% total is therefore not applied uniformly above those ceilings and no single cap is recorded. Long-term care 0.81% from age 40 is extra.

## Digital nomad visa

### Digital nomad visa (Designated Activities), from 2024

Six-month stay for remote workers earning at least JPY 10m a year from foreign sources, with private insurance covering at least JPY 10m of medical treatment. Limited to nationals of countries and regions with both a visa-exemption arrangement and a tax convention with Japan; no residence card is issued and the status cannot be extended.

- Treatment: standard
- Minimum income: 10,000,000 JPY a year
- Duration: 0.5 years. Six months; extension not allowed, new application only after six months outside Japan.
- Foreign income required: True; local employment required: False; social security applies: False
- Confidence: medium (partially_verified)

## Expat regimes

### Non-permanent resident taxpayer status

A resident foreign national with an aggregate stay of five years or less in the preceding ten years is a non-permanent resident (PwC residence page). Per the Japan jurisdiction research in this repository, foreign-source income is taxed only to the extent paid in or remitted to Japan (not confirmed on an opened page). It does not cover a remote worker's pay for work done in Japan: PwC states that Japan-source employment income is remuneration earned for services rendered in Japan, regardless of where or when it is paid.

- Treatment: standard
- Minimum income: none recorded
- Duration: 5 years. Status lasts while aggregate stay is five years or less in the preceding ten.
- Foreign income required: True; local employment required: False; social security applies: True
- Covers pay for work done in the country: False
- Confidence: low (unverified)

## Residence routes

EU, US and other citizens: the digital nomad status is only for nationals of countries with both visa exemption and a tax convention (the US qualifies per the source); stay is six months, non-extendable. Skilled employees need a work visa sponsored by an employer; the specific categories (Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services, Highly Skilled Professional points) were not verified from opened official pages.
