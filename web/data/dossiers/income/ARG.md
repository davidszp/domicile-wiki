# Argentina — earned income, nomad and expat dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026

## Summary

Argentine residents pay Ganancias on a progressive scale of 5% to 35% (article 94 scale, values for July-December 2026, indexed twice a year by INDEC CPI). Employees and autonomos get a non-taxable minimum and a special deduction. Employees contribute 17% of salary up to a monthly cap; autonomos pay fixed monthly amounts by category.

- Self-employed notes: Autonomos (general regime) pay a fixed monthly amount by category, not a share of actual income: from 15 January 2026 category I is ARS 62,743.08 (reference income ARS 196,072.13) up to category V ARS 276,065.92 (reference income ARS 862,706.01), i.e. 32% of the reference income in every category (calcularsueldo.com.ar, 19 Jan 2026 page). Category V is the highest, so the contribution stops growing with income: the cap field is category V's reference income x 12 (ARS 10,352,472) and rate 0.32 applies up to it (about ARS 3.31 million a year; on a EUR ~54k income this is a small share of income). Later 2026 category amounts (a prior pass noted ARS 318,759 for category V from mid-June, not re-sourced) are higher, so this slightly understates. Monotributo (simplified) alternative exists but has revenue ceilings and was not researched.
- Local tax note: No city-level personal income tax in Buenos Aires; provincial turnover tax (Ingresos Brutos) applies to professionals but is not modelled. Scale and allowances are the July-December 2026 values (the latest ARCA update, 8.42% above the January-June values, taken as the year-end annual scale).

## Digital nomad visa

**Digital nomad transitory residence (Disposicion 758/2022)**. Transitory residence of up to 180 days for foreigners from visa-exempt countries who work remotely for clients or employers abroad; extendable once for the same length. No minimum income is set at application; extension requires income receipts and at least 50% of the period spent in Argentina. It carries no tax benefit; non-working residents become tax resident from the 13th month.

- Minimum income: not published.
- Duration: 1 years. 180 days plus one extension of the same length (about 12 months).
- Tax treatment: standard; social security applies: False.

## Residence routes

EU and US citizens can enter without a visa as tourists; remote workers can use the digital nomad transitory residence (180 days, extendable once). Temporary or permanent residence for a skilled employee needs a local employer; Mercosur nationals have a simplified residence route. Citizenship and other routes were not researched.

## Open questions

- Monotributo not researched.
- The scale is from a secondary calculator site citing ARCA; the definitive annual values for 2026 are not published yet.
- No expat tax regime found.
- Scale and deductions are the July-December 2026 values from calcularsueldo.com.ar citing ARCA (scale page and personal-deductions page, opened 2026-10-15); consistent with a uniform 8.423% uplift of the January-June values, but the ARCA resolution itself and the definitive annual-return values for 2026 were not read. The calcularsueldo scale page carries a stale footnote dated 2024.
- personal_allowance = non-taxable minimum ARS 5,585,736.93 + general special deduction ARS 18,031,308.76 x 1.08423 = ARS 19,550,079.27 (July-December 2026); the special deduction applies to employees and certain autonomos only.
- Employee cap converted from the PwC monthly cap ARS 4,414,652.38 (1 June 2026) x 12; the cap is indexed monthly and bonus months add 50%.
- Self-employed 0.32 is the ratio of contribution to reference income in the category table (consistent across categories), not a rate stated by a source; the legal split (pension, PAMI, health) was not read.
- PwC Argentina pages do not cover autonomos or monotributo.
- Source is a May 2022 article; current requirements and fees on the Migraciones site were not reached.
- A longer stay needs another residence category.
- Treatment is standard (visa carries no tax benefit), so covers_local_work does not apply; Argentine residents are taxed on worldwide income (PwC).
