# Dominica — family-office tax dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026
Reviewer: research agent (Claude), unreviewed by a tax adviser

## Summary

Dominica taxes resident individuals on worldwide income at up to 35% (above XCD 80,000 including the XCD 30,000 allowance), so foreign dividends and interest are taxed, with a unilateral foreign tax credit. There is no capital gains, inheritance, estate or wealth tax. Domestic dividends carry a dividend tax credit that largely removes the tax. Residents who are not ordinarily resident are taxed on foreign income only when received in Dominica. Citizenship by investment from USD 200,000.

## Residence and eligibility

Resident: present 183 days or more in the year (Deloitte; IRD); the Act also treats as resident a person present for a continuous period spanning adjacent years. 'Ordinarily resident' individuals are taxed on worldwide income; residents not ordinarily resident only on foreign income received in Dominica. CBI: EDF USD 200,000 single / USD 250,000 family of 4; real estate USD 200,000 + USD 100,000 government fee for a family of 4.

## Capital gains

No CGT (Deloitte 2026; CBIU). JSON 0. Stamp duty on transfers of immovable property: 2.5% paid by the seller and 2% by the buyer (also on sales of shares in companies with more than 50% real property). Additional fees may apply: assurance fund 1%, judicial fee 1%, solicitor's fee up to 2.5% plus 15% VAT. Non-citizens need an alien landholding licence (cost not verified).

## Dividends

Income Tax Act Chap. 67:01: s.8(1) residents taxed on income from all sources in or out of Dominica; s.8(2) residents not ordinarily resident only on foreign income received in Dominica; s.36 dividends included; s.63(2) credit on domestic dividends (whole tax if marginal rate ≤30%, else 35% of net dividend), so JSON domestic 0; ss.64-65 unilateral credit for foreign tax. Rates (IRD, from 2018): XCD 30,000 allowance, then 15%/25%/35%. JSON foreign 0.35, credit.

## Inheritance and gifts to children

No inheritance, estate or gift tax (Deloitte 2026; CBIU). Parent-to-child property transfer: parent pays 2% stamp duty; assurance and judicial fees may apply. JSON basis none, fully_exempt.

## Wealth tax

None. No national property tax; municipal tax.

## Special regimes

None modelled. Residents who are not ordinarily resident are taxed on foreign income only when received in Dominica (s.8(2)); noted in dividends.

## Company taxation (kept separate from personal tax)

Corporate income tax 25%; branch remittance tax 15%; no CGT; unilateral foreign tax credit; no Pillar Two commitment. 15% WHT on dividends, interest and royalties to non-residents.
