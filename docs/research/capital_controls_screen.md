# Capital controls on personal wealth: screen of 133 countries and territories

Date: 2026-10-15. Criterion: a country FAILS if (a) the IMF (Article IV staff report informational annex, or AREAER) reports exchange controls on most capital transactions or controls on residents' capital outflows, AND (b) a FOREIGN national resident cannot freely transfer personal savings, investment proceeds or property-sale proceeds abroad (annual quota, central-bank approval or case-by-case authorisation). Mainland China already fails (IMF CR 26/44, see china_hong_kong_foreign_wealth.md); Hong Kong passes (Basic Law art. 112) and is not repeated here.

## Reading limits (read first)

- The AREAER online database and IMF eLibrary pages returned 403/login redirects. The IMF side therefore rests on Country Reports (PDFs) opened one by one. Many reports are silent on capital controls (they list only current-account restrictions), so (a) is often "not stated" rather than "denied".
- Foreign-resident rules (b) are mostly from central-bank texts or law-firm material. Where only a commercial or consultancy source was opened, it is flagged. Several rule texts are older than 2026; check the central bank before acting.
- Sources were opened by me or by three research sub-agents in this session; claims that came only from a search snippet are marked "snippet only". Verdict strength: firm = primary text opened for both parts; moderate = one part rests on a secondary or older source; low = thin.
- Not IMF members (no IMF test possible): TWN, BMU (from general knowledge, not an opened page).
