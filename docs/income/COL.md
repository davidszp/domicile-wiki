# Colombia — earned income, nomad and expat dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026

## Summary

Colombian fiscal residents (183 days in any 365) are taxed on worldwide income at progressive rates from 0% to 39% (thresholds in UVT, UVT = COP 52,347 for 2026). Employees pay 4% health and 4% pension (plus solidarity surcharges); independents contribute 12.5% health and 16% pension on a base of 40% of income, capped at 25 minimum wages.

The cap is expressed on gross income (IBC cap / 0.4).

## Digital nomad visa

**Visa V - Digital nomad**. Colombian digital nomad visa for remote workers paid from abroad. Requires regular foreign-source income of at least three monthly minimum wages for the previous three months, health insurance, and no work for Colombian employers or clients. It does not change tax: after more than 183 days in a 365-day period the holder is a fiscal resident taxed on worldwide income.

- Minimum income: 63,032,580 COP a year.
- Duration: 2 years. Valid up to 24 months from issue per the cited guide; renewal not addressed by the source.
- Tax treatment: standard; social security applies: True.

## Residence routes

EU, US and other visa-exempt nationalities can stay as tourists (up to 180 days a year) without working locally. Remote workers can apply for the Visa V digital nomad visa (foreign income of at least three minimum wages, health insurance), which does not lead to permanent residence by itself. Skilled employees of a Colombian employer need a work visa (Visa M) sponsored by the employer; other visa routes (partner, investor, Mercosur-style agreements for some nationalities) exist but were not researched.
