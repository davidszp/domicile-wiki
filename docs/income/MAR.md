# Morocco - earned income, nomad and expat dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026

## Summary
Resident individuals pay income tax (IR) on worldwide income on a six-band scale from 0% to 37% (0% to MAD 40,000; 37% above MAD 180,000). Employee social security is 4.48% on pay up to MAD 6,000 a month plus 2.26% mandatory health (AMO) with no ceiling. Independents in the single professional contribution (CPU) regime pay 1.5% of turnover.

## Income tax and social security
Marginal rates on taxable income (MAD):
- from 0 MAD: 0%
- from 40,000 MAD: 10%
- from 60,000 MAD: 20%
- from 80,000 MAD: 30%
- from 100,000 MAD: 34%
- from 180,000 MAD: 37%
- Personal allowance: 35,000 MAD.

- Employee social security: 2.26%, income cap none stated.
- Self-employed social security: 1.5%, income cap none stated. Self-employed in the CPU regime pay a flat 1.5% of turnover covering AMO, pension and family allowances (Wafir, secondary). Independents outside CPU fall under the TNS regime; rates not found.
- Notes: No local income surtax found. Personal allowance of MAD 35,000 is the salaried professional-expense deduction: capped at MAD 35,000 (PwC: 25% rate above MAD 78,000 gross), which is reached at any gross salary above MAD 140,000; it is for employees, not for independents, who deduct actual expenses. Family-charge and mandatory-contribution deductions are not applied. Employee rate shown is AMO 2.26% (uncapped), the marginal rate above the ceiling; an additional 4.48% (CNSS short-term 0.33%, retirement 3.96%, IPE 0.19%) applies to pay up to MAD 6,000 a month (MAD 72,000 a year), about MAD 3,226 a year at most (Wafir).
- Confidence: medium (partially_verified).

## Digital nomad visa
No digital-nomad visa was found in the sources opened.

## Expat regimes
No expat or impatriate regime found; the standard resident rules apply.

## Living costs
City: Casablanca.

- 1-bedroom: 6,800 MAD a month. Casablanca 1-bedroom average monthly rent, 2026 (range MAD 5,000-11,000; Mubawab-based estimate using '2 pieces' listings as proxy, asking rents discounted for negotiation). Numbeo city-centre 1-bedroom is MAD 5,892.
- 2-bedroom: 9,200 MAD a month. Casablanca 2-bedroom average monthly rent, 2026 (range MAD 6,500-15,500; Mubawab-based estimate, luxury listings removed, sample size not disclosed).

## Residence routes
EU, US and other citizens: non-Moroccans need a residence card (carte de sejour) to settle; skilled employees generally need a work contract approved by the labour authorities and a Moroccan employer. No Moroccan digital-nomad visa was found. Casablanca Finance City offers a company regime but no personal tax relief (existing jurisdiction file).

## Open questions
- Family-charge deductions and deductibility of mandatory CNSS/AMO contributions are not applied (persona is single).
- TNS (non-CPU) self-employed contribution rates not found; CPU 1.5% of turnover comes from Wafir only.
- The 4.48% capped contribution is not representable in the single-rate field.
- PwC page reviewed 30 April 2026 does not state the scale's effective year.
- No digital-nomad visa found; not confirmed from an official source.
- Work contract approval (ANAPEC) and residence card process not researched.
- Deductibility of the AMO health contribution (the recorded 2.26%) and CNSS contributions could not be established: the opened PwC deductions page addresses only pension insurance contributions (capped at 50% of net taxable salary).
