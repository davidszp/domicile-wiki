# Greece — earned income, nomad and expat dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026

## Summary

Greek income tax tops out at 44% above EUR 60,000. Employees pay 13.37% social security up to about EUR 93,100, the self-employed a fixed EUR 250 to 676 a month. The 5C regime exempts 50% of income for seven years but needs a Greek employer and prior non-residence. The nomad visa needs EUR 3,500 a month. Athens median asking rents: EUR 650 for one bedroom, EUR 880 for two (centre).

## Income tax and social security

Marginal rates on taxable income (EUR):

- from 0.00: 9.00%
- from 10,000.00: 20.00%
- from 20,000.00: 26.00%
- from 30,000.00: 34.00%
- from 40,000.00: 39.00%
- from 60,000.00: 44.00%

Personal allowance deducted before the brackets: 0.00 EUR.

Employee social security: 13.37% (cap: 93,143.28). Self-employed: 16.00% (cap: 50,690.00).

Resident income tax on employment, business and pension income: 9% to EUR 10,000, 20% to 20,000, 26% to 30,000, 34% to 40,000, 39% to 60,000 and 44% above (scale as listed by PwC after Law 5246/2025, in force from 1 January 2026). Employees pay 13.37% EFKA contributions up to a monthly ceiling of EUR 7,761.94; the self-employed pay fixed monthly amounts by insurance category.

Self-employed notes: Fixed EFKA amounts from 1 January 2026 per insurance category (main pension + health in kind + health in cash, EUR a month; ministerial decision D.15/D'/1865/2026 as shown on TaxHeaven, where e.g. 6th category = 597.06 + 7.16 + 71.65): 1st 250.77, 2nd 300.93, 3rd 360.63, 4th 433.47, 5th 519.45, 6th 675.87. The 6th (top) category is EUR 8,110.44 a year, i.e. about 15.0% of EUR 54,000; the 1st category is EUR 3,009 a year, about 5.6% of EUR 54,000. The recorded 16% up to EUR 50,690 is a derived equivalent of the TOP category (it reproduces EUR 8,110.44 at any income above EUR 50,690), a conservative assumption: the TaxHeaven page does not say how a category is assigned (by choice or by income), and supplementary-pension and lump-sum contributions are extra. Reduced tax scales apply to those under 30 and with children (not modelled). The employee tax credit was not on the pages opened and is not modelled.

Local tax: No municipal income tax. The 2026 scale is the one on the PwC page; the page does not label the year, but the significant-developments page says Law 5246/2025 changes apply from 1 January 2026.

## Digital nomad visa

Non-EU remote workers with EUR 3,500 a month of non-Greek income after taxes (about EUR 66,000 gross, our arithmetic) get a visa for up to 12 months, then a two-year permit. No special tax rate; residents (183 days) are taxed on worldwide income.

## Expat regimes

Article 5C: 50% income tax exemption for seven years on income from a new Greek job or business, for people not resident in Greece for 5 of the last 6 years and relocating from an eligible state. A lump-sum regime for wealthy new residents (5A) exists but is not an earned-income regime.

Regimes recorded in the JSON file:

- **Digital nomad visa** (digital_nomad_visa, treatment: standard): National visa and residence permit for non-EU remote workers employed by or serving clients outside Greece, who may not work for a Greek company. Official portal (workfromgreece.gr): salary of at least EUR 3,500 a month AFTER TAXES (plus 20% for a spouse or cohabitant and 15% per child); visa up to one year, then a residence permit of up to two years renewable for two more. min_income is a gross equivalent: EUR 42,000 net a year needs about EUR 66,000 gross for a self-employed person in the top EFKA category (about EUR 61,000 in the lowest) and about EUR 66,800 for an employee, using the PwC 2026 scale and 13.37% contributions (our arithmetic, no tax credits). The visa does not set a tax rate; someone present over 183 days becomes a Greek tax resident taxed on worldwide income at the normal scale. The 5C regime is a separate matter and is not automatic. Minimum income: 66,000 EUR. Maximum income: none. Duration (years): 4. Visa up to 1 year to enter; the digital nomad residence permit is valid up to 2 years and renewable for 2 more years (workfromgreece.gr), so 4 years of permit. Foreign income required: True. Local employment required: False. Covers work done locally: None. Social security still applies: True.
- **Article 5C: relocated employees and business owners (50% exemption)** (expat_regime, treatment: partial_exemption 0.5): 50% of Greek-source employment or business income is exempt from income tax for up to seven tax years, with no extension. Requires non-residence in Greece for 5 of the previous 6 years, a transfer of tax residence from an EU/EEA state or a state with a tax administrative cooperation agreement, a new job with a Greek legal entity or a foreign company's Greek permanent establishment (or a new business), and an intention to stay at least two years. Apply by the end of the year of starting work (before 2 July) or the following year. Minimum income: none stated. Maximum income: none. Duration (years): 7. Seven tax years, no extension. Foreign income required: False. Local employment required: True. Covers work done locally: None. Social security still applies: True.

## Living costs

Athens (centre): 1-bedroom EUR 650 a month, 2-bedroom EUR 880 (Global Property Guide Q2 2026, median asking rents).

## Residence routes

EU citizens register their right of residence. US and other non-EU remote workers can use the digital nomad visa (EUR 3,500 a month after taxes, up to 12 months then a two-year permit). Skilled employees from outside the EU need an employer-sponsored work permit or a Blue Card, which were not researched. The start-up investor residence permit (EUR 250,000 into a registered start-up, from 2025) exists but is out of scope.

## Open questions

- Skilled-employee work permit routes not researched.
- Rents rest on one aggregator (Global Property Guide); no second source obtained.
- Employee tax credit and the 2026 interaction with the new scale were not verified.
- The 13.37% employee rate is PwC's 1 January 2025 figure; 2026 rate not separately confirmed (ceiling EUR 7,761.94 a month is stated for 1 January 2026).
- Self-employed: how a freelancer is placed in an EFKA category (choice vs income) was not found; the model uses the top category (about 15% of EUR 54k, derived) while the lowest category would be about 5.6%. Supplementary pension and lump-sum contributions not included.
- Self-employed rate is a derivation from fixed amounts, not a statutory percentage.
- Digital nomad visa: Measured AFTER taxes per the official portal; the gross equivalent (EUR 66,000) is our derivation and a consulate may apply a gross test in practice.
- Digital nomad visa: The Ministry of Migration page (migration.gov.gr) returned 404; the official portal workfromgreece.gr was used instead.
- Digital nomad visa: Whether the permit renewals stop after 4 years was not confirmed (the portal says 'renewable every 2 years for 2 more years').
- Article 5C: relocated employees and business owners (50% exemption): The condition that the person moves from an EU/EEA or cooperation-agreement state may exclude some non-EU citizens depending on the country of previous residence.
- Article 5C: relocated employees and business owners (50% exemption): Remote work for a foreign employer does not meet the Greek-employer test.
- Article 5C: relocated employees and business owners (50% exemption): For self-employed relocators PwC says a similar application is 'envisaged' for individual business activity but lists no conditions, so the self-employment application is unverified; requires_local_employment is set for the employee route.
