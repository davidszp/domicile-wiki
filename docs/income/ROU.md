# Romania — earned income, nomad and expat dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026

## Summary

Romania has a flat 10% income tax with no local income tax. Employees pay 25% pension (CAS) plus 10% health (CASS); the self-employed pay 25% CAS on a chosen base (minimum 12 or 24 minimum gross salaries, RON 48,600 or 97,200) and 10% CASS (capped at 72 minimum salaries). A nomad visa exists (income of three times the average gross monthly wage, company registered abroad for at least three years per one source) but the 2026 amount, duration and tax exemption could not be verified. Bucharest asking rents are about EUR 600 for a two-room and EUR 899 for a three-room flat.

## Income tax and social security

Marginal rates on taxable income (RON):

- from 0.00: 10.00%

Personal allowance deducted before the brackets: 0.00 RON.

Employee social security and health: 35.00% (cap: none stated). Self-employed: 19.00% (cap: 291,600.00).

Flat 10% income tax on most income (exceptions for dividends, capital gains, property transfers and gambling); no local personal income tax. Employees pay CAS 25% and CASS 10% of gross; no ceiling for employees given by PwC. Minimum gross salary implied for 2026 is RON 4,050 a month (RON 48,600 / 12, computed from PwC).

Self-employed notes: Recorded 19% is a derived approximate rate on an income of about EUR 54,000 (about RON 270,000 at an assumed ~5.0 RON/EUR): CAS 25% on a base of 24 minimum salaries (RON 97,200) = RON 24,300, plus CASS 10% of net income = RON 27,000, total RON 51,300 = 19%. At about EUR 130,000 (RON 650,000) CASS is capped at RON 29,160 (10% of 72 minimum salaries), so the total is RON 53,460, about 8%; the recorded 19% with the RON 291,600 cap gives a close figure. Statutory components: Independent activities: 10% on net income (real system or fixed income quota); CAS 25% on a base of the person's choosing, not below 12 minimum gross salaries (RON 48,600) if annual income is 12-24 minimum salaries, or 24 (RON 97,200) if income is 24 or more; CASS 10% on net annual income, capped at 72 minimum salaries (RON 291,600). The recorded cap is the CASS cap; CAS has no separate ceiling stated. Non-salary income (rent, investments) triggers CASS from 6 minimum salaries (RON 24,300).

Local tax: There are no local taxes on personal income in Romania (PwC).

## Digital nomad visa

Romania has a digital nomad long-stay visa (Law 22/2022 per secondary sources). Required: income of at least three times the average gross monthly salary, a foreign employer or own foreign company, health insurance, accommodation and a clean record. The 2026 threshold, the duration and the exact tax exemption could not be confirmed from official pages; secondary sources disagree.

## Expat regimes

No impatriate regime was found; the IT-sector tax exemption and any nomad-related exemption were not confirmed from an opened source.

Regimes recorded in the JSON file:

- **Digital nomad long-stay visa** (digital_nomad_visa, treatment: standard): Long-stay visa for non-EU remote workers earning at least three times the average gross monthly salary in Romania for the months before applying (and during the stay), employed by or owning a company registered abroad. One source requires the foreign company to be at least three years old, an older news report says one year. Sources conflict on duration and on the tax exemption (one article says exemption from taxes on income earned outside Romania and from social contributions for up to six months within 12 months if the stay does not exceed 183 days). Legal basis cited by one secondary source: Art. 49(1)(f1) of Government Emergency Ordinance 194/2002 on foreigners. The only exemption found on an opened page is PwC's non-taxable item 'salary income obtained from a foreign employer for employment activities rendered abroad', which does not cover work performed in Romania; a secondary source's statement that the holder's foreign income is exempt for up to six months in 12 (stay under 183 days) was not confirmed in a statute, so covers_local_work stays unset. Minimum income: none stated. Duration (years): None. Social security still applies: False.

## Living costs

Bucharest asking rents Q1 2026: two-room EUR 600, three-room EUR 899 (Imobiliare.ro Market 360 via Finorum).

## Residence routes

EU citizens may live and work freely. Non-EU remote workers can apply for the digital nomad long-stay visa; skilled employees need a work permit sponsored by a Romanian employer. No official Romanian page could be opened (mae.ro returned 503), so these routes are unverified.
