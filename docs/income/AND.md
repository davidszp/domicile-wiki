# Andorra — earned income, nomad and expat dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026

## Summary

Andorra taxes residents at 10% above a EUR 24,000 personal minimum, which with the capped EUR 800 bonus gives 0% to EUR 24,000, 5% to EUR 40,000 and 10% above. Employees pay 6.5% CASS; the self-employed 22% of a base capped near EUR 3,675 a month. No nomad visa or expat regime found. Asking rents: EUR 1,250 for one bedroom, EUR 1,750 for two.

## Income tax and social security

Marginal rates on taxable income (EUR):

- from 0.00: 0.00%
- from 24,000.00: 5.00%
- from 40,000.00: 10.00%

Personal allowance deducted before the brackets: 0.00 EUR.

Employee social security: 6.50% (cap: none stated). Self-employed: 22.00% (cap: 44,096.64).

IRPF is a flat 10% on the general base after a EUR 24,000 personal minimum, minus a bonus of 50% of the tax on that base capped at EUR 800, so the effective marginal rates are 0% to EUR 24,000, 5% from EUR 24,000 to 40,000 and 10% above. Employees pay 6.5% CASS (pensions 3.5% + general 3%); the self-employed 22% of a chosen base, ceiling EUR 3,674.72 a month.

Self-employed notes: CASS self-employed: 22% (10% general branch + 12% pensions) of a chosen base set as a share of the previous year's mean monthly salary: 25% (EUR 668.13, EUR 146.99/month, restricted to certain criteria), 50% (EUR 1,568.67), 75% (EUR 2,004.39), 100% (EUR 2,672.52, EUR 587.95/month, the DEFAULT for those who do not qualify for a reduced rate), 125% (EUR 3,340.65) and 137.5% (EUR 3,674.72, EUR 808.44/month, requires higher net income or turnover). The recorded 22% with a cap of 12 x EUR 3,674.72 = EUR 44,096.64 reproduces the TOP tier (EUR 9,701 a year, about 18% of EUR 54k); the default 100% tier would be EUR 7,055 a year, about 13% of EUR 54k. Figures are from the CASS page updated 30 January 2025; 2026 amounts were not found and the CASS site says it is informational only.

Local tax: No municipal income tax. The 0% and 5% bands model the EUR 24,000 personal minimum and the EUR 800 bonus; the guide is for tax year 2025, the 2026 guide was not opened. Residents with more than 183 days or the centre of economic interests in Andorra are taxed on worldwide income.

## Digital nomad visa

Andorra has a digital nomad residence (D.3, Govern d'Andorra): location-independent work cleared by the economy ministry, a quota, at least 90 days a year in Andorra, 2-year initial permit then 2, 3 and 10-year renewals. The means threshold and quota are set by regulation and were not found.

## Expat regimes

No expat or impatriate regime found.

Regimes recorded in the JSON file:

- **Residence for digital nomads (D.3)** (digital_nomad_visa, treatment: standard): Residence authorisation (D.3) for any newcomer, EU or non-EU, whose work requires no fixed geographic location and uses telecommunications and technology (Govern d'Andorra). Conditions: a favourable ruling from the ministry in charge of the economy that the applicant's work is location-independent, admission within the regulatory quota, effective principal residence in Andorra for at least 90 days a calendar year, proof of owned or rented housing meeting minimum standards (or purchase under way), sufficient financial means as set by regulation, and health and disability insurance valid in Andorra. Initial authorisation two years, first renewal two years, second renewal three years, later renewals ten years. The visa itself does not change tax: residents (183 days or centre of economic interests) pay IRPF on worldwide income under the standard schedule. The income threshold and quota are set by regulation and were not found. Minimum income: none stated. Maximum income: none. Duration (years): 7. 2 + 2 + 3 years, then 10-year renewals (Govern d'Andorra); 7 years counted as the first three periods. Foreign income required: True. Local employment required: False. Covers work done locally: None. Social security still applies: True.

## Living costs

Andorra la Vella: 1-bedroom EUR 1,250 a month, 2-bedroom EUR 1,750 (Global Property Guide Q3 2026, median asking rents).

## Residence routes

Andorra is outside the EU; EU, US and other citizens all need a residence authorisation. The Govern d'Andorra lists, among others: residence without work (passive; investment amounts and the financial-authority deposit not verified here), residence and work for employees (quota-based) and for the self-employed (J.1/J.2, including foreign-investment reservations), residence for entrepreneur programme participants (D.4), and a digital nomad residence (D.3: quota, location-independent work cleared by the economy ministry, at least 90 days a year in Andorra, initial two years then 2, 3 and 10-year renewals). Amounts and quotas were not verified.

## Open questions

- No expat tax regime was found; not positively ruled out from an official source.
- Rents rest on one aggregator (Global Property Guide); a relocation consultancy quoted EUR 1,300 to 1,600 for a 2-bedroom in June 2026 (not used); no second source opened.
- PwC's Andorra pages returned 404, so the schedule rests on the Govern d'Andorra e-tramits page and the 2025 IRPF guide; the e-tramits page confirms the 10% general rate, the 5% equivalent between EUR 24,000 and 40,000 via a bonus capped at EUR 800, but not the EUR 24,000 personal minimum (from the guide).
- The employee contribution ceiling (CASS page lists none) and the 2026 self-employed bases were not found.
- Which self-employed tier a EUR 54k earner is assigned to is not established; the top tier is modelled, the default 100% tier would cost about 5 points of income less.
- Deductions available to employees (e.g. CASS contributions) were not extracted; the guide shows them as images.
- Residence for digital nomads (D.3): The minimum means/income amount, the annual quota and the regulation text were not found on the opened pages (the page defers to 'reglamentariament'); min_income is therefore null, which makes the visa score treat it as open to all nomads, probably generous.
- Residence for digital nomads (D.3): Whether social security (CASS) is mandatory for a nomad resident who is self-employed or employed abroad was not checked.
