# Saint Kitts and Nevis — earned income, nomad and expat dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026

## Summary

Saint Kitts and Nevis has no personal income tax. Employees pay 5% social security (cap XCD 78,000 a year) and a Housing and Social Development Levy of 3.5% / 10% / 12% on wages above about XCD 27,040 a year; this is modelled as the brackets, but the levy is a payroll levy and does not apply to self-employed income (Cap. 20.21, s.3). The self-employed pay 10% of their selected income category to social security. A 'digital resident' status for up to 12 months exists in a 2021 Bill but is unconfirmed. Rents (SKN Real Estate asks, some old): 1-bed USD 925, 2-bed USD 1,750.

## Income tax and social security

Deloitte 2026: no individual income tax. Employee social security 5% up to XCD 6,500 a month; the Housing and Social Development Levy starts above XCD 2,253.33 a month: 3.5% to XCD 6,500, 10% to XCD 8,000, 12% above. At a USD 150,000 salary the levy is roughly 10% of pay. The Levy Act (Cap. 20.21, 2017 revision) charges the levy on 'the wages of every person employed' and defines an employed person by the Social Security Act's employments, so self-employed income is not subject to it; the IRD now refers to a Social Services Levy on wages. Self-employed social security: 10% of the selected income category up to the XCD 6,500 monthly ceiling (Social Security Regulations Schedules 1 and 2, 2017 revision).

## Digital nomad visa

Digital resident status (Immigration (Amendment) Act 2021): up to 12 months, full-time employee of a company registered and operating abroad, no tax statement, fees by regulation. Third-party guides disagree on whether it is operating; treated as low confidence.

## Expat regimes

None found.

## Living costs

SKN Real Estate long-term rentals (17 distinct listings, asking rents in USD, postings 1 month to 6 years old, furnishing mixed): 1-bed median USD 925 from 8 units (Frigate Bay, Basseterre, St Peters; range 750 to 1,500; two marked Currently Rented excluded); 2-bed median USD 1,750 from 4 units (1,400 to 2,550). One agency and ageing postings: indicative only.

## Residence routes

The 2021 'digital resident' category (up to 12 months, full-time employee of a foreign company) exists in law but its operation was not confirmed; otherwise remote workers enter as visitors. Skilled employees of a local employer need a work permit (not researched). Citizenship by investment (Sustainable Island State Contribution USD 250,000, or real estate from USD 325,000, per data/jurisdictions/KNA.json) is separate. EU, US and other citizens are treated alike. US citizens remain taxable in the US on worldwide income; there is no local income tax to credit.

## Open questions

- Treating the levy as bracketed income tax is a modelling choice; it is a payroll levy on wages (Cap. 20.21) and does not apply to self-employed income, so the brackets overstate a self-employed person's tax.
- The Law Commission's 2017 revision of the self-employed rate and ceiling was read; later amendments were not checked, but Deloitte 2026 still gives the XCD 6,500 monthly ceiling and 5% employee rate.
- Whether the Social Services Levy named in a December 2025 IRD notice is the renamed Housing and Social Development Levy, and whether its rates changed, was not confirmed.
- Only the 2021 Bill text (as posted by the Toronto consulate) was found; no current immigration-department page confirms the category is being issued, and other guides say it is not. No income threshold, fee or tax statement was found.
- KNA has no personal income tax, so the status is tax-neutral for earnings apart from the payroll levy.
- Whether the digital resident status is currently issued.
- Rents come from one agency's page with old postings (8 one-bed, 4 two-bed).
