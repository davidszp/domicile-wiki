# Hong Kong — earned income, nomad and expat dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026

## Summary

Salaries tax is the lower of progressive rates (2% to 17%) on net chargeable income after the HKD 145,000 basic allowance (2026/27, up from HKD 132,000) and a standard rate of 15% on the first HKD 5 million of net income (16% above), so the effective top rate is 15%. Employees and the self-employed each pay 5% MPF capped at HKD 1,500 a month. Self-employed profits are under profits tax (7.5% to HKD 2m, then 15%) on a territorial basis, and fees for services performed in Hong Kong are Hong Kong-source.

## Income tax and social security

Tax year recorded: 2026/27. Currency: HKD. Marginal brackets on taxable income (local tax folded in where noted):

| From | Rate |
|---|---|
| 0 | 2% |
| 50,000 | 6% |
| 100,000 | 10% |
| 150,000 | 14% |
| 200,000 | 17% |

- Personal allowance: 145000
- Employee social security: 5%, cap 360000
- Self-employed social security: 5%, cap 360000

Self-employed pay MPF at 5% of relevant income between HKD 7,100 and HKD 30,000 a month, maximum HKD 1,500 a month (HKD 18,000 a year). A sole trader pays profits tax, not salaries tax: 7.5% on the first HKD 2,000,000 of assessable profits and 15% above (IRD). Only profits arising in or derived from Hong Kong are taxed. Re-checked on the MPFA page on 2026-10-15: 5%, relevant income HKD 7,100 to 30,000 a month, maximum HKD 1,500 a month.

No local or municipal income tax. The progressive schedule is capped by the standard rate: 15% on the first HKD 5,000,000 of net income (before personal allowances), 16% above, so the marginal rate paid by a high earner is effectively 15-16% rather than 17%. The brackets above are applied after the basic allowance. The 2026/27 basic allowance of HKD 145,000 (2025/26: HKD 132,000) is on the IRD 2026-27 Budget page, where the allowance measures are recorded as passed by LegCo on 13 May 2026 and gazetted on 22 May 2026; progressive rates are unchanged per secondary sources. Employment income: Hong Kong employment is taxable; for non-Hong Kong employment only services rendered in Hong Kong are taxed (time apportionment), ignoring visits of 60 days or fewer (PwC).

## Expat regimes

### Top Talent Pass Scheme (TTPS)

Visa without a job offer for people with HKD 2.5m annual income in the year before applying (Category A) or a degree from a listed university plus 3 years of experience in the last five years (Category B), or a recent graduate (Category C, quota). It does not change tax: salaries tax follows the source of the employment income, not immigration status.

- Treatment: standard
- Minimum income: none recorded
- Duration: 6 years. Initial stay 36 months for Category A and 24 months for B and C; an extension is normally up to 3 years and is not guaranteed (ImmD), so 3+3 = 6 is used for Category A. A top-tier stream gives a six-year extension to holders with 2 years under the scheme and salaries tax income of HKD 2m or more.
- Foreign income required: False; local employment required: False; social security applies: True
- Confidence: medium (verified)

### General Employment Policy (GEP)

Employer-sponsored work visa for professionals with a job offer that cannot readily be filled locally and market-rate pay. No special tax regime; standard salaries tax.

- Treatment: standard
- Minimum income: none recorded
- Duration: 8 years. Initial stay normally 36 months, extensions normally follow a 3-2 year pattern (ImmD), read here as 3+3+2 = 8 years. The ImmD page states a fixed HKD 2m salary only for the top-tier stream (2 years under GEP and salaries tax income of at least HKD 2m, five-year stay); the ordinary GEP has no salary figure beyond market rates.
- Foreign income required: False; local employment required: True; social security applies: True
- Confidence: medium (verified)

### Territorial profits tax (offshore profits untaxed)

Profits tax applies only to profits arising in or derived from Hong Kong; profits earned abroad are not taxed even if remitted (IRD). It does not cover a contractor who does the work while living in Hong Kong: IRD Departmental Interpretation and Practice Notes No. 21 (revised) set the source of service fee income as the place where the services are performed, so fees earned by working in Hong Kong are Hong Kong-source and taxable, whoever the client is. The exemption only reaches fees for services performed outside Hong Kong.

- Treatment: exempt
- Minimum income: none recorded
- Duration: not stated years. Permanent feature of the tax system, not a time-limited regime.
- Foreign income required: True; local employment required: False; social security applies: True
- Covers pay for work done in the country: False
- Confidence: high (verified)

## Residence routes

EU, US and other citizens: no digital nomad visa was found. Skilled employees come through the General Employment Policy (job offer, market-rate pay, initial 36 months, extensions in a 3-2 pattern); high earners (HKD 2.5m the previous year) or graduates of listed universities with 3 years of experience can apply for the Top Talent Pass Scheme without a job offer (36 months for Category A, 24 for B and C). The Immigration Department pages do not distinguish nationalities; visa-free visitor stays do not allow local work.
