# Hungary — earned income, nomad and expat dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026

## Summary

Hungary taxes personal income at a flat 15% with no local income tax; employees pay 18.5% social security. The White Card nomad visa needs EUR 3,000 net a month, lasts up to 1 year plus one extension, and bars Hungarian-source work. KATA now covers only sole traders serving private individuals, so freelancers use itemised costs or the flat-rate (atalanyado) regime with a 45% cost ratio in 2026. A Budapest 1-bedroom is about HUF 270,000 and a 2-bedroom HUF 380,000.

## Income tax and social security

Marginal rates on taxable income (HUF):

- from 0.00: 15.00%

Personal allowance deducted before the brackets: 0.00 HUF.

Employee social security and health: 18.50% (cap: none stated). Self-employed: 31.50% (cap: none stated).

Flat 15% personal income tax on nearly all income; independent-activity income counts at 90% of gross or gross minus deductible costs; family and other allowances exist. Employee social security contribution 18.5% of gross, no cap stated (minimum wage HUF 322,800 a month from 1 Jan 2026).

Self-employed notes: Recorded 31.5% = 18.5% social security contribution (NAV payable-contributions page; stated for insured persons generally) plus 13% social contribution tax (szocho, NAV rate table), both applied to the sole trader's contribution base. The base is NOT confirmed: NAV states that from 1 Jan 2026 the monthly minimum base for full-time sole traders is 100% of the minimum wage or guaranteed wage minimum (HUF 322,800 a month, HUF 3,873,600 a year, so a minimum of about HUF 1.22m a year at 31.5%); how the base is set for flat-rate (atalanyado) traders and any cap were not found, so the effective rate on EUR 54,000 and EUR 130,000 is unverified. KATA is limited since 1 Sep 2022 to full-time sole proprietors whose income is only from private individuals (HUF 50,000 a month standard, ceiling HUF 18m); a revival is announced but not enacted. The flat-rate regime (atalanyado) has a 45% cost ratio for 2026 (50% in 2027), VAT exemption threshold HUF 20m.

Local tax: No local income tax in the PwC page.

## Digital nomad visa

The White Card is Hungary's digital nomad permit: monthly net income of at least EUR 3,000 (about EUR 36,000 a year) for six months before entry and throughout the stay; one year plus one year extension; the applicant must not work in Hungary or hold shares in a Hungarian company; health insurance and accommodation proof are needed. Family reunification is generally unavailable.

## Expat regimes

No expat tax regime was found. KATA is not available to most freelancers (private-individual clients only).

Regimes recorded in the JSON file:

- **White Card (digital nomad residence permit)** (digital_nomad_visa, treatment: standard): Residence permit for non-EEA remote workers with a foreign employer or foreign company income of at least EUR 3,000 NET a month for at least six months before entry and throughout the stay (OIF factsheet, official; a secondary guide says gross, the official source governs). No gainful activity in Hungary and no shares in a Hungarian company. Valid up to 1 year, extendable once for 1 year (extension needs 90 days of stay in the prior 180). Family members generally cannot get a reunification permit. Under 183 days a year the holder is not Hungarian tax-resident per a secondary guide; as a resident the flat 15% applies. The OIF factsheet contains no tax statement. Minimum income: 36,000 EUR. Duration (years): 2. Social security still applies: False.
- **Flat-rate tax (atalanyado) for sole traders** (other, treatment: partial_exemption): Sole traders may deduct a fixed share of revenue instead of actual costs: 45% in 2026, 50% in 2027, so 55% of revenue is taxable at the normal 15% rate. VAT exemption threshold HUF 20 million for 2026. Minimum income: none stated. Duration (years): None. Social security still applies: True.

## Living costs

Budapest: 1-bedroom HUF 270,000, 2-bedroom HUF 380,000 a month (Investropa, June 2026 estimate).

## Residence routes

EU citizens may live and work freely. US and other non-EEA remote workers can use the White Card (up to 2 years in total); skilled employees need a work-based permit (e.g. guest worker or Hungarian Card), which was not researched from an official page.

## Open questions
