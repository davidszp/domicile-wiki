# South Africa - earned income, nomad and expat dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026/27

## Summary
2026/27 resident tax runs from 18% to a 45% top rate above ZAR 1,878,600, with the primary rebate of ZAR 17,820 folded into the brackets as a 0% band to the ZAR 99,000 tax threshold (under age 65). The only employee levy is UIF at 1% up to ZAR 212,544 a year; the self-employed pay no equivalent social security. The Remote Work Visa requires gross annual income of about ZAR 650,796.

## Income tax and social security
Marginal rates on taxable income (ZAR):
- from 0 ZAR: 0%
- from 99,000 ZAR: 18%
- from 245,100 ZAR: 26%
- from 383,100 ZAR: 31%
- from 530,200 ZAR: 36%
- from 695,800 ZAR: 39%
- from 887,000 ZAR: 41%
- from 1,878,600 ZAR: 45%

- Employee social security: 1%, income cap 212,544 ZAR a year.
- Self-employed social security: 0%, income cap none stated. No social security levy for self-employed persons (PwC: no comprehensive social security system). Employer-only Skills Development Levy of 1% is not counted.
- Notes: The primary rebate (ZAR 17,820, SARS 2026/27) is folded into the brackets exactly as a 0% band up to the ZAR 99,000 threshold (18% x 99,000 = 17,820); the brackets above are SARS's. Secondary/tertiary rebates and medical scheme credits are not applied. Tax year 1 March 2026 to 28 February 2027.
- Confidence: high (verified).

## Digital nomad visa
### Remote Work Visitor's Visa (s11(1)(b)(iv)) (digital nomad visa)
Visa for people employed by or contracting with a foreign employer or clients, effective 9 October 2024; local employment is not allowed and status cannot be changed inside the country. Holders are taxed at normal South African rates once tax resident; SARS registration is needed after 183 days in a 12-month period (treaty residents) or on arrival otherwise, and may apply regardless of a tax treaty.

- Treatment: standard; minimum income: 650,796 ZAR a year; duration: 3 years (Valid up to 36 months, reported not renewable (WhereNext).); foreign income required: True; local employment required: False; local social security applies: False.
- Confidence: medium (partially_verified).

## Expat regimes
No expat or impatriate regime found; the standard resident rules apply.

- 2-bedroom: 18,500 ZAR a month. Cape Town 2-bedroom average monthly rent, 2026 (range ZAR 11,500-45,000; PayProp, Rode Q4 2025, Private Property based estimate, sample size not disclosed).

## Residence routes
EU, US and other citizens: visa-free entry varies by passport, but the Remote Work Visitor's Visa lets a remote worker for a foreign employer stay more than three months and up to three years (reported minimum gross income ZAR 650,796). It does not lead to permanent residence. Skilled employees need a general work visa or critical skills visa tied to a South African employer; those rules were not researched here.
