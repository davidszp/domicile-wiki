# Vietnam — earned income, nomad and expat dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026

## Summary

Residents pay 5% to 35% on employment income under a five-bracket schedule, after a VND 15.5 million monthly (VND 186 million a year) personal deduction for tax periods from 1 January 2026 (Resolution 110/2025). The new Personal Income Tax Law 109/2025/QH15 takes effect 1 July 2026; KPMG and Rödl report its resident salary and wage rules, and the five brackets, as applying from the 2026 tax period. Residents are taxed on worldwide income. Self-employed business income is taxed on revenue (0.5-5%) or profit (15-20%), with a revenue threshold below which no PIT is due (PwC: VND 1 billion; KPMG and Rödl report VND 500 million under the new law). Employees pay 9% social and health insurance up to VND 50.6 million a month.

## Income tax and social security

Rates apply to taxable income in VND; allowance 186,000,000.

| From | Marginal rate |
|---|---|
| 0 | 5% |
| 120,000,000 | 10% |
| 360,000,000 | 20% |
| 720,000,000 | 30% |
| 1,200,000,000 | 35% |

- Employee social security: 9%, annual income cap: 607,200,000.
- Self-employed social security: 0%, cap: none.
- Notes: Employee: social insurance 8% + health insurance 1% (as listed by PwC) on salary up to 20x the statutory base salary (VND 50.6 million a month from 1 July 2026, VND 46.8 million before); unemployment insurance 1% is for Vietnamese only. The annual cap shown uses VND 50.6 million x 12. SI needs a work permit and a Vietnamese labour contract of one year or more; HI applies from one month. Independent foreign contractors have no mandatory SI. Business income: 0.5-5% of revenue or 15-20% of profit; PIT-free revenue threshold: PwC says VND 1 billion; KPMG (24 Feb 2026) and Rödl (Feb 2026) report a rise from VND 100 million to VND 500 million under Law 109/2025, so treat VND 1 billion as unconfirmed.
- Local tax: No local income tax. Dependant deduction VND 6.2 million a month each (Resolution 110/2025).

## Living costs

City: Ho Chi Minh City.

- 1-bedroom rent: VND 15,215,659 a month (2026). Numbeo average 1-bedroom apartment in city centre, 1,080 entries from 109 contributors in 12 months (all city price data); range VND 12-25 million.
- 2-bedroom rent: USD 1,750 a month (2026). Midpoint of the USD 1,300-2,200 monthly range that Cities Insider gives as its 2026 estimate for a 2-bedroom apartment in Thao Dien (District 2), the main expat area. No sample size or method is stated and furnishing is not specified; a guide estimate, not a market-report median. Individual agent listings seen for Thao Dien 2-bedrooms ranged about VND 22-38 million.

2-bedroom value is a weak guide estimate quoted in USD (Numbeo has no 2-bedroom line; 3-bedroom city centre VND 32.97 million).

## Residence routes

EU, US and other citizens: Vietnam has no digital nomad visa as of February 2026; remote workers rely on visa exemption (about 14-45 days by nationality) or the 90-day multiple-entry e-visa, which is a tourist permit so working remotely sits in a grey area. Skilled employees need a work permit and temporary residence card via a Vietnamese employer; a five-year Talent Visa launched in August 2025 for high-profile professionals. Work permit rules were not researched in detail.

## Open questions

- No nomad visa; no expat tax regime found.
- Work permit and residence card requirements not researched.
- 2-bedroom rent is a USD guide range midpoint without a stated method.
- Law 109/2025/QH15 takes effect 1 July 2026; KPMG's alert gives no separate date for the brackets and Rödl says resident salary rules apply from the 2026 tax period. Whether the five-bracket table applied from 1 January or 1 July 2026 was not confirmed from the law text.
- Revenue threshold for PIT-free business income: PwC VND 1 billion vs KPMG/Rödl VND 500 million.
- Treatment of foreign contractors paid from abroad (business income vs. employment) not verified; KPMG's alert does not cover sourcing rules.
