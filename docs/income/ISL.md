# Iceland — earned income, nomad and expat dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026

## Summary
Top marginal rate 46.29% (31.35% state + 14.94% municipal). Employee pension 4%; no separate employee social security. Remote-work visa: yes, non-EEA only, ISK 1,000,000 a month, 180 days maximum, no tax change. No expat tax regime found. 1-bed rent about USD 1,956 in central Reykjavik.

## Income tax and social security
State brackets (monthly): 16.55% on the first ISK 498,122, 23.05% on the next 900,328, 31.35% above 1,398,450; municipal 14.94%. Personal tax credit ISK 72,492 a month (ISK 869,904 a year; confirmed on Skatturinn). Pension: 4% employee, 11.5% employer, minimum 15.5% also for the self-employed.

## Digital nomad visa
Yes, for non-EEA citizens: ISK 1,000,000 a month (1,300,000 with partner), 90-180 days, not renewable, no work for Icelandic employers, no special tax regime (Wise summary of island.is).

## Expat regimes
None found. PwC's Iceland pages mention none.

## Living costs
Central Reykjavik 1-bed about USD 1,956 a month (range 1,800-2,400); no 2-bed figure.

## Residence routes
EEA: free. Non-EEA: remote-work visa (180 days) or employer-sponsored permit.
