# Lithuania — earned income, nomad and expat dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026

## Summary

From 2026 Lithuania taxes aggregated income at 20% up to EUR 83,237, 25% up to EUR 138,729 and 32% above; there is no local income tax. Employees pay 19.5% (including 6.98% health) up to 60 average wages; individual-activity earners pay 19.5% on 90% of income up to 43 average wages (EUR 99,422), with a credit giving effective income tax of 5-20% below EUR 42,500. No nomad visa or expat regime was found. Vilnius rents are about EUR 700 (1-bed) and EUR 950 (2-bed).

## Income tax and social security

Marginal rates on taxable income (EUR):

- from 0.00: 20.00%
- from 83,237.40: 25.00%
- from 138,729.00: 32.00%

Personal allowance deducted before the brackets: 0.00 EUR.

Employee social security and health: 19.50% (cap: 138,729.00). Self-employed: 17.55% (cap: 110,469.00).

From 1 Jan 2026 most income is aggregated: 20% up to 36 average wages (EUR 83,237.40), 25% to 60 average wages (EUR 138,729), 32% above; some income is taxed at a flat 15%. NPD (non-taxable allowance): maximum EUR 747 a month at pay up to the EUR 1,153 minimum wage, reduced by 0.49 x (income - minimum wage), ending at EUR 2,677.49 a month; not modelled (personal_allowance 0). Employee social security 19.5% including 6.98% health, withheld; above 60 average wages (EUR 138,729) only the 6.98% health applies (recorded cap EUR 138,729 for the capped part).

Self-employed notes: Recorded 17.55% is the effective rate on income: 19.5% (including 6.98% health) levied on 90% of individual-activity income; the cap is the EUR 99,422 base ceiling (43 average wages) expressed as income (99,422 / 0.9 = EUR 110,469), derived. Statutory: 19.5% including 6.98% health, on 90% of taxable individual-activity income, capped at 43 average wages (EUR 99,422). Business certificate holders pay 15.7% on the official minimum monthly salary. For individual activity with annual income up to EUR 42,500, a tax credit gives effective rates from 5% to 20% (KPMG); above that the main progressive rates apply.

Local tax: There are no local, state or provincial personal income tax rates (PwC).

## Digital nomad visa

No Lithuanian digital nomad visa was found.

## Expat regimes

No expat or impatriate regime was found in the sources opened.

Regimes recorded in the JSON file:

- **Individual activity credit (effective 5-20% income tax up to EUR 42,500)** (other, treatment: standard): Self-employed individual activity with annual income up to EUR 42,500 gets a tax credit that gives effective income tax rates from 5% to 20%; above that the main progressive rates apply. Social contributions at 19.5% on 90% of income, capped at EUR 99,422. Minimum income: none stated. Duration (years): None. Social security still applies: True.

## Living costs

Vilnius: 1-bedroom EUR 700, 2-bedroom EUR 950 a month (Investropa, 2026).

## Residence routes

EU citizens may live and work freely. Non-EU nationals need a temporary residence permit for work or business; no remote-worker route was found and no official Lithuanian page was opened, so the details are unverified.

## Open questions

- Official residence routes for non-EU contractors and employees not opened.
- NPD effect not modelled for low earners.
