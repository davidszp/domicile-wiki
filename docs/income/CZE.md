# Czechia — earned income, nomad and expat dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026

## Summary

Czechia taxes resident income at 15% up to CZK 1,762,812 and 23% above, with no local income tax. Employees pay 11.6% (7.1% social, 4.5% health). Self-employed people can use a flat-rate regime (pausalni rezim) with a fixed monthly payment of CZK 9,162, 16,745 or 27,139 covering tax, social and health insurance, if income is up to CZK 2 million and they are not VAT payers. No nomad visa was researched; Prague rents are about CZK 19,000 (1+kk) and 25,000 (2+kk).

## Income tax and social security

Marginal rates on taxable income (CZK):

- from 0.00: 15.00%
- from 1,762,812.00: 23.00%

Personal allowance deducted before the brackets: 0.00 CZK.

Employee social security and health: 11.60% (cap: 2,350,416.00). Self-employed: 22.81% (cap: 2,350,416.00).

Resident income tax is 15% up to CZK 1,762,812 (36x average monthly wage) and 23% above; no local income taxes. Employee share of insurance: 7.1% social (cap CZK 2,350,416 in 2026) and 4.5% health (no cap); the recorded rate is the combined 11.6% and the cap applies to the social part only.

Self-employed notes: Ordinary regime (derived effective rate on profit, recorded 22.81%): social insurance 29.2% (pension 28% plus 1.2% state employment policy, ČSSZ premium-rates page; sickness insurance 2.7% is voluntary and not included) on an assessment base of 55% of profit (the 55% share is from the statute as recalled and was NOT confirmed on an opened page; ČSSZ states only that the minimum base is 50% of the previous year's average tax base), giving about 16.06% of profit; health insurance 13.5% on a base of 50% of profit after expenses (VZP), giving 6.75%, minimum advance CZK 3,306 a month in 2026. The social cap (48x average wage, CZK 2,350,416) applies to the assessment base, not to profit, so it is not reached on EUR 54,000 or 130,000 of profit. In the flat-rate regime (income up to CZK 2m, not a VAT payer) the monthly payments for 2026 are CZK 9,162 (band I, income to 1m), 16,745 (band II, to 1.5m) and 27,139 (band III, to 2m), of which tax is 100 / 4,963 / 9,320, pension 5,756 / 8,191 / 12,527 and health 3,306 / 3,591 / 5,292. Lump-sum expense deductions in the ordinary regime: 80% (crafts, agriculture), 60% (some trades), 40%, 30% (rental), on a base of at most CZK 2m. Entrepreneurs may opt out of the sickness fund.

Local tax: No local income tax. Taxpayer tax credit not stated by the PwC page and not included, so personal_allowance is 0.

## Digital nomad visa

No Czech digital nomad visa was researched. Non-EU freelancers typically use a long-term residence permit for business (Zivno); no official page was opened, so conditions are unverified.

## Expat regimes

No expat or impatriate tax regime found in the sources opened.

Regimes recorded in the JSON file:

- **Flat-rate regime (pausalni rezim) for self-employed** (other, treatment: standard): Fixed monthly payment covering income tax, pension and health insurance: CZK 9,162 (band I), 16,745 (band II), 27,139 (band III) in 2026. Requires income up to CZK 2 million (band limits 1m / 1.5m / 2m, higher for 60%-80% expense activities), not a VAT payer, and limits on dependent-work income. Minimum income: none stated. Duration (years): None. Social security still applies: True.

## Living costs

Prague median asking rents: 1+kk CZK 19,000, 2+kk CZK 25,000 (June sample of 585 listings via Investropa).

## Residence routes

EU citizens can live and work freely and register a trade or employment. Non-EU nationals, including US citizens, need a long-term visa or permit: an employee card or blue card for skilled employees, or a long-term residence for business (trade licence) for freelancers; conditions were not verified from an official page.
