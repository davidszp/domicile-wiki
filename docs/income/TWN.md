# Taiwan — earned income, nomad and expat dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026

## Summary

Resident individuals pay progressive tax of 5% to 40% (top rate above TWD 4.98m), plus an alternative minimum tax. Employees' own social insurance is small: labor insurance 2.3%, employment insurance 0.2% (both capped at TWD 45,800 monthly insured salary) and national health insurance about 1.55% (insured-salary cap TWD 313,000). The Employment Gold Card exempts 50% of salary above TWD 3m for up to five years.

No local income tax. Rates are for the 2025 return (filed 2026), the latest on PwC (page reviewed 20 August 2026); 2026 rates were not published. Allowance = personal exemption TWD 97,000 + standard deduction TWD 131,000 for a single filer; employees also get a salary special deduction of TWD 218,000 (not included). Alternative minimum tax: 20% on income above TWD 7.5m, payable if higher. Employee social insurance: labor insurance 11.5% x 20% = 2.3% and employment insurance 1% x 20% = 0.2% on insured salary up to TWD 45,800 a month (about TWD 1,145 a month), plus NHI 5.17% x 30% = 1.551% on insured salary up to TWD 313,000 a month; the recorded 1.551% rate and TWD 3,756,000 annual cap cover only NHI, so labor and employment insurance (small, capped) are excluded. Foreigners cannot join the labor pension.

## Expat regimes

### Employment Gold Card: 50% exemption on salary above TWD 3m

Gold Card holders working in Taiwan for the first time may apply for a 50% exemption on annual salary above TWD 3 million for the first 5 years (Gold Card site); KPMG adds that the holder must reside more than 183 days in the year and not have been a Taiwan tax resident in the five prior years, and that the exempt portion is excluded from the AMT. exemption_threshold is TWD 3,000,000: only salary above it is 50% exempt. The card itself lets a professional stay and work 1 to 3 years with no sponsor or job offer.

- Treatment: partial_exemption (exempt share 50%)
- Minimum income: none recorded
- Exemption applies only above: 3,000,000 TWD a year
- Duration: 5 years. Five years of tax incentive; card issued for 1 to 3 years and presumably renewed (renewal not verified). Exemption applies only to the part of salary above TWD 3m, so there is no minimum income to qualify for the card's tax benefit.
- Foreign income required: False; local employment required: False; social security applies: True
- Confidence: medium (partially_verified)

### No digital nomad visa (Gold Card is the nearest route)

No digital nomad visa was found. Foreigners staying 183 days or more in a calendar year are tax residents; the Gold Card needs no sponsor and is the route for independent professionals. Resident status taxes income per the normal regime.

- Treatment: standard
- Minimum income: none recorded
- Duration: 3 years. Gold Card lasts 1 to 3 years.
- Foreign income required: False; local employment required: False; social security applies: True
- Confidence: low (partially_verified)

## Residence routes

EU, US and other citizens: the Employment Gold Card (assessed on professional skills, no sponsor or job offer, 1-3 years) is the route for both remote workers and skilled employees; ordinary employer-sponsored work permits also exist but were not researched. Foreigners spending 183 days or more in a calendar year are tax residents.

## Open questions

- Official visa pages beyond the Gold Card site not opened.
- No dedicated digital nomad visa.
- Self-employed social insurance rates not found.
- Labor and employment insurance (max about TWD 1,145 a month) excluded from the employee rate; NHI cap of TWD 313,000 from a payroll blog (PwC says only 'various caps').
- 2026 tax-year brackets/allowances not yet published; 2025 figures used.
- KPMG alert dates from 2023; conditions may have changed.
- Whether the incentive applies to self-employment income (the text says 'salary') is not confirmed; applies_to is employment only.
- Taxation of foreign-source income of residents (overseas income basic tax) not found on opened pages; only a 183-day residence test was confirmed.
- Deductibility of NHI premiums (the recorded 1.551%) not established: PwC deductions page mentions only labour and group insurance premiums (TWD 24,000 a person, itemised deductions).
- Salary special deduction of up to TWD 218,000 (or total salary if lower) per person, alternative to itemised work expenses; not included in personal_allowance.
