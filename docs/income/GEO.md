# Georgia — earned income, nomad and expat dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026

## Summary
Flat 20% income tax; resident foreign-source income exempt; 1% small-business status for individual entrepreneurs up to GEL 500,000 of turnover. Pension 2% employee; self-employed exempt from mandatory joining (4% voluntary). Remotely from Georgia: reported USD 2,000 a month, status uncertain. Tbilisi (Vake) rent USD 750 (1-bed), USD 1,150 (2-bed).

## Income tax and social security
Flat 20% on Georgian-source income. Residents are exempt on income without a Georgian source (PwC). Pension 2% employee; self-employed are exempt from mandatory joining (PwC), the voluntary rate being 4%. No other social security. Georgian-source income includes work physically done in Georgia (Tax Code Art. 104(1)(a),(c),(c.a)); the foreign-source exemption (Art. 82(1)(u)) does not reach it.

## Digital nomad visa
Remotely from Georgia: USD 2,000 a month, health insurance, clean record, one year renewable; status in 2026 unconfirmed. The programme gives no tax relief of its own and, under Tax Code Arts. 82 and 104, the territorial exemption does not cover remote work performed while living in Georgia (covers_local_work = false). Visa-free stay of 365 days for many nationalities.

## Expat regimes
Small business status 1% of turnover up to GEL 500,000 for individual entrepreneurs. Not a nomad regime but relevant for the contractor persona if the work is Georgian-sourced.

## Living costs
Tbilisi Vake long-term rents (2026): 1-bed USD 600-900, 2-bed USD 900-1,400.

## Residence routes
Visa-free 365 days; then residence permit (not verified).

## Open questions
- The Tax Code text opened (matsne.gov.ge) is a consolidated version dated December 2023; later amendments to Art. 82/104 not checked.
- Self-employed pension: PwC lists self-employed as exempt from mandatory joining yet states a 4% rate; modelled as 0% mandatory.
- Whether the programme is still operating in 2026 is unconfirmed; no official page opened (georgia.travel and mfa.gov.ge URLs returned 404).
- Statute text is a Dec 2023 consolidation; a later amendment or Revenue Service ruling treating remote work for foreign clients as foreign-source was not found. CountryTaxCalc asserts it without citation.
- Exclusion list and revocation rule are from consultancies (Andersen, PB Services via search results), not from the Revenue Service.
- 1% applies to turnover, not profit.
- Official government and Revenue Service pages not opened.
- Programme status unclear.
- Whether the 2% employee pension contribution is deducted before the 20% income tax is not established: PwC says employees may not claim deductions and is silent on the contribution; secondary guides disagree. Check the Tax Code / Law on Funded Pensions or rs.ge. social_security_deductible left null.
