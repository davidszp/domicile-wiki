# Sweden — earned income, nomad and expat dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026

## Summary
Top marginal rate 52.38% (32.38% average municipal + 20% state above SEK 643,000). Employee pension fee 7% (creditable), self-employed 28.97%. Expert tax relief exempts 25% of salary for up to 7 years (salary above SEK 88,800 a month). No nomad visa. 1-bed rent about SEK 18,500 central second-hand; 2-bed not found.

## Income tax and social security
Municipal 32.38% average; state 20% above SEK 643,000 of taxable income; basic allowance SEK 17,400-45,600. Employee public pension fee 7% up to SEK 673,038 (maximum SEK 47,100), normally credited in full. Self-employed egenavgifter 28.97%.

## Digital nomad visa
None identified.

## Expat regimes
Expert tax relief: 25% exemption, seven years, minimum SEK 88,800 a month (1.5 x price base amount SEK 59,200), five-year look-back, apply within three months.

## Living costs
Stockholm second-hand central 1-bed (tvåa) SEK 17,000-20,000; regulated first-hand average SEK 9,729.

## Residence routes
EU: free. Others: employer-sponsored work permit.

## Open questions
- Stockholm municipal rate not verified; average used.
- Whether egenavgifter has a ceiling on parts of the contribution was not checked (28.97% total is Skatteverket's 2026 figure).
- Source is a BDO page; Skatteverket's own expert-tax page returned 404.
- Secondary sources disagree on the look-back (five vs ten years) and on pending 2027 reform.
- 2-bedroom rent missing.
- Residence-route details not verified on Migrationsverket.
- The 7% general pension fee (allman pensionsavgift) is offset by a tax reduction for the full amount (Skatteverket: "de allra flesta far skattereduktion for hela beloppet"), so net effect is roughly zero extra tax on it; the model treats it as a pure cost.
