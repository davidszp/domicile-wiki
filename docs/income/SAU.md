# Saudi Arabia - earned income, nomad and expat dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026

## Summary
There is no individual income tax scheme in Saudi Arabia; employment income is untaxed (PwC, reviewed 29 Jul 2026). Non-employment income is taxed under entity/permanent-establishment rules. Non-Saudi employees pay no GOSI contribution (the 2% occupational-hazard charge is borne by the employer); Saudi employees pay 9.75% (9% plus 0.75% SANED).

## Income tax and social security
Marginal rates on taxable income (SAR):
- from 0 SAR: 0%

- Employee social security: 0%, income cap none stated.
- Self-employed social security: 0%, income cap none stated. Self-employed contributions not covered by PwC. A freelancer's non-employment income can fall under the entity (corporate) rules rather than personal tax; the 20% foreign-owned profit tax is noted in the existing jurisdiction file.
- Notes: No local income-based taxes. Saudi employees' GOSI base is basic salary plus housing, capped at SAR 45,000 a month.
- Confidence: medium (partially_verified).

## Digital nomad visa
No digital-nomad visa was found in the sources opened.

## Expat regimes
No expat or impatriate regime found; the standard resident rules apply.

## Living costs
City: Riyadh.

- 1-bedroom: 2,500 SAR a month. Riyadh average 1-bedroom monthly rent, 2026 (range SAR 2,000-5,500; portal asking rents adjusted, sample size not disclosed). Numbeo city-centre 1-bedroom is SAR 4,452, so central expat areas are far above this citywide figure.
- 2-bedroom: 4,800 SAR a month. Riyadh average 2-bedroom monthly rent, 2026 (range SAR 3,000-10,000; Bayut, JLL, CBRE based, sample size not disclosed).

## Residence routes
EU, US and other citizens: no visa-free right to work. Skilled employees are sponsored by a Saudi employer for an iqama (residence and work permit). Premium Residency for wealthy foreigners is noted in the existing jurisdiction file. No dedicated digital-nomad visa was found.
