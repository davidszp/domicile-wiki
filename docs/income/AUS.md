# Australia — earned income, nomad and expat dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026

## Summary

Residents pay 0% to AUD 18,200, then 15% (from 1 July 2026, down from 16%), 30% above AUD 45,000, 37% above AUD 135,000 and 45% above AUD 190,000, plus a 2% Medicare levy. There is no social security tax; employers pay 12% superannuation on top. Temporary visa holders are exempt on foreign-source income.

## Income tax and social security

Tax year recorded: 2026/27. Currency: AUD. Marginal brackets on taxable income (local tax folded in where noted):

| From | Rate |
|---|---|

- Personal allowance: 0
- Employee social security: 2%, cap none recorded
- Self-employed social security: 2%, cap none recorded

The Medicare levy of 2% of taxable income applies to residents (surcharge of 1% to 1.5% without private hospital cover above income thresholds, not included). Superannuation at 12% is an employer cost for employees; PwC does not address compulsory super for the self-employed, so none is counted.

No state or municipal income tax. The tax-free threshold of AUD 18,200 is built into the brackets (0% band). Rates for 2025-26 were 16% on AUD 18,201-45,000. Low Income Tax Offset not included. The Medicare levy is recorded as the 'social security' rate, not in the brackets, to avoid double counting.

## Expat regimes

### Temporary resident: foreign-source income exemption

A temporary visa holder who is not an Australian resident under the Social Security Act and has no Australian spouse is a temporary resident for tax and is exempt from Australian tax on foreign-source income (PwC residence page). It does not cover pay for work done in Australia: PwC states that foreign nationals are taxable on salary and allowances for services performed in Australia regardless of where the payment is made and whether it is remitted. A remote worker's salary or fees earned while living in Australia stay taxable, so the benefit is on passive foreign income.

- Treatment: exempt
- Minimum income: none recorded
- Duration: not stated years. Applies while holding a temporary visa; length depends on the visa. No minimum income is a condition of the exemption (the Core Skills Income Threshold for the employer-sponsored 482 visa is AUD 79,423 from 1 July 2026, specialist stream AUD 146,576).
- Foreign income required: True; local employment required: False; social security applies: True
- Covers pay for work done in the country: False
- Confidence: medium (partially_verified)

### No digital nomad visa; skilled route via Subclass 482

No digital nomad visa was found. Employees use employer-sponsored Subclass 482 visas (income thresholds A$79,423 Core Skills and A$146,576 Specialist Skills from 1 July 2026). A visitor-visa remote worker would not generally be allowed to work; this was not verified.

- Treatment: standard
- Minimum income: 79,423 AUD a year
- Duration: not stated years. Visa length not verified.
- Foreign income required: False; local employment required: True; social security applies: True
- Confidence: low (partially_verified)

## Residence routes

EU, US and other citizens: no digital nomad visa found; remote workers cannot rely on a visitor visa (not verified). Skilled employees are sponsored by an employer on a Subclass 482 visa (Core Skills at least A$79,423, Specialist Skills at least A$146,576 from 1 July 2026), with later options for permanent residence (not researched).
