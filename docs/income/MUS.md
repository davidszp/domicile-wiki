# Mauritius - earned income, nomad and expat dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026

## Summary
Resident income tax is 0% to MUR 500,000, 10% on the next MUR 500,000 and 20% above, from 1 July 2025. A Fair Share Contribution of 15% on leviable income above MUR 12 million (income years from 1 July 2025 for three years) lifts the top marginal rate to 35%. Employee CSG is 1.5% on pay up to MUR 50,000 a month and 3% above. Self-employed pay a fixed monthly CSG amount. Foreign-source income of residents is taxed only when remitted.

## Income tax and social security
Marginal rates on taxable income (MUR):
- from 0 MUR: 0%
- from 500,000 MUR: 10%
- from 1,000,000 MUR: 20%
- from 12,000,000 MUR: 35%

- Employee social security: 3%, income cap none stated.
- Self-employed social security: 0.06%, income cap none stated. Self-employed CSG is a fixed MUR 150 a month regardless of income (Rogers Capital page, undated, appears to predate 2025 changes): MUR 1,800 a year, about 0.06% of an income of EUR 54,000 (about MUR 2.9 million at roughly MUR 54 per EUR; approximate conversion). Premium Visa holders are reported to be exempt from CSG and NSF under an MRA FAQ (seen via search result only). Employee NSF levy 1% (PwC), CSG employee 1.5% (to MUR 50,000 a month) or 3% above; 3% shown for a high earner.
- Notes: The 35% band above MUR 12 million is the Fair Share Contribution (15%) added to 20%. Tax year runs 1 July to 30 June; a 2026 income year starts 1 July 2026. A 2026/27 budget reportedly replaces CSG with a National Pensions Fund from July 2027 (not verified).
- Confidence: medium (partially_verified).

## Digital nomad visa
### Mauritius Premium Visa (digital nomad visa)
One-year renewable visa for remote workers, retirees and long-term visitors with foreign income, with no right to local employment. Income Tax Act s.73B (as summarised by Sovereign Group and the Andersen Mauritius Tax Card 2025/26) deems income derived by a Premium Visa holder for work performed remotely from Mauritius to be derived in Mauritius only when it is remitted to Mauritius. So the relief does cover work physically done in Mauritius (covers_local_work true), but only for income left abroad: remitted income is taxed at 0/10/20%; spending through a foreign card is not a remittance, and Mauritian bank deposits are taxable unless tax paid abroad is declared. At 183 days the holder becomes tax resident.

- Treatment: exempt; minimum income: 18,000 USD a year; duration: 2 years (Valid one year, renewable; renewal not guaranteed (Sovereign Group).); foreign income required: True; local employment required: False; local social security applies: False; covers work done locally: true.
- Confidence: medium (partially_verified).

## Expat regimes
No expat or impatriate regime found; the standard resident rules apply.

## Living costs
City: Port Louis.

- 1-bedroom: 22,633 MUR a month. Port Louis city-centre 1-bedroom monthly rent, Numbeo typical value (range MUR 10,000-29,600; Port Louis had 222 entries from 16 contributors in 12 months for all categories). Port Louis is not the main expat area; coastal areas such as Grand Baie run higher.
- 2-bedroom: not found from an opened page.

## Residence routes
EU, US and other citizens: a tourist visa is usually granted on arrival; stays over 180 days a year need a visa such as the Premium Visa (online application, reported free, processed in about 48 hours). Skilled employees and investors use Occupation Permits (professional, investor, self-employed); an investor permit reportedly needs a transfer of at least USD 50,000. Residence through property starts at USD 375,000 (wiki, secondary).
