# Thailand — earned income, nomad and expat dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026

## Summary

Residents (180 days or more in a calendar year) pay progressive tax from 0% to 35% on net income; employment income gets a 50% expense deduction capped at THB 100,000 plus a THB 60,000 personal allowance. Foreign income earned from 2024 is taxable when remitted to Thailand. Employees pay 5% social security on wages up to THB 17,500 a month (max THB 875).

## Income tax and social security

Rates apply to taxable income in THB; allowance 60,000.

| From | Marginal rate |
|---|---|
| 0 | 0% |
| 150,000 | 5% |
| 300,000 | 10% |
| 500,000 | 15% |
| 750,000 | 20% |
| 1,000,000 | 25% |
| 2,000,000 | 30% |
| 5,000,000 | 35% |

- Employee social security: 5%, annual income cap: 210,000.
- Self-employed social security: 0%, cap: none.
- Notes: Section 33 applies to employees (foreign employees with work permits included). Independent contractors without a Thai employer pay no mandatory contribution; whether foreigners may join voluntary Section 40 was not verified from an official SSO page. Self-employed (section 40(2) etc.) may deduct actual expenses or a standard 10-60% depending on the business; the exact rate per activity was not listed on the PwC page.
- Local tax: No local income tax. Bracket thresholds are on net income after the 50% employment expense deduction (max THB 100,000) and allowances (THB 60,000 taxpayer, THB 60,000 non-filing spouse). The social security cap is THB 17,500 x 12 = THB 210,000 of wages.

## Digital nomad visa

### Destination Thailand Visa (DTV)

Five-year multiple-entry visa with stays of up to 180 days each (extendable) and a THB 500,000 funds requirement per the e-visa site. It does not change tax: a holder staying 180 days or more in a year is a Thai tax resident, taxed on foreign income earned from 2024 only when remitted to Thailand.

- Treatment: standard
- Covers work performed locally: not established
- Minimum income: not stated; duration: 5 years. Five-year validity, 180 days per stay with an extension option. THB 500,000 in funds. Income test not given on the opened page.
- Foreign income required: True; local employment required: False; social security applies: False
- Confidence: medium (partially_verified)

## Expat regimes

### Long-Term Resident (LTR) visa - Highly-Skilled Professional

BOI Long-Term Resident visa for highly skilled professionals working in a targeted industry. The Revenue Department's LTR tax presentation states the 17% rate is a flat withholding rate on employment income, granted to Highly-Skilled Professionals (LTR-H) who work for a company operating in a targeted industry (as defined under the Competitiveness Enhancement for Target Industries Act, the Investment Promotion Act or the Eastern Economic Corridor Act). Conditions: the employer must notify the Area Revenue Office on the prescribed form and the 17% applies from the date the office acknowledges it; tax withheld at 17% can be treated as final, provided the holder does not claim a refund or credit; the holder still files PND 95 and needs a Tax ID. The separate exemption for foreign-sourced income belongs to the Wealthy Global Citizen, Wealthy Pensioner and Work-from-Thailand Professional categories, not to LTR-H.

- Treatment: flat_rate (17%)
- Minimum income: USD 80,000; duration: 10 years. First permission 5 years, extendable by another 5 if still qualified. USD 80,000 a year average over the past two years; USD 40,000-80,000 needs extra proof (e.g. a master's degree in science or technology). Needs health insurance of USD 50,000 or Thai social security or USD 100,000 on deposit for 12 months. Fee THB 50,000.
- Foreign income required: False; local employment required: True; social security applies: True
- Confidence: high (verified)

- 2-bedroom rent: THB 39,000 a month (2026). Midpoint of an indicative THB 28,000-50,000+ range for 2-bedroom condos in Thonglor, Asoke and Phrom Phong (Sukhumvit); the article gives no sample size or method. Treat as rough.

Numbeo provides no 2-bedroom figure; the 2-bedroom value is a weak midpoint estimate.

## Residence routes

EU, US and other citizens: the Destination Thailand Visa (DTV, five years, 180-day stays, THB 500,000 funds) suits remote workers; highly skilled employees can apply for the BOI Long-Term Resident visa (Highly-Skilled Professional: USD 80,000 average income, five years, extendable by five) or take a work visa and permit through a Thai employer. Visa-exempt entry does not allow work. Nationality does not change the routes, though visa-exemption and e-visa details vary by passport.
