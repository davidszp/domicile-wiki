# Portugal — earned income, nomad and expat dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026

## Summary

Portugal's resident income tax tops out at 50.5% to 53% with the solidarity surcharge; employees pay 11% social security, the self-employed an effective 14.98% of income. IFICI offers a flat 20% for ten years, but only for eligible activities, not for remote work for a foreign employer. The D8 visa needs EUR 3,680 a month. Lisbon median asking rents: EUR 1,500 for one bedroom, EUR 1,850 for two.

Employee social security: 11.00% (cap: none stated). Self-employed: 14.98% (cap: 110,495.00).

Resident income tax is progressive from 12.5% to 48% on taxable income, plus a solidarity surcharge of 2.5% above EUR 80,000 and 5% above EUR 250,000, which gives top marginal rates of 50.5% (EUR 86,634 to 250,000) and 53%. Employees pay 11% social security with no stated cap. Self-employed pay 21.4% on a base of 70% of service income (effective 14.98% of income).

Self-employed notes: Statutory rate 21.4% on a monthly base equal to one third of the relevant quarterly income, which is 70% of service income (20% of sales); so the recorded effective rate on service income is 21.4% x 70% = 14.98%. The base is capped at 12 times the IAS; with an IAS of EUR 537.13 (the minimum base figure on the PwC page) that is a monthly base of EUR 6,445.56, reached at about EUR 110,495 of annual service income (derived: the 2026 IAS value itself was not found on an official page; EUR 537.13 is the PwC minimum base). Simplified regime (revenue below EUR 200,000): 75% of listed service income is taxed (35% for unlisted services), with a minimum-expense add-back of up to 15% of gross income (EUR 4,587.09 or social security if higher).

Local tax: No municipal income tax. The specific employment deduction and personal tax credits are not modelled, so personal_allowance is 0.

## Digital nomad visa

D8: non-EU applicants, four times the minimum wage (EUR 3,680 a month in 2026, EUR 44,160 a year) in active income, a two-year first permit then three-year renewals. Holders become tax residents and pay IRS at the normal scale, or on the simplified regime for freelancers.

## Expat regimes

IFICI replaced the NHR from 2024: a flat 20% on Portuguese-source employment and self-employment income for ten years for people in eligible activities, with foreign-source income mostly exempt; five prior years of non-residence required. Old NHR holders keep their status.

Regimes recorded in the JSON file:

- **D8 digital nomad visa** (digital_nomad_visa, treatment: standard): Residence visa for non-EU remote workers earning at least four times the Portuguese minimum wage (official consular page: average monthly income over the last three months of at least four times the monthly minimum guaranteed remuneration; EUR 920 x 4 = EUR 3,680 in 2026, minimum wage per PwC), with income from active work outside Portugal. The first residence permit lasts two years, renewals three years. Holders become tax residents and pay normal IRS (freelancers can use the simplified regime); the visa itself gives no tax relief, though IFICI may be available depending on the activity. Minimum income: 44,160 EUR. Maximum income: none. Duration (years): 5. 2-year first permit then 3-year renewals; permanent residence possible after five years. Foreign income required: True. Local employment required: False. Covers work done locally: None. Social security still applies: True.
- **IFICI (NHR 2.0)** (expat_regime, treatment: flat_rate 0.2): Flat 20% on Portuguese-source employment and self-employment income from eligible activities for ten years, with most foreign-source income exempt (pensions excluded). Requires tax residence in Portugal, no residence in the previous five years, no prior NHR, and an eligible activity in an eligible entity: higher-education research, certified start-ups, R&D centres, listed highly qualified professions (a PhD or a degree plus three years of experience), exporting companies (50% of turnover exported) or investment-contract beneficiaries. Remote work for a foreign employer does not qualify by itself. Apply by 15 January of the following year. PwC confirms: residents in the year with no Portuguese residence in the previous five years and no prior NHR, 20% on category A and B income from eligible activities in eligible entities, ten years, foreign-source income exempt except pensions and blacklisted-country income, apply by 15 January of the following year. Minimum income: none stated. Maximum income: none. Duration (years): 10. Non-renewable ten years. Foreign income required: False. Local employment required: True. Covers work done locally: None. Social security still applies: True.

## Living costs

Lisbon: 1-bedroom EUR 1,500 a month, 2-bedroom EUR 1,850 (Global Property Guide Q2 2026, median asking rents). No coworking source was opened.

## Residence routes

EU/EEA/Swiss citizens register as residents after three months. US and other non-EU remote workers use the D8 visa (EUR 3,680 a month of active income in 2026, 2-year permit then 3-year renewals); skilled employees need a work visa or a job-seeker route, which was not researched, and the EU Blue Card exists. IFICI is a tax regime open to any new resident with an eligible activity, regardless of nationality.

## Open questions

- Work-visa routes for skilled employees (including the Tech Visa) were not researched.
- Rents rest on one aggregator (Global Property Guide); no second source obtained.
- Employment-income specific deduction and dependants credits were not found on the PwC pages opened.
- IAS 2026 not confirmed from an official page (Seg-Social page had no figure); the self-employed cap is derived from the PwC EUR 537.13 minimum base.
- First-year exemption from self-employed contributions: not mentioned on the PwC page and not verified.
- D8 digital nomad visa: Income threshold (4x minimum wage, 3-month average, annualised to EUR 44,160) confirmed on the consular page vistos.mne.gov.pt; that page does not state the permit duration. The 2-year then 3-year renewal pattern rests on secondary sources.
- D8 digital nomad visa: Social security is owed unless a certificate of coverage applies.
- IFICI (NHR 2.0): The legal list of eligible activities and entities (Art. 58-A Codigo Fiscal do Investimento, Portaria 352/2024/1) could not be opened (Portal das Financas and DRE URLs returned 404); the PwC page only refers to 'eligible entities as defined in the applicable legislation'. The statement that a remote worker for a foreign employer does not qualify is therefore not verified from the primary text.
- IFICI (NHR 2.0): Whether the 20% rate is final or interacts with the solidarity surcharge was not checked.
