# Qatar - earned income, nomad and expat dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026

## Summary
Qatar taxes no employment income of individuals (salaries, wages and allowances are not taxed regardless of residence). The system is territorial: a self-employed individual may owe income tax on qualifying Qatar-source income, and the PwC page states no personal rates. Social insurance is mandatory for Qatari employees only.

## Income tax and social security
Marginal rates on taxable income (QAR):
- from 0 QAR: 0%

- Employee social security: 0%, income cap none stated.
- Self-employed social security: 0%, income cap none stated. Self-employed individuals may be taxed on Qatar-source business income (PwC states no rates; corporate-style 10% applies to foreign-owned profits per the existing jurisdiction file). Foreign-source income from foreign clients is outside the territorial charge. Social insurance applies to Qatari nationals only.
- Notes: No local income-based taxes.
- Confidence: medium (partially_verified).

## Digital nomad visa
No digital-nomad visa was found in the sources opened.

## Expat regimes
No expat or impatriate regime found; the standard resident rules apply.

## Living costs
City: Doha.

- 1-bedroom: 8,250 QAR a month. Midpoint of the Qatar Living July 2026 West Bay range for a 1-bedroom (QAR 7,000-9,500). The Pearl/Lusail range is 7,000-10,000. Numbeo city-centre 1-bedroom is QAR 6,914.
- 2-bedroom: 11,250 QAR a month. Midpoint of the Qatar Living July 2026 West Bay range for a 2-bedroom (QAR 9,500-13,000). The Pearl/Lusail range is 10,000-16,000. Listing-based typical ranges, not a measured median.

## Residence routes
EU, US and other citizens: no visa-free right to work or settle. Skilled employees are normally recruited by a Qatari employer, which arranges the work permit and residence; no dedicated remote-work or digital-nomad visa was found. Residence for property buyers (QAR 3.65 million and above in designated areas) is described in the existing jurisdiction file.
