# Finland — earned income, nomad and expat dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026

## Summary
Marginal rate in the model tops out near 45% before church tax; PwC suggests up to about 48.4% at the highest municipal rate. Employee contributions about 10.17%, self-employed YEL 24.4%. Key employee regime: flat 25%, 84 months, salary at least EUR 5,800 a month. No nomad visa identified. 1-bed rent EUR 1,150; 2-bed EUR 1,550.

## Income tax and social security
State scale 12.64% / 19% / 30.25% / 33.25% / 37.5% at 22,000 / 32,600 / 40,100 / 52,100 (PwC; low confidence on structure, conflicts with Wikipedia's 2025 table above EUR 52,100), plus municipal 4.7-10.90%. Employees: TyEL 7.30%, unemployment 0.89%, health 1.98%. YEL 24.4%.

## Digital nomad visa
None identified.

## Expat regimes
Key employee: 25% flat from 2026 (formerly 32%), EUR 5,800 minimum monthly salary, 84 months, five-year look-back, apply within 90 days.

## Living costs
Helsinki 1-bed EUR 1,150 (900-1,600), 2-bed EUR 1,550 (1,250-2,400), modelled from Statistics Finland.

## Residence routes
EU: register. Others: employee permit.
