# Barbados — family-office tax dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026
Reviewer: research agent (Claude), unreviewed by a tax adviser

## Summary

Barbados has no capital gains, inheritance, gift or wealth tax, but it does tax resident and domiciled individuals on worldwide income at 11.5% up to BBD 50,000 and 27.5% above (2026). Local dividends bear a 15% final withholding tax; foreign dividends are taxed at the marginal rate with a foreign tax credit. Resident but non-domiciled individuals are taxed on foreign income only to the extent a benefit is obtained in Barbados. Real estate and private-company share transfers bear 2.5% property transfer tax plus 1% stamp duty. Treaty network of 30-40 countries, including the US and UK.

## Residence and eligibility

Resident if present more than 182 days in a calendar year, or ordinarily resident. For non-domiciled individuals 'ordinarily resident' may apply: a permanent home in Barbados and election to reside for at least two consecutive income years. Domicile follows common law. Routes: Welcome Stamp (12 months, USD 50,000 income, USD 2,000/3,000 fee, holders not liable to income tax); SERP (amounts unverified).

## Capital gains

No CGT (PwC, Deloitte, Invest Barbados). JSON 0. Property transfer tax 2.5% on the value of land with buildings above BBD 150,000 (on the whole value of land without buildings) and on private-company shares above BBD 50,000, plus stamp duty BBD 10 per BBD 1,000 (1%). Listed shares on the Barbados Stock Exchange are exempt. Gains from trading are taxed as business income at income tax rates; there are no specific capital gains rules.

## Dividends

Standard (resident and domiciled): local dividends 15% final WHT (JSON domestic 0.15); foreign dividends at the top marginal rate 27.5% for 2026 (PIT cut from 12.5%/28.5% to 11.5%/27.5% for income year 2026, PwC significant developments) with foreign tax credit not reducing Barbados tax below 1% of taxable income (JSON foreign 0.275, credit). 2% foreign exchange fee on some foreign-currency transactions.

## Inheritance and gifts to children

No inheritance, estate or gift tax (Invest Barbados; Deloitte 2026). Gifts may attract property transfer tax 2.5% and stamp duty 1% on land and private-company shares. JSON basis none, fully_exempt.

## Wealth tax

None. Land tax 0-1% of improved value for residences.

## Special regimes

Resident non-domiciled: foreign income taxed only to the extent a benefit is obtained in Barbados (PwC; Deloitte). JSON foreign dividends 0 assuming nothing is enjoyed in Barbados. Welcome Stamp: 12-month remote-work visa, holders not liable to income tax (official page); JSON dividends 0, duration 1 year.

Duration and remittance (second pass, 2026-10-15): the resident non-domiciled basis has no statutory time limit and no deemed-domicile rule is reported (PwC, reviewed 9 October 2026); it lasts while the family keeps a foreign domicile under common law and ends if a Barbados domicile of choice is acquired. The JSON 0% on foreign dividends assumes no remittance: the dividends are kept and spent outside Barbados and no benefit is obtained in Barbados. Any foreign income from which a benefit is obtained in Barbados is taxed at 11.5%/27.5%.

## Company taxation (kept separate from personal tax)

Corporate income tax 9% in general (5.5% for some companies; 0-2% insurance; 4.5% qualifying IP income). Qualified domestic minimum top-up tax of 15% from 2024 for MNE groups with revenue of EUR 750m+. Dividends to non-residents: 0% from foreign-source income, 5% otherwise, treaty rates 0-15%.
