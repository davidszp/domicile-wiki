# United Arab Emirates (emirates) — family-office tax dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026
Reviewer: research agent (Claude), unreviewed by a tax adviser

## Summary

No emirate levies personal income, capital gains, inheritance or wealth tax, so no tax metric is overridden. Emirates differ in property registration fees (Dubai 4%, verified via PwC; Abu Dhabi commonly 2%, not officially confirmed), municipality fees on rent, courts (DIFC in Dubai, ADGM in Abu Dhabi), will registries and cost of living.

## Residence and eligibility

Residence visas and tax residence are federal (see ARE.md). Golden Visas are issued by the federal ICP or, in Dubai, GDRFA.

## Capital gains

No emirate-level tax. No override of the federal file (all rates 0).

## Dividends

No emirate-level tax. No override.

## Inheritance and gifts to children

No emirate inheritance tax. Succession practice differs: Dubai hosts the DIFC Courts Wills Service (Full Will AED 10,000; guardianship clauses for minors in Dubai or Ras Al Khaimah); Abu Dhabi has its own non-Muslim personal status law (2021) and ADGM will registration. See ARE.md for the federal forced-heirship rules.

## Wealth tax

None in any emirate. Dubai municipality fee: 5% of annual rental value on residential property (tenant pays, or owner if not let). Other emirates: similar fees, rates not verified.

## Special regimes

None at emirate level.

## Company taxation (kept separate from personal tax)

Corporate Tax is federal. DIFC and ADGM are financial free zones with their own common-law courts; other free zones (e.g. DMCC, JAFZA) offer the federal QFZP 0% regime.

## Cost of living notes

### Dubai (AE-DU)

No emirate income or inheritance tax. Dubai Land Department registration fee 4% of value on third-party sales (generally paid by the buyer; can also apply to transfers of shares in property-owning entities). Municipality fee 5% of annual rent on residential property. DIFC: common-law financial free zone with its own courts and the DIFC Courts Wills Service (Full Will AED 10,000). Schools regulated by KHDA.
- **prime_rent_monthly**: 50,322 AED (2026). Dubai Hills Estate, average annual rent of a 4-bedroom villa AED 603,860 (Property Monitor data, May 2026 YTD) / 12. 5-bedroom: AED 892,073 a year; Palm Jumeirah 4-bed AED 1,280,418; Dubai-wide 4-bed average AED 343,461. Source: [Villa rental prices in Dubai (Property Monitor data, May 2026 YTD)](https://www.engelvoelkers.com/ae/en/resources/villa-rental-prices).
- **intl_school_annual_fee**: 110,305 AED (2026). Dubai College (British curriculum, KHDA-regulated), Years 12-13 annual tuition 2026-27 (Years 9-11: AED 97,415); plus 5% annual re-registration fee. Source: [Dubai College - School fees 2026-2027](https://www.dubaicollege.org/admissions/school-fees).
- Rent: Dubai Hills Estate 4-bed villa average (Property Monitor via Engel & Völkers, May 2026 YTD). Purchase prices not sourced.

### Abu Dhabi (AE-AZ)

Federal capital. No emirate income or inheritance tax. Property registration fee on sales commonly reported as 2% of value (law-firm commentary; not confirmed from an official ADREC schedule). ADGM (Al Maryah and Al Reem islands) is a financial free zone that applies English common law directly, with its own courts and a non-Muslim wills registry; Abu Dhabi has its own non-Muslim personal status law (2021). Hotel municipality fee 4%. Schools regulated by ADEK.
- **prime_rent_monthly**: 46,500 AED (2026). Saadiyat Island (ultra-luxury villa area), average asking rent of a 4-bedroom villa AED 558,000 a year in H1 2026 / 12 (5-bed: AED 762,000; Yas Island 4-bed AED 351,000). Bayut asking prices for ready properties advertised by agencies, not transactions. Source: [Bayut's Abu Dhabi Rental Market Report H1 2026](https://www.bayut.com/mybayut/abu-dhabi-rental-market-report-h1-2026/).
- **intl_school_annual_fee**: 80,780 AED (2026). Brighton College Abu Dhabi (ADEK-regulated), Years 10-13 annual tuition 2026/27 (Year 9: AED 68,560). Source: [Brighton College Abu Dhabi - School fees 2026/27](https://www.brightoncollege.ae/admissions/school-fees).
- In June 2026 Abu Dhabi froze rent increases on renewals (reported; not verified from ADREC). Purchase prices not sourced.

### Sharjah (AE-SH)

No emirate income or inheritance tax. Property ownership by non-GCC foreigners is more restricted than in Dubai or Abu Dhabi (not verified in this pass). Lower housing costs than Dubai; commuting distance to Dubai.
- **prime_rent_monthly**: 15,500 AED (2025). Tilal City, average asking rent of a 4-bedroom villa AED 186,000 a year in 2025 / 12 (5-bed AED 352,000; Hoshi 4-bed AED 152,000; Al Rahmaniya AED 148,000). Bayut advertised prices, not transactions. Source: [Sharjah's Annual Market Report for 2025 by Bayut](https://www.bayut.com/mybayut/sharjah-market-report-2025/).
- **intl_school_annual_fee**: 72,765 AED (2026). Sharjah English School (British), Years 12-13 annual tuition 2026-27, excluding 5% VAT (Year 10: AED 61,035). Victoria English School's fee page still shows 2018-19 fees. Source: [Sharjah English School - School fees 2026-2027](https://www.sharjahenglishschool.org/fees).

### Ajman (AE-AJ)

No emirate income or inheritance tax. Small emirate north of Sharjah; property fees and municipality charges set by the emirate (not verified).
- **prime_rent_monthly**: 8,500 AED (2025). Al Yasmeen, average asking rent of a 4-bedroom villa AED 102,000 a year in 2025 / 12 (5-bed AED 113,000; Al Zahya 4-bed AED 90,000). Bayut advertised prices. Source: [Bayut's Ajman Annual Market Report for 2025](https://www.bayut.com/mybayut/ajman-market-report-2025/).
- **intl_school_annual_fee**: 66,350 AED (2026). Ajman Academy (British with IB PYP/MYP elements), Grade 12 annual tuition 2026/27 plus AED 1,500 book levy. Source: [Ajman Academy - Fees structure 2026/2027](https://www.ajmanacademy.com/admissions/fees-structure).

### Umm Al Quwain (AE-UQ)

No emirate income or inheritance tax. Small, lightly developed emirate; emirate fees not verified.
- **prime_rent_monthly**: 8,333 AED (2026). Median AED 100,000 a year / 12 of the 25 4-5 bedroom villa listings on Property Finder's first results page (of 88), 15 October 2026, mostly Al Salamah; range AED 65,000-140,000. Source: [Property Finder - 4-5 bedroom villas for rent in Umm Al Quwain](https://www.propertyfinder.ae/en/rent/umm-al-quwain/villas-for-rent.html?bdr%5B%5D=4&bdr%5B%5D=5).
- **intl_school_annual_fee**: 66,350 AED (2026). No leading British/IB school in Umm Al Quwain was found; a family would commute about 25-30 km to Ajman Academy (Grade 12, 2026/27). Source: [Ajman Academy - Fees structure 2026/2027](https://www.ajmanacademy.com/admissions/fees-structure).

### Ras Al Khaimah (AE-RK)

No emirate income or inheritance tax. Mountains and coast; growing resort development. DIFC Courts Wills guardianship clauses cover minors resident in Dubai or Ras Al Khaimah. Cost of living lower than Dubai (not sourced).
- **prime_rent_monthly**: 11,333 AED (2025). Al Hamra Village (main expat villa community), average asking rent of a 4-bedroom villa AED 136,000 a year in 2025 / 12 (5-bed AED 320,000, likely a small sample). Bayut advertised prices, not transactions. Villa rents rose about 8% year on year to H1 2026 (Cavendish Maxwell, as reported; not opened). Source: [Bayut's Annual Ras Al Khaimah Market Report for 2025](https://www.bayut.com/mybayut/rak-market-report-2025/).
- **prime_price_per_m2**: 12,960 AED (2025). Al Hamra Village villas, average asking price AED 1,204 per sq ft in 2025 (x 10.764 = AED 12,960 per m2); Mina Al Arab AED 1,171 per sq ft. Source: [Bayut's Annual Ras Al Khaimah Market Report for 2025](https://www.bayut.com/mybayut/rak-market-report-2025/).
- **intl_school_annual_fee**: 46,475 AED (2025). RAK Academy (international school, IB/American programmes), Grade 12 annual tuition on the 2025-26 schedule (Grade 9: AED 44,550; Grade 11: AED 46,200); AED 5,000 registration deducted from Term 1. The page does not name the year or the campus; dates imply 2025-26. Source: [RAK Academy - Fees (2025-26 fee schedule)](https://www.rakacademy.org/admissions/fees3/).
- Much cheaper than Dubai (about 45-60 minutes' drive). The 2026-27 fee schedule was not found.

### Fujairah (AE-FU)

No emirate income or inheritance tax. East-coast emirate on the Gulf of Oman; emirate fees not verified.
- **prime_rent_monthly**: 7,917 AED (2026). Median AED 95,000 a year / 12 of all 15 4-5 bedroom villa listings on Property Finder, 15 October 2026; range AED 60,000-170,000; thin market. Source: [Property Finder - 4-5 bedroom villas for rent in Fujairah](https://www.propertyfinder.ae/en/rent/fujairah/villas-for-rent.html?bdr%5B%5D=4&bdr%5B%5D=5).
- **intl_school_annual_fee**: 40,470 AED (2026). GEMS Winchester School - Fujairah (British), Grade 12 on the 2026-2027 fee structure, plus AED 1,800 book fee; the table's columns are unlabelled, so tuition vs book fee is inferred. Sixth-form provision not confirmed. Fujairah Private Academy's website was under maintenance. Source: [GEMS Winchester School - Fujairah - Fees and Payments (2026-2027)](https://www.gemswinchesterschool-fujairah.com/en/Admissions/Fees-and-Payments).
