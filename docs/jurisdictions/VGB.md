# British Virgin Islands — family-office tax dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026
Reviewer: research agent (Claude), unreviewed by a tax adviser

## Summary

The BVI has a zero-rated income tax for individuals and no capital gains, dividend, inheritance, gift or wealth tax. Employees pay 8% payroll tax on remuneration above USD 10,000. Property purchases by non-Belongers carry 12% stamp duty. The source used is Deloitte's January 2025 edition; no 2026 edition was available.

## Residence and eligibility

No income tax. Immigration permission needed for non-Belongers (not researched).

## Capital gains

None (Deloitte BVI Highlights 2025). JSON 0. Stamp duty 12% on property (4% Belongers).

## Dividends

None; no WHT; no treaties. JSON 0.

## Inheritance and gifts to children

No inheritance or estate tax. BVI probate needed for BVI-situs assets. JSON basis none.

## Wealth tax

None. Land tax small fixed amounts; house tax 1.5% of annual rental value.

## Special regimes

None.

## Company taxation (kept separate from personal tax)

0%; economic substance requirements; payroll tax 8% employee / 2-6% employer above USD 10,000.

## Cost of living notes

City: Road Town (Tortola).

- Prime rent: USD 3,500 a month, the median of the three 4-5 bedroom long-term rentals on Smiths Gore's long-term rental page (15 Oct 2026): Havers House villa (5 bed) USD 7,500, McNamara (4 bed apartment) USD 3,500, Great Mountain (4 bed apartment) USD 3,200. Thin sample; the only villa is USD 7,500, so the median is a floor for a family-size house.
- International school: Cedar International School (IB), DP1-DP2 tuition 2026-27 USD 23,870 a year paid annually (MYP4-5 USD 23,480), plus a USD 75 a month development fee and secondary lab/textbook costs.
- Price per m2, health insurance, housekeeper: not sourced.
