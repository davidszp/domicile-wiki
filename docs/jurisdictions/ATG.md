# Antigua and Barbuda — family-office tax dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026
Reviewer: research agent (Claude), unreviewed by a tax adviser

## Summary

Antigua and Barbuda does not tax individuals' income (personal income tax was abolished in 2016), and has no capital gains, dividend, inheritance, estate or wealth tax. Self-employment and unincorporated business income bears unincorporated business tax (0-25%). Real estate transfers are costly: 7.5% seller and 2.5% buyer stamp duty, a 5% alien landholding licence for non-resident buyers and 5% tax on appreciation for non-resident sellers. Citizenship by investment from USD 230,000 (National Development Fund) or USD 300,000 real estate.

## Residence and eligibility

No PIT. CBI: NDF USD 230,000 (any family size) or approved real estate USD 300,000 held 5 years; processing USD 20,000 for a family of 4 + USD 10,000 per extra dependant; due diligence USD 8,500/5,000/2,000/4,000 (CIU pages, undated).

## Capital gains

No CGT (Deloitte 2026). JSON 0 for securities and business sales; real_estate_local null. Stamp duty on sale of real property: 7.5% seller and 2.5% purchaser on value. Non-resident purchasers also need an Alien Landholding Licence costing 5% of value; non-resident sellers pay 5% stamp tax on the appreciation in value (the IRD lists a 'Non-Citizen Land Appreciation Tax' paid at the time of sale). Share sales: 5% seller, 2.5% buyer. Undeveloped land owned by non-residents: annual 10-20% tax on land value.

## Dividends

No PIT, no WHT on dividends (Deloitte 2026). JSON 0. Treaties: Switzerland, UAE, UK, CARICOM.

## Inheritance and gifts to children

No inheritance or estate tax; gifts may attract stamp tax (Deloitte 2026). JSON basis none, fully_exempt.

## Wealth tax

None. Property tax 0.2-0.5%; non-resident undeveloped land tax 10-20%.

## Special regimes

None.

## Company taxation (kept separate from personal tax)

Corporate income tax 25% (22.5% for certain banks); no participation exemption; foreign tax credits only for Commonwealth jurisdictions with reciprocal relief or by treaty. No Pillar Two commitment. No WHT on dividends.
