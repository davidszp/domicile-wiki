# Saint Kitts and Nevis — family-office tax dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026
Reviewer: research agent (Claude), unreviewed by a tax adviser

## Summary

St. Kitts and Nevis has had no personal income tax since 1980 and has no inheritance, estate, gift or wealth tax. Capital gains are untaxed unless the asset is sold within one year of purchase (then at most 20% under the Income Tax Act). Dividends are not taxed for residents. Real estate transfers bear 6-10% seller stamp duty and a 10% alien landholding licence for non-resident buyers. Citizenship by investment from USD 250,000 (Sustainable Island State Contribution).

## Residence and eligibility

No PIT. CBI: SISC USD 250,000 (family of 4), developer real estate USD 325,000, private home USD 600,000 (7-year hold), PBO USD 250,000; due diligence USD 10,000/7,500 (CIU pages, 2026). Biometric enrolment for all CBI citizens from 14 April 2026.

## Capital gains

No tax on gains of assets held over one year (Deloitte 2026). JSON 0. Short-term rule: lower of half CIT or 20%. Stamp duty on transfers of real property 6%-10% depending on consideration and location, payable by the seller; non-resident purchasers need an alien landholding licence costing the greater of 10% of value or XCD 750. Share transfers: 2% stamp duty paid by the seller.

## Dividends

No individual income tax (IRD: abolished 1980). JSON 0. WHT 15% to non-residents.

## Inheritance and gifts to children

No inheritance or estate tax (Deloitte 2026). JSON basis none, fully_exempt.

## Wealth tax

None. Property tax 0.2-0.3% of market value.

## Special regimes

None.

## Company taxation (kept separate from personal tax)

Corporate income tax 25%; short-term capital gains (asset held under one year) at the lower of half the CIT rate or 20%; no participation exemption; companies doing business exclusively abroad generally not taxed. No Pillar Two commitment. 15% WHT on dividends to non-residents.
