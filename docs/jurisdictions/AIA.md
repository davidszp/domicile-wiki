# Anguilla — family-office tax dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026
Reviewer: research agent (Claude), unreviewed by a tax adviser

## Summary

Anguilla levies no income, capital gains, dividend, inheritance, gift or wealth tax on individuals. Indirect taxes changed on 1 August 2025 (9% goods tax on imports, 13% general services tax). Real estate carries a 5% transfer tax and, for non-residents, licence/stamp duty up to 12.5%.

## Residence and eligibility

No direct tax, so residence tests are irrelevant for tax. Immigration routes not researched.

## Capital gains

None (Deloitte Anguilla Highlights 2026, updated Jan 2026). JSON 0. Real property transfer tax 5% (seller); non-resident buyers: alien landholding licence + stamp duty up to 12.5%.

## Dividends

None; no WHT; no treaties. JSON 0.

## Inheritance and gifts to children

None. JSON basis none.

## Wealth tax

None. Property tax 0.3% residential.

## Special regimes

None needed.

## Company taxation (kept separate from personal tax)

No CIT. 2025 change: GST replaced on 1 Aug 2025 by Goods Tax Act 2025 (9%) and General Services Tax Act 2025 (13%).

## Cost of living notes

City: The Valley.

- International school fee: USD 9,000 a year (Omololu International School, international students, Grades 7-11; USD 900 a month for 10 months, plus a USD 500 registration fee). Omololu stops at Grade 11; no school on Anguilla offers upper-secondary international years. The page states no school year.
- Prime rent: USD 4,000 a month (2026), a single listing: Axarealtor's fully furnished 4-bed, 4-bath villa on two acres at Sile Bay, long-let rate USD 4,000 a month (negotiable), read 15 Oct 2026. It is the only 4+ bedroom home with a monthly long-let rate found; 3-bedroom houses on the same page run USD 2,000-3,300 (one new West End house USD 8,500). Very thin sample.
- Price per m2, health insurance, housekeeper: not sourced.
