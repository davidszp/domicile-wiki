# Malaysia — family-office tax dossier

Last reviewed: 2026-10-15
Tax year: 2026
Reviewer: research agent (Claude), unreviewed by a tax adviser

## Summary

Malaysia taxes individuals on Malaysian-source income at up to 30%. There is no capital gains tax for individuals on shares (the 2024 capital gains tax applies to companies and other entities), and foreign-sourced income received in Malaysia by resident individuals is exempt until 31 December 2036 (subject to conditions). Malaysian dividends are exempt except for a 2% tax on annual dividend income above MYR 100,000 from YA 2025. There is no inheritance, estate, gift or wealth tax. Real property gains tax applies to Malaysian property: non-citizens pay 10% even after 5 years. Malaysia has no comprehensive tax treaty with the US, so US dividends bear 30% US withholding.

US citizens: US citizens remain fully US-taxed; Malaysia has no comprehensive income tax treaty with the US and does not tax their foreign income, so the US layer is the full cost.

## Residence and eligibility

Resident if present in Malaysia 182 days or more in a calendar year (further linking rules for shorter periods exist; not detailed by PwC).

- **Malaysia My Second Home (MM2H) - Silver, Gold, Platinum, SEZ/SFZ** (EU, US, OTHER): Age 25+, fixed deposit and property purchase requirements per tier (amounts shown only in images on the official site and not verified; commonly cited 2024 figures: USD 150,000 / 500,000 / 1,000,000 fixed deposit). Minimum stay 90 days a year for participants under 50. Spouse, children under 21 (and unmarried unemployed children to 34) and parents can be included. Pass renewed every 5 years.

## Capital gains

### Standard regime

No general capital gains tax for individuals. Gains on Malaysian real property and shares in real property companies are subject to real property gains tax (RPGT): 30% within 3 years, then 20%/15% (citizens and PRs) or 30% (non-citizens) in years 4-5, and from year 6: 0% for citizens/PRs, 10% for non-citizens. Foreign gains received by resident individuals are covered by the foreign-sourced income exemption to 2036.

- **Exemptions and thresholds:** Shares (listed or unlisted, Malaysian or foreign) held by individuals: no CGT. RPGT nil for citizens/PR after 5 years.
- **Important exceptions, statute references and announced changes:** The capital gains tax introduced from 1 January 2024 on disposals of unlisted shares and foreign capital assets applies to companies, LLPs, trusts and co-operatives, not individuals (PwC: no general capital gains tax for individuals). A family holding company would be subject to it.
- **Tax year / confidence / verification:** 2026 / high / partially_verified (checked 2026-10-15)
- **JSON numbers:** headline 30% (0.3); listed domestic 0% (0.0); listed foreign 0% (0.0); private business sale 0% (0.0); local real estate 10% (0.1); foreign real estate 0% (0.0).
- **How derived:** Individuals: no CGT on shares. Local real estate: RPGT 10% for a non-citizen selling after year 5 (citizens/PR 0%). Foreign real estate: gain is foreign-sourced, exempt to 2036. Headline 30% = top RPGT rate for disposals within 3 years.
- **Holding period:** RPGT rates fall with holding period (6th year onward lowest).
- **Trader recharacterisation:** Share or property dealing as a business is taxed as business income at up to 30%.
- **Foreign assets:** Foreign income (including gains) received in Malaysia by resident individuals exempt 1 January 2022 to 31 December 2036, subject to conditions; foreign income through a Malaysian partnership is excluded.

## Dividends

### Standard regime

Malaysian single-tier dividends are exempt, but from YA 2025 resident individuals pay 2% on annual dividend income above MYR 100,000 (after eligible deductions). Foreign dividends received by resident individuals are exempt under the foreign-sourced income exemption to 31 December 2036 (subject to conditions), so source-country withholding is the final cost.

- **Exemptions and thresholds:** First MYR 100,000 of dividends a year not subject to the 2% tax.
- **Important exceptions, statute references and announced changes:** No comprehensive treaty with the US (only air/sea transport), so US dividends bear 30% US WHT. Without a treaty, Malaysian credit relief is limited to half the foreign tax (relevant only if foreign income were taxable).
- **Tax year / confidence / verification:** 2026 / medium / partially_verified (checked 2026-10-15)
- **JSON numbers:** headline 2% (0.02); domestic 2% (0.02); foreign gross 0% (0.0); foreign tax relief: exemption.
- **How derived:** Domestic: 2% on dividend income above MYR 100,000. Foreign: exempt (FSI exemption for individuals to 2036).
- **Outbound withholding:** No WHT on single-tier dividends (general rule; not separately verified).
- **Treaty network:** About 74 comprehensive treaties (PwC lists 78 entries including pending and limited agreements); the US agreement covers only air and sea transport.
- **Holding companies:** Companies pay 24%; since 2024 companies pay CGT on unlisted Malaysian shares and on foreign capital gains received (10% of net gain or 2% of gross for pre-2024 assets, general knowledge, not verified). Foreign dividends received by companies are exempt only with economic substance.

## Inheritance and gifts to children

### Standard regime

No inheritance, estate or gift tax (estate duty abolished in 1991). Transfers of Malaysian real property between parents and children are generally exempt from RPGT, and stamp duty relief applies to some family transfers (not verified).

- **Exemptions and thresholds:** No tax of any amount.
- **Important exceptions, statute references and announced changes:** Reintroduction of an inheritance tax has been discussed publicly but no law exists (general knowledge).
- **Tax year / confidence / verification:** 2026 / high / verified (checked 2026-10-15)
- **JSON numbers:** basis `none`, children `fully_exempt`, exemption 0 MYR, brackets (marginal): none; other transfer costs 0% (0.0).
- **Gifts:** None. Stamp duty can apply to transfers of Malaysian property or shares by gift (rates not verified). (same as inheritance: True; tax-free after years: None)
- **Foreign assets:** No tax.
- **Residence/domicile rules:** Not applicable.
- **Business relief covers listed shares:** False

## Wealth tax

### Standard regime

No net wealth tax.

- **Tax year / confidence / verification:** 2026 / high / verified (checked 2026-10-15)
- **JSON type:** `none`
- **Property tax (not a wealth tax):** State quit rent and local assessment rates on property; stamp duty on property transfers (rates not verified). Foreign buyers face state-set minimum purchase prices.

## Special regimes

None modelled; see Residence and eligibility.

## Company taxation (kept separate from personal tax)

Headline CIT rate: 24% (0.24). 24% CIT (SME rates 15%/17% on the first MYR 600,000). Single-tier system. Since 1 January 2024 companies, LLPs, trusts and co-operatives pay capital gains tax on unlisted Malaysian shares and foreign capital assets (details not verified). Pillar Two: QDMTT and MTT from financial years starting 1 January 2025; UTPR not adopted.
Special zones/regimes: Labuan international business centre; Forest City Special Financial Zone; Iskandar Malaysia (not researched).
